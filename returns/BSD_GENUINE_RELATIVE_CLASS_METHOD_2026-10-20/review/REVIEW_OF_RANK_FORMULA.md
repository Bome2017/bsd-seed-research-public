# Review of the relative-support elimination formula

Date: 2026-10-20. Desk review of `arithmetic/gcd_refinement.gp` and
`arithmetic/support_rank.gp`, the displayed matrix layout, and their output.
I also inspected `integer_kernel.gp`, `unit_kernel_check.gp`, and their
completed outputs. I did not rerun all number-field ideal calculations. Their exact arithmetic
validation remains the arithmetic lane's output and the separate replay.

## The relation space retains the residual ideals

Each authenticated batch identity has the form

    I_i=(alpha_i)j(a_i)J_i.

The code includes all scalar conjugates of every residual J_i, together
with the selected original prime ideals. Gcd refinement splits overlapping
supports, retains the resulting composite ideals as atoms, checks that the
atoms are pairwise coprime, and checks that the scalar automorphism permutes
them. The decomposition routine subsequently requires every input ideal
to reconstruct exactly from these atoms; it rejects leftover support.

Projection to a faithful scalar character kills the base ideal j(a_i).
For a four-cycle of atoms the coordinate weights 1,chi,chi^2,chi^3 are
correct because e_chi(sigma^k D)=chi^k e_chi(D). Shorter scalar cycles have
zero faithful projection. A common nonzero scaling from an idempotent
normalization does not change any rank. Projecting one row from every
arithmetic identity also spans the projections of its scalar conjugates.

The resulting matrix R has eight original columns and 177 private columns.
No private column is discarded before testing whether it can be eliminated.

## The elimination rank is exact for this presentation

Let V be the rowspace of R over F_5 and let pi be projection onto its private
columns. The generated rows supported only on the original columns are
exactly ker(pi|V). Therefore their dimension is

    rank(R)-rank(R_private).

This is a rank-nullity identity, not a heuristic. The recorded values are

    character     rank(R)   rank(R_private)   closed-original rank
    chi=2           156          156                    0
    chi=3           156          156                    0.

Thus no linear combination of the 196 generated rows eliminates the private
columns while giving a nonzero relation on the eight original columns in
the named-atom presentation. The larger presentation has 185-156=29
remaining dimensions, but 29 is not the rank of the actual class group.
It is an upper bound on the image of these selected ideal columns after
imposing only these relations. Unrepresented classes remain a separate
generator defect; additional relations can also shrink this quotient.

## A required precision about composite atoms

Pairwise coprime nontrivial ideals are independent as integral divisors.
They need not remain independent modulo five if an atom is itself a fifth
power ideal. In that case the formal atom presentation can miss additional
modulo-five closures, although any relation it does produce is still valid.

For the stronger statement about *all actual divisor closures generated by
these rows*, every relevant atom must have a valuation not divisible by
five. An ideal norm that is not a perfect fifth power is a sufficient exact
certificate. An atom whose norm is a perfect fifth power needs a separate
valuation/root check, or must retain a possible undercount qualification.
The arithmetic lane performed this check: all 743 atom norms are NOT
perfect fifth powers (`PERFECT_FIFTH_NORMS=[]`). Thus every atom has a
nonzero divisor modulo five. Their disjoint supports imply that the formal
atom columns inject into the actual divisor space modulo five. This closes
the maximality qualification: the zero closed-original rank is exhaustive
for divisor combinations of the generated family, not merely for its
formal atom representation. It is not exhaustive for all principal ideals
of A, which were not generated or searched.

## A modulo-five cycle is not automatically a unit

Vanishing of a principal divisor modulo five means that it is five times
an integral divisor. It does not mean it is the zero divisor. Therefore
neither a zero projected row nor a modulo-five row-kernel vector certifies
an ordinary unit. An actual unit must come from an exact integral divisor
cancellation (with the base term cancelled, for example by 1-tau), followed
by a check that the resulting principal ideal is the unit ideal.

Even an authenticated unit only contributes to the desired rank after its
nontriviality and relevant norm/local evaluation have been checked modulo
fifth powers. The displayed support ranks supply no such unit claim.

## The separate exact unit-kernel calculation

The subsequent calculation uses an actual integral anti-divisor matrix H,
with 392 rows and 743 atom columns. For each original row it includes its
1-tau anti-divisor and its sigma conjugate; the other two scalar translates
are their negatives. Hence it includes the whole scalar-generated family.
Its rational rank is 312, and a rational kernel basis has 80 columns.
Denominators of each column were cleared, and H^t K=0 was checked exactly.

For every basis column, `unit_kernel_check.gp` multiplies the corresponding
alpha/tau(alpha) factors, with sigma applied for the conjugate rows. It
checks that the principal ideal is O_A and that the relative norm is one.
Its exact output is 78 copies of +1 and two copies of -1.

A full saturated integral kernel basis is unnecessary for the resulting
zero-unit conclusion. If v is any integral vector in the rational kernel,
some positive multiple Nv lies in the integral span of the tested columns.
Writing u for its multiplier, one has u^N=+1 or -1. Thus u is a root of
unity. The retained real embeddings of A imply that its only roots of
unity are +1 and -1. Every such unit is a fifth power in A. Consequently
saturation of this exact relation family cannot reveal a missing
nontorsion unit or a nonzero unit class modulo fifth powers.

This proves exhaustion only for units obtainable as products of the
specified 392 multipliers with zero divisor. It does not bound the full
relative unit group and does not prevent a new arithmetic relation from
producing a new unit. No real-logarithm rank or floating-point recognition
is used in this argument.

## Scope

The matrix formula is sound and retains the class-support bookkeeping
needed for an upper-bound attempt. The zero closed-original rank is scoped
to the generated rows and this presentation; it is neither a class-group
computation nor a proof that further relative relations cannot exist.
No complete source bound, FIN, ORDER9, or Q36 conclusion follows.
