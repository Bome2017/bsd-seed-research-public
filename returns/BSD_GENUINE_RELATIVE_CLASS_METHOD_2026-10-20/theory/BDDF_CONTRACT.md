# Bounded unconditional generator test

Evaluate only Belabas–Diaz y Diaz–Friedman Corollary 5.2, inequality (23),
at T=419776 using the completed actual A24 prime-ideal norm inventory
through 419775. Do not substitute the paper's GRH Corollary 2.2.

Success requires its weighted prime-ideal sum to exceed its displayed
discriminant/signature expression. If a simple rigorous upper bound on
the left side is below a rigorous lower bound on the right side, record
FAIL_AT_THIS_CUTOFF. That does not imply the prime ideals fail to generate,
and does not exclude a different certificate or test function.

Use every prime ideal and every positive power of its norm below T, not
only the faithful scalar orbits. The complete inventory and maximal-order
source are retained inputs; this check does not repeat their generation.
No number-field computation is launched. Exact rational inequalities are
the acceptance test; any higher-precision decimal result is diagnostic.
