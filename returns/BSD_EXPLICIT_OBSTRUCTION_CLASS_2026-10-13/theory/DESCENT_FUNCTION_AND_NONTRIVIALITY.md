In fact this gives the full group Sha(F/Q)[hatphi], without using the analytic main-conjecture calculation. The anti-invariant S-unit rank is2: the ordinary unit and the split11 direction. The inert prime5 contributes no anti-invariant rank. The S-class group is C20 modulo the class5 of the split11 prime, hence C5; its 5-torsion contributes one further dimension. Consequently

Sel_hatphi(F/Q)=<epsilon,alpha11,f(P1)> over F5,

The displayed equality uses the represented cohomology classes, not literal equality of multiplicative subgroups of K*. The dimension statement uses certified quadratic class/unit arithmetic and the retained rank/saturation input. It says nothing by itself about any deeper 5-primary layers.

This construction explains **where the quotient directions sit in this descent**: the rational point image carries the class-group direction; the unit and split11 directions survive its quotient. It is not a general claim that units or split primes always produce Sha, and it does not transplant the rational5-isogeny to the seed.
