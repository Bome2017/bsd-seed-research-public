# Bounded partner admission experiment

The field computation was performed before this protocol and found a one-dimensional required eigenspace of K'(empty,5). This is not a blind prediction of that dimension.

Next, evaluate a normalized Miller descent function on the two retained points Q1,Q2 of F, and show that they span that eigenspace. Then scan rational primes q in ascending order up to5000, excluding5 and bad3,11,61,239, and require K' to split completely. At the lexicographically first residue root embedding, compute the two point descent residues. Stop at the first q for which both are fifth powers, and exhibit a local F-point whose value is not a fifth power. Record all tested primes. Do not use a Sha order, analytic L-value, Kato coefficient, or target result in this calculation.

Success: a complete globally eligible source maps to zero at q while an actual locally admissible class maps nonzero. Failure: no such q in bound, no claimed nonexistence beyond that bound.
