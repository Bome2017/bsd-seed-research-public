## 1. A global exclusion was actually computed

For the retained degree-five isogeny `phi:A -> F`, the positive classes from the previous return lie in `Sha(F)[hatphi]`. We computed the complementary descent for `Sha(A)[phi]` in the quartic field

\[
K'=\mathbf Q(a),\qquad
a^4-a^3+54671a^2-54671a+597816451=0.
\]

Certified class/unit arithmetic gives a complete eligible global Kummer eigenspace of dimension one. Exact descent evaluations of the two displayed rational points on F are `(3,3)` and `(2,2)` in the recorded coordinates: they fill that entire eigenspace. Hence the isogeny Selmer group equals the rational-point image, proving `Sha(A)[phi]=0` by this descent calculation. No prior Sha value or rank upper bound was used for this conclusion. It is not a computation of the entire `Sha(A)[5]`.

At the auxiliary prime 331, both global generators have trivial local fifth-power signature. But the actual local point `(1,102)` has descent value 40, and

\[
40^{66}\equiv150\not\equiv1\pmod{331}.
\]

Thus a nonzero, locally admissible signature is excluded by the independently computed global source. This is the kind of admission test the experiment requested; it is completed on the control partner, not on the seed.

## 2. The obstruction has an explicit arithmetic row

The computation also identifies a concrete reason that an apparent extra ramification direction cannot enter the global source. In the recorded order of the four primes above 11, the ideal-class boundary is

\[
c=(4,1,3,2):\mathbf F_5^4\longrightarrow\mathrm{Cl}(K')/5.
\]

Any principal-element valuation vector, with valuations divisible by five elsewhere, must lie in its kernel. The character required by the isogeny selects the divisor line generated by

\[
v=(1,4,3,2),\qquad c\cdot v=1\pmod5.
\]

Therefore every nonzero vector on that line is excluded. This row comes from actual ideal classes, not from an analytic quotient or a previously known Sha group. The complete enlarged source has valuation image exactly `ker(c)`, which was checked independently by exhaustive finite linear algebra.

## 4. What the experiment changes

We now have an evaluated example of the missing distinction: a local signature can fail global admission because an independently computed ideal-class boundary is nonzero. The control's admissible positive classes and the partner's excluded local class can be compared without feeding their known Sha answers into the computation.

For the seed, arbitrary lifts of a desired local polynomial do not enforce global support. The executed repair separated two requirements that must be handled together: coefficient incidence and global divisor compatibility. The full descent acceptance equations and a precise reciprocity-witness target are in [ADMISSION_RULE.md](duality/ADMISSION_RULE.md). The quartic boundary does not compute the seed's degree-24 boundary.

We have **not** constructed the seed's required global witness or independently excluded its 1783 signature. Even that one-place exclusion alone would not bound classes invisible at 1783, nor prove cofinite vanishing across coefficient primes. FIN, ORDER9, Q36 and seed-prime coverage remain unchanged.

The bounded experiment is complete at this scope. Its result is a computed control admission mechanism plus explicit tested seed candidates, not a claim that descent theory itself is new or that the seed is now close to completion.
