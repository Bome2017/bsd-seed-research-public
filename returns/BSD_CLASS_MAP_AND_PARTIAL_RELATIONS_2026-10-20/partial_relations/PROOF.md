# Exact private-prime exclusion for the selected partial relations

## Objects and retained inputs

Let A be the retained degree-48 nonzero 7-torsion point field of the rank-two -47 twist of 11a1, with the supplied maximal-order basis, six real embeddings, and automorphism sigma=[3] of order six. Put tau=sigma^3. The field, maximal order and scalar interpretation are retained inputs; this run does not recertify their original construction.

Eighty integral elements alpha_i were reconstructed from saved deterministic weighted-LLL coordinates and matched to their saved exact norms. Let beta_i=alpha_i/tau(alpha_i). No unit or Selmer claim was used to select them.

## Actual prime-ideal witnesses

For each i the checked files give a rational prime q_i such that:

1. q_i divides Norm(alpha_i) exactly once;
2. q_i divides none of the other 14,325 saved norms;
3. P_i=(alpha_i)+(q_i) has norm q_i;
4. alpha_i belongs to P_i but to neither P_i^2 nor tau(P_i);
5. the six ideals sigma^j(P_i), 0<=j<6, are pairwise distinct.

Primality of q_i is checked with PARI isprime, not a probable-prime test. Norm q_i proves P_i is a prime ideal. Exact HNF and ideal-membership calculations prove all the listed assertions. The unique rational-prime support check is separately performed in standard-library Python on the entire saved norm log. An unmatched power in a batch-GCD quotient is not used as proof of uniqueness.

Since alpha_i is integral and v_{q_i}(Norm(alpha_i))=1, P_i is its only prime factor above q_i and occurs with exponent one. Thus on the six actual primes above q_i in this scalar orbit, the divisor of sigma^j(beta_i) is e_j-e_{j+3}. Every scalar translate of every other saved element has zero divisor at these six primes, since its rational norm is prime to q_i. Indices are modulo six.

## Exact ranks and cancellation

For each i the 6-by-6 block B=(e_j-e_{j+3}) has rank three over Q and over F_7. Its last three columns are negatives of its first three, and the first three contain an identity minor. These eighty distinct rational-prime blocks imply rank 240 for all 480 scalar-translated divisors, over both fields. In particular the original eighty relative-ratio divisors have rank eighty.

The integer kernel for the full scalar family is generated by the relations beta_{i,j} beta_{i,j+3}=1, which are exact identities because tau(beta_i)=beta_i^{-1}. Consequently the only ordinary unit produced by multiplying elements of this selected scalar family is 1. This is stronger than failure to find a cycle: the witnesses exclude every divisor cancellation in that family.

For the retained faithful character sigma -> 3 modulo 7, use weights (6,2,3,1,5,4). The projected block is (5,4,6,2,3,1), which is nonzero. Hence the eighty projected divisor columns have rank eighty. No nonzero faithful virtual-unit class occurs in this selected scalar family.

The identity minor also proves seven-saturation of the divisor lattice of the independent first three translates for each i. If x^7 lies in the selected multiplicative subgroup, its exponents in that independent basis are divisible by seven. Dividing x by the corresponding product leaves a seventh root of unity. The retained real embeddings exclude nontrivial seventh roots of unity, so no new source is obtained by adjoining seventh roots within A. This conclusion concerns this selected subgroup only.

## Boundaries

Only eighty elements were reconstructed. Their witness primes were compared with all 14,326 saved norms, but the other 14,246 element vectors and their prime ideals were not reconstructed. The result does not exclude relations among the remaining elements, unrelated unit sources, or the entire faithful unit group. It supplies no rejection-map rank, no class contribution bound, and no original-seed conclusion. FIN, ORDER9 and Q36 are unchanged.
