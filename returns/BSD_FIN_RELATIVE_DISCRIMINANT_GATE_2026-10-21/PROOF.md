## Proof

At 4547, a tame type-III prime of residue characteristic greater than three, the retained action of inertia on V is generated by J with J^2=-I, hence order four. The embeddings of A correspond to X=V\{0} and those of K to antipodal pairs Y=X/{+/-1}; their cardinalities are n and n/2. All nonidentity elements of the inertia group act without fixed points on X, so X has n/4 inertia orbits. J descends to a fixed-point-free involution on Y: if [Jv]=[v], then Jv=+/-v and thus J^2v=v, contradicting J^2v=-v for nonzero v in odd characteristic. Therefore Y also has n/4 orbits.

For a tame permutation representation, the Artin conductor exponent, equal to the field discriminant exponent, is degree minus inertia-orbit count. Thus the two local exponents are n-n/4=3n/4 and n/2-n/4=n/4. Subtraction with Disc(A)=Disc(K)^2*delta_p gives n/4.

At q=3,2243,5843, the retained multiplicative reduction has v_q(Delta_min)=2,1,2 respectively. None is divisible by a good coefficient p>=5. Inertia modulo p is the full tame transvection subgroup generated by U(x,y)=(x+y,y) of order p. On X there are p-1 fixed nonzero points (y=0) and p-1 length-p orbits (one for each y!=0): total 2(p-1). On Y there are p-1 orbits. The tame discriminant exponents are n-2(p-1)=(p-1)^2 and n/2-(p-1)=(p-1)^2/2, leaving zero relative exponent.

By Neron-Ogg-Shafarevich, the torsion representation is unramified outside the original bad places and p. Thus the only possible additional rational prime support of the relative discriminant norm is at 2 and p, with nonnegative exponents. Since 4547>16, its known 4547-power alone exceeds 16^(n/4)=2^n, proving the gate. The displayed lambda inequality follows by substitution into the previous scaling proof.
