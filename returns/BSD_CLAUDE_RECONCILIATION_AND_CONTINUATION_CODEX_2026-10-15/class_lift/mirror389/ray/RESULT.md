## The exact objects and maps

Let K be the saved degree-eight field of the nonzero 3-torsion point of the rank-zero -3 twist control. For every prime ideal above 3 or389, use the depth already required by the local Kummer calculation:

    n_P = floor(3e_P/2)+1 above3; n_P=1 above389.

The finite modulus m is the product of P^n_P. There are no infinite places in the modulus. Its norm is

    Norm(m)=109520639591706729 = 3^14 * 389^4.

The original ordinary class ideal I is moved away from m using an explicit approximation element gamma. The resulting J=I(gamma) is coprime to m and satisfies J^3=(beta'), where beta'=beta*gamma^3 is a unit at every prime dividing m. This exact ideal identity and coprimality are checked before any ray calculation. J has ordinary class coordinate1 in Cl(K)=C3.

Write R=Cl_m(K) and K_res=ker(R→Cl(K)). The actual BNR result is

    R = C3492 × C9 × C3 × C3 × C3 × C3.

The ray coordinates of J are

The matrix KG in `clean_replay/quotient_certificate.gp` maps actual CRT residue generators into the BNR coordinates. Each CRT element is chosen to equal the specified local unit generator at one prime-power factor and1 at all the others; its principal ideal is then evaluated with `bnrisprincipal`. The two quotient maps are:

    R → R/(3 K_res) → R/(3 K_res + A_loc),

where A_loc is generated by the images of the **actual local Kummer unit allowance**, not by the scalar/global-additivity rows. Global units have already been divided out in the ray class group.

## Exact results

Smith normal form of those actual integer relation matrices gives

    R/(3 K_res) = C9 × C3^5,
    R/(3 K_res + A_loc) = C3^4.

The chosen lift J has order9 in the first quotient and order3 in the second. Thus the raw ray extension does contain an order-nine lift, but that extra factor does **not** survive the relevant local Kummer allowance.

The independent residue-pushout computation agrees. There are10 residue-unit mod-cube coordinates; the global-unit image has rank4 and the local allowance rank3, together rank7. Its residual kernel quotient has dimension3. The ordinary-class boundary into that quotient is zero, producing the extension C3^4 rather than a group with an order-nine factor.

The beta' ray coordinates were checked against the CRT residue coordinates modulo3K_res, and the integer relation expressing3[J] in the final quotient was solved explicitly. Saved Smith transformations verify the two concrete lift orders, not just the abstract invariant factors.

## Why this is different from the nonzero joint obstruction

The earlier joint calculation uses both G (scalar plus necessary global additivity) and L (local Kummer constraints). Its unique repairs are different:

    u_G=(0,1,2,2),  u_L=(2,2,2,2).

The ordinary ray pushout allows all global-unit corrections, including u_L. It therefore has zero class boundary and an order-three lift after the allowance. The joint calculation permits only corrections that preserve G, and rejects the class. Ordinary ray quotienting forgets exactly the distinction between those unit repairs.

Consequently the nonzero repair-compatibility class cannot simply be renamed the ordinary ray-class boundary. A future construction retaining it must specify an additional global compatibility structure; the usual ray quotient does not retain it automatically. This calculation verifies that limitation on actual arithmetic objects.
