## Correction 1. The Fitting-to-Heegner equivalence is withdrawn

**What the first version claimed.** PROOF.md Proposition 1 said that, on a set of primes D,
four statements are equivalent. The fourth was "the augmentation image Θ_1(0) ⊗ F_p of the first
Euler-system ideal is the unit ideal". RESULT.md and ENDPOINT_EFFECT.md used this to say the
higher-Fitting source "reduces exactly" to the derived-Heegner criterion (A1 → A2).

**Why it does not hold as written.** Part (b) of the proof (statement 4 ⇔ statement 3) moves
between three different Euler-system constructions and proves no comparison between them:

| Step | Object actually used | Source of the object |
|---|---|---|
| Θ_i defined | ideals generated by coordinates of Howard's Λ-adic Heegner Kolyvagin system | CONSTRUCTION.md A1(i) |
| Θ_i ≈ Fitt_i(H²_Λ,tors) up to height two | whatever Euler system Da Ronche–Longo–Vigni use | LOCAL_ATTEMPT.md summary; the paper is not in the archive, and the summary does not say which system it is |
| ν_p, Lemma 8.4 | Kolyvagin's classical derived classes κ_n mod p, built from Heegner points of conductor n on X_0(N), in Zhang's normalization | Zhang, Lemma 8.4, as recorded in HEEGNER_NU.md |

The following comparison steps are missing. Each would be needed for statement 4 ⇔ statement 3.

- **C1 (which system).** It is not established that the DLV ideals are built from Howard's Λ-adic
  system. This return never read the DLV text.
- **C2 (specialization factor).** Howard's Λ-adic classes come from p-stabilized Heegner points of
  conductor n·p^k. At T = 0 the norm-compatible family recovers the classical class only after
  multiplying by an Euler-type factor at p that depends on α_p. Nothing in D controls the p-adic
  valuation of that factor. (I expect it to be a non-unit at anomalous primes, but I have not
  checked this against Howard's text.) So "Howard specializes to the classical system" holds only
  up to an uncontrolled, p-dependent factor.
- **C3 (coordinate versus class).** A generator of Θ_1 is a Λ-coordinate of a class in H¹_Λ.
  The classical κ_ℓ mod p is a class in H¹(K, E[p]). It is ramified at ℓ, so it lies in a modified
  Selmer group, not in H¹_f(K,T). In addition H¹_Λ/T has Z_p-rank 1, while H¹_f(K,T) has rank 3,
  because rank E(K) = 3. The control sequence of part (a) concerns H¹_f. It does not identify
  "Θ_1(0) ⊗ F_p is a unit" with "some κ_ℓ ≢ 0 mod p".
- **C4 (normalization).** Even classically, Howard's system and Zhang's κ_n can differ by fixed
  normalizations: the modular parametrization, the Manin constant, and the choice of Heegner point.
  These are probably p-units for large p, but the first version neither stated nor checked this.

**What is kept.**
- Part (a), the exact control sequence for a perfect complex of amplitude [1,2] with H² kept.
  This is standard homological algebra. Its identification with H¹_f(K,T) is Nekovář's
  control theorem, cited at statement level. It is not a new result.
- Statements 1 ⇔ 2 ⇔ 3 on the set where s⁻ = 1 and Lemma 8.4 applies. These are **not new**:
  they are the record's kernel-checked bookkeeping `nu_le_two_iff_sha_of_twist`, which takes
  Lemma 8.4 as the hypothesis `ZhangLemma84` (HEEGNER_NU.md §1). The W3 return's
  HEEGNER_NU_NOTE.md §3 states the same chain together with its numerical twist value.

So Proposition 1 has no content beyond the record. The return's "one new derivation" is
withdrawn. **Candidate A1 is not reduced to A2.** Its status is now "control map written (with H²
kept); the comparison between its augmentation generator and any classical Heegner quantity is
open (C1–C4); not developed further". The common-failure statement in RESULT.md is rescoped to
A2 and A3, which are unaffected.
