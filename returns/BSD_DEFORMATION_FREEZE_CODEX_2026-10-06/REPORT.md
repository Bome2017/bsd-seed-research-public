input and show how it bounds or annihilates the ORIGINAL excess. An
independently controlled cohomology index, specialization, or annihilator
could qualify; simply naming one does not.

construction supplying its control, its actual seed hypotheses, and the
implication to a BSD obligation. It must identify which previously missing
implication it changes. The same class in another notation does not qualify.

or a new pairing without such control is not a main-line reopening.
