## 2. The useful common object is explicit

All successful analytic prime certificates use the same fixed rational modular symbol of E. Let Phi_m(a) be its plus value at a/m in one fixed period convention. For a cyclic quotient of the unit group, let psi be its discrete logarithm and form

    Theta_(m,psi)(U) = sum_a Phi_m(a) (1+U)^(psi(a)),
    M2(m,psi) = sum_a binomial(psi(a),2) Phi_m(a).

For the tame singleton, m=ell and psi(a)=ind_g(a) modp. The actual certificate is M2 modp nonzero, with the lower moments zero modp. For the ordinary depth2 calculation, m=p^2, psi(a) is the principal-unit exponent for gamma=1+p. Modulo p it is the negative Fermat quotient

    psi(a) = -(a^(p-1)-1)/p modp.

Ordinary stabilization contributes the explicitly matched unit alpha^(-2); the lower-denominator contribution vanishes modulo p in these depth2 runs. The4 small ordinary prime calculations and10 tame successful runs are therefore concrete quadratic moments of one modular-symbol source along different cyclic directions. This is an exact shared construction, not the tautology that they all prove Sha zero. It is already present in the source formulae; this return does not claim its discovery as a new theorem.

The common degree2 is forced by the two rational point directions and the retained order-of-vanishing theorems. Constant and linear moments vanish at the certified precisions. Generator changes rescale the first nonzero quadratic term by a square unit. Shared rational integrality/lattice bounds allow rigorous balls to recover the moments exactly. These ingredients are useful for transferring implementations and proposing comparison experiments.

They do NOT by themselves force M2 nonzero. The cyclic group, conductor and evaluation direction vary with p. A fixed rational symbol is not yet a fixed nonzero integer whose prime divisors contain the entire Sha support. The two-isogeny descent at2 is a genuinely different arithmetic input, not one of these moments.

## 3. Complementarity, not one numerical prime feature

The checked small point counts show ordinary and supersingular successes. For tame certificates the coefficient traces range over -8,-6,0,4,6,10; auxiliary traces vary as well. All have the theorem's required local eligibility, but that eligibility holds for many unresolved primes too. Rational2-torsion and Hasse already give good non-anomalous reduction throughout the relevant good-prime domain. That shared property cannot explain which completed computations succeed.

Two particularly useful comparisons survive actual evidence:

1. **Same prime, different auxiliary:** at29, ell59 gives c2=24360=0 mod29; ell233 gives6. Both runs are valid and both auxiliaries satisfy the theorem's eligibility conditions. The old tame-regulator calculation gives determinant zero at59 and nonzero at233. The failure at59 is therefore compatible with a degenerate chosen direction rather than a different global group. The coefficient at349 was only predicted to vanish; no saved successful production was located for it.
2. **Same prime, different direction:** at17, the cyclotomic numerator has valuation1, whereas the tame103 moment is a unit. The normalized ordinary height determinant also has valuation1. Thus requiring a raw cyclotomic unit would miss a prime genuinely settled by the tame direction. A unification must retain the regulator/Bockstein/local contribution rather than declare all successful raw values units. The cyclotomic17 value here is a theorem-backed reverse deduction, so this is not a blinded two-numerator experiment.

The independently evaluated 389a1 controls have the same within-prime contrast at7: ell29 yields a nonzero moment and ell379 yields zero. These and seed29/59 were already compared in `mt_prescreen/MT_PRESCREEN.md`, lines29–49; the claimed11/11 zero-pattern matches are historical data, not new held-out evidence. The six-curve October7 experiment also already tested nonunit tame determinants. Its104 clean-depth and275 defined-level rows must not be counted as additional seed primes or independent implementations.

This supports a precise experimental theme: **separate intrinsic excess from degeneracy of the selected realization.** It does not prove that a nondegenerate realization exists whenever the excess is zero, nor produce a uniform bound on intrinsic excess. The successful methods' diversity mainly reflects choices of direction and theorem domain, not demonstrated independent cancellations of unknown Sha factors.

## 4. What can be combined without circularity

The accumulated data support trying to connect two independently computed blocks:

- the admission source: actual unit/class/local matrices, computed before using an L-value or known Sha answer;
- the modular-symbol realization: the actual quadratic moment in a named cyclic direction, computed without using the admission rank or regulator prediction.

The nontrivial proposed comparison is a factorization/rank statement distinguishing a global admission defect from a direction-specific regulator defect. Existing determinant/Bockstein and main-conjecture identities explain part of this after their hypotheses, but no independent cross-prime bound results merely by substituting Sha into both sides. The present full-image control experiment can test whether one local-rank block or unit-repair obstruction tracks a particular direction-specific degeneracy. Such a test must retain both units and class lifts; a small unit kernel alone is not a Selmer upper bound.

Acceptance should require a relation on actual maps or minors, including a zero case, followed by a proof from independently defined arithmetic. A repeat of `lengthSha = corrected coefficient valuation` is a calibration of a known theorem, not a new predictor. A fitting scalar chosen from known Sha, q or the observed coefficient is not an independently supplied global source.

The K288 observations remain relevant data. The governing audit explicitly permits a sufficient cross-prime comparison at only one suitable auxiliary per p and up to controlled unit factors; exact288 at every auxiliary is stronger than needed. But the audit also withdraws the unsupported claim that the existing up-to-unit statements already prove exact auxiliary independence. The new experiment must not revive that inference.

## 5. Independence and verification performed here

`build_coverage.py` reads the actual ten singleton checker JSONs and report files, verifies all reported c0/c1/c2 congruences and NONZERO statuses, rehashes each entire saved msl output against its checker hash, and recomputes22 distinct small seed reduction-group orders by elementary quadratic-residue enumeration. It does not evaluate a modular symbol, verify an Arb tail, replay the17,100 p83 work units, or re-prove Ota. The ordinary coefficient integers are bound to their original written proofs, not freshly reconstructed from all symbols here.

The shared Arb engine, partially overlapping Fourier corpus, common Manin lattice, common rank/basis and residual-image inputs are explicitly recorded in every applicable row. Multiple downstream scripts reading one table are not multiple independent arithmetic producers. The p19 first failed run is INVALID and never counted as a mathematical zero; its larger-cutoff successor is the accepted run. The observed prime sample was selected partly by computational cost and auxiliary availability. It is not an unbiased sample from which to infer a density or all-prime law.

**Outcome:** the requested distillation is concrete: the successful analytic certificates are different degree2 evaluations of one symbol, and their local/regulator degeneracies differ. The current joint-admission experiment attacks an arithmetic block not measured by merely repeating those successful values. This ledger adds no seed prime and no FIN, ORDER9 or Q36 conclusion.
