## A local condition really removes a surviving global-screen direction

For 433a1, the scalar-plus-finite-additivity kernel is three-dimensional. One basis vector complementary to the point plane has source exponents

    [1,2,0,2,1,2,0,0,1,0,0,0,1].

Its full S-prime valuation vector modulo3 is

    [1,2,0,2,1,2,0,0,1].

Thus silently restricting first to valuation-zero classes would discard a real necessary test. The actual field element is printed in `433a1/analyze.out`. Its local-defect column at3 is

    [1,2,0,0,1,0,2,2,0,2,1,1,0,2]^t,

and at433 is

    [2,0,0,0,0,1,0]^t.

Each is nonzero. Either place removes this one residual direction after the scalar/additivity screen. This measures an actual combination of different admission conditions; it is not inferred from known Sha vanishing.

In contrast, the scalar-plus-six-split-prime screen already leaves exactly the point plane for 389 and655. So successful admission is not always caused by the same individual row block.

## Full mod3 image verification

`field3` rejects any reducible degree-eight polynomial, so its success gives transitivity on the eight nonzero torsion points. The curves have multiplicative reduction with discriminant valuation one at389,433,131 respectively; the c4 values112,1,624 are units at those places. Tate inertia thus contains a transvection of order3. The determinant is the surjective mod3 cyclotomic character by the Weil pairing.

`check_gl2_3.py` exhausts all seven subgroups of GL2(F3) containing a specified transvection. Exactly one is transitive on nonzero vectors and has surjective determinant: GL2(F3) itself. Any transvection is conjugate to the chosen one. Thus the source image claim uses three arithmetic facts, not mere degree-eight irreducibility.

## Local precision and repairs

The local images use exact ideal-star quotients: precision n=floor(3e/2)+1 over3, and n=1 elsewhere. Local elliptic points were first constructed at35 digits. A separate run at45 digits and a different random seed reproduces the identical local row spaces at every place. Since each computed Kummer image reaches the independently determined dimension dim E(Q_l)[3] + [l=3], the sampling supplies a full local image under the retained local descent implementation. This is not formal proof-assistant verification of the implementation.

Known-zero local images were imposed as full evaluation-zero conditions: at389 for389a1, and131 for655a1. The historical skipped-zero-image bug is not present.

The negative controls did not inherit a saturation assumption from a rank routine.
