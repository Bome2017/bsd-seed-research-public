## The object

Fix an admissible good prime p ≥ 5, p ∤ 7N, and the retained conductor-p² traced Gross packet P₁. Put

    v_j = ψ̄(γ^j P₁),   M_r = Σ_{j∈F_p} j^r v_j,   A(d) = Σ_{j∈F_p} v_j v_{j+d}.

The packet has M₀ = M₁ = 0 in F_p. Elementary expansion gives

    S_p := Σ_d d⁴ A(d) = 2M₀M₄ − 8M₁M₃ + 6M₂² = 6M₂²   (p ≥ 5).

**This identity is known.** It says that the square of the witness is determined by collective toric data, even when the
individual point values are not. The following do **not** accomplish the task:
- re-proving the identity;
- Fourier-inverting an already known A(d);
- assuming the packet values are available.

## The attempt

Construct an **integral** relative-trace, reciprocity or geometric-correspondence formula that evaluates or controls S_p
**directly**, before resolving it into individual toric characters or individual pair kernels. Choose one mechanism and
develop it. You are not required to have the breakthrough before you start.

- Give the actual arithmetic expression and its normalization. Keep the seed eigenform and all local factors. A formula
  for an unrelated Gaussian theta sum is not the target.
- **Account for the 1/p of Fourier inversion** on the order-p group, which is not a unit at the prime being tested.
  Either work integrally before reducing, or prove the divisibility or cancellation that makes reduction valid. A
  rational identity with an uncontrolled p-denominator is not enough.
- If the output is a certified real interval for a normalized kernel, use the degree-zero integral lift and the correct
  final lattice test (Theorem N4). Individual anchor diagonals do not inherit the sharp denominator theorem.
- If you need traces, Hecke isolation, Fourier coefficients, local-frame data or ring-class-field inputs, **name and
  cost them.** Do not hide the old large computation inside an unevaluated black box.
- The following have no demonstrated seed-scale advantage and must not be re-presented unchanged:
  - the character-by-character central-value approach;
  - the dense-plane explicit long-filter realization.
