# What the earlier work does and does not supply

## Scope of the search

The nine named Claude source roots preserved in the October 15 reconciliation were re-hashed against all 64,883 archived entries. Every entry matches; no regular files were added or removed. This confirms custody and absence of a delta in those roots, not that every file was mathematically reread.

The live branch lists of both GitHub repositories were checked. Four explicitly named Claude branches in the lab still end at September 24–27 commits. Their pinned result/task/status files were retrieved and saved. They contain the older determinant specialization, Kato-content, aggregate-kernel task and Neron-normalization work, not a later number-field norm-rank or class-kernel computation. Their original proof grades are not upgraded by this check.

Top-level Markdown in 60 older named Claude/Opus/norm-relation roots was keyword-screened. Selected relevant reports were read. This is not an exhaustive audit of the computer or every artifact in Downloads.

## Useful arithmetic to carry forward

The actual class-lift/compatible-repair computations are relevant to the current upper bound. They test whether the same unit correction can make a class lift satisfy both global necessary conditions and local Kummer conditions. The later mirror continuation supplied the missing nonzero case on a complete source. Merely bounding the entire ordinary class kernel can discard this information. The new calculation in class_contribution applies the complete-source matrices to this distinction.

The eight rank-two controls also show why a class direction occupied by a rational point need not be killed: the eventual target is the quotient by the full point plane. These are existing arithmetic data being reapplied, not new independent number-field computations or prime coverage.

## Older items and their precise role

- The two-leg and wild-leg experiments test determinant shape and local factors. They do not give a map from those scalar values to independent incidence-norm images of actual units. No such map is supplied by the mock-plectic proxy either.
- The Kato/Kolyvagin constructions naturally produce cohomology classes. Under the matched Kummer/incidence realization these lie in the norm kernel. They could control that kernel through an additional index theorem, but their mere existence does not furnish nonzero norm-image rank. See unit_rank for the source-level argument and scope.
- The September 17 C1 norm-relations packet is older supporting material, not a new Claude result. Its stated mod-5 obstruction concerns a specific faithful permutation-module projector factored through lower-degree subfields. It must not be promoted to impossibility of other source constructions or a new all-prime theorem.
- The October 1 prime-to-p twist report supplies the elementary orthogonality of character components for equivariant maps. Its broader phrases such as 'the only bridge' are not retained as universal claims.
- The September 30 candidate report already contains explicit corrections: the arithmetic objects may depend on p, provided a theorem covers all primes outside one fixed finite set. A fixed integer is sufficient, not mandatory. Those corrections continue to govern this experiment.

No earlier report is imported as a proof that the seed is impossible, or as evidence that a missing upper estimate has been established.
