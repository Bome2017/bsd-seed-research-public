# Corrections to the preceding source screen and historical construction stop

October 11, 2026. Earlier sealed files remain unchanged.

## 1. The two auxiliary-prime domains must not be conflated

The previous screen correctly observed that a plus-invariant row cannot be transplanted unchanged to G2's scalar Frobenius `-I` at odd p. However, it used that observation while discussing reuse in the BD two-place construction. That construction has **positive-sign admissible primes**, with Frobenius eigenvalues `1,ell` and `p` not dividing `ell^2-1`. The `-I` calculation does not reject this different construction. This return works with the actual BD source domain.

The OpenAI source's full local Schwartz factors, discriminant twists and nonzero rank-one Heegner witness still do not transfer automatically. Correcting the prime domain is not proof of the desired scalar identity.

## 2. A per-prime proof is not by itself a uniformity obstruction

The historical C3 stop said that the multiplicity-one theorem was only proved prime by prime. That is insufficient to block a finite-modulus construction. A theorem quantified over every prime power, under hypotheses supplied outside one fixed finite set, applies uniformly to that domain. Natural maps on the same curve then assemble by CRT.

`notes/C3_ASSEMBLY.md` writes that finite-modulus argument, starting with an idempotent in the **actual fixed base Hecke algebra** and retaining the finite geometric hypotheses of BD. Its scope is the first raised quotient, not all higher levels or the full Euler system. The stronger demand for one rational scalar across *different raised curves* remains open. No prime has been shown to avoid the uncomputed base denominator merely by naming it.

## 3. Repeated construction is not a new BSD bound

The C4 Kummer/reduction square and C6 Hom-line cancellation were already in the project. The explicit degree correction in this return realizes a step inside them; it does not replace either with a newly discovered reciprocity law. Likewise a coefficient-valued CM sum gives an actual arithmetic input but no theorem that its selected value is nonzero.

The canonical endpoint status is unchanged. These corrections remove overstated reasons to reject a construction; they do not supply its missing value control.
