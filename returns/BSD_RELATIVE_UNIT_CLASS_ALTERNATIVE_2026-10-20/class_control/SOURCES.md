# Sources and scope

Primary sources freshly opened on 2026-10-20:

- Biasse, Fieker, Hofmann and Page, *Norm relations and computational
  problems in number fields*, introduction and the statements of
  Propositions B and C:
  https://www.normalesup.org/~page/Recherche/Documents/articles/brauer.pdf
  The applications require a genuine norm relation; their existence is
  not asserted for the cyclic quartic scalar tower here.
- Etienne, *Computing class groups by induction with generalised norm
  relations*, introduction, generalized norm relation definition and
  Theorems A-C:
  https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~fetienne002/computing_class_groups.pdf
  The theorem's conditional requirement of a generalized norm relation
  is retained.

The ideal extension/norm identities in PROOF.md are derived directly;
no unverified application of a Chevalley formula is needed. The
module countercontrol and the character annihilation are elementary
calculations supplied here.
