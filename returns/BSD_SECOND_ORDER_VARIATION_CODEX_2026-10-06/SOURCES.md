# Sources and grade boundaries

## Primary sources consulted in this turn

1. Adam Morgan and Alexander Smith, *The Cassels--Tate pairing for finite
   Galois modules*, arXiv:2103.08530, v3. Definition 1.1, Theorem 1.3,
   Definition 3.2 and Proposition 4.4 were read directly in the primary
   text. They supply the finite-module pairing, its local-condition
   category, naturality/kernel properties, cochain definition, and
   Baer linearity. The two-variable diagram, nonsplit evaluation lemma,
   commutator prescription and tuning proof are new derivations here.
   <https://arxiv.org/html/2103.08530>

2. Kazuya Kato, *p-adic Hodge theory and values of zeta functions of
   modular forms*, Asterisque 295 (2004), 117--290, Theorem 14.2(2),
   printed page 235. Read in the retained primary-source transcription,
   whose file is bound in INPUT_BINDINGS.json. Its public source was
   located; the browser could not load the 18 MB PDF. The theorem is
   used only for the all-order scope check, not for arithmetic tuning.
   <https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_2004__295__117_0/>

Other standard ingredients carried by Stage A are local Tate duality,
Brauer--Hasse--Noether, finite-character class field theory and Chebotarev.
Their applications specific to the new construction are written out.
The finite algebra and perfectness/averaging arguments are proved in
the return; no general higher-height theorem is imported.
