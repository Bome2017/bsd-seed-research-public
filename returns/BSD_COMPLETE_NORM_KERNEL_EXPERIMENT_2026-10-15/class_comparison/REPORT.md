# Class-supported excess survives local admission in the seed control

Status: PROPOSED_UNIT_ONLY_GENERALIZATION_REFUTED_ON_RETAINED_SEED3_CLASSES.

This is the adaptive test sealed in QUESTION.md before the calculation. The complete mirror control had a three-dimensional global norm kernel, comprising two valuation-supported directions and one ordinary-class-supported direction, all rejected by local admission. That observation does not imply that local admission always removes class-supported excess. The original seed's already authenticated four-class span provides a counterexample at coefficient 3. This is a reuse and comparison of established arithmetic, not a newly found seed class or a new primary bound.

## The test quotients out rational points first

Let A be the span of the supplied genuine Selmer classes in the valuation-zero multiplicative source, U the global-unit image, and W the full rational-point image. The ordinary ideal-class boundary is c: A -> Cl(K)[3]. The relevant map is

    A/W -> Cl(K)[3]/c(W).

It is well-defined and its image has dimension rank(c(A)) - rank(c(W)). Its kernel is (A intersect U + W)/W. Thus a class-supported image rank of one means that one direction of the known excess remains class-supported even after every rational point is removed. Merely displaying a nonzero class image of A would not prove this: that image might have come entirely from W.

The checker verifies zero valuations for every supplied class, solves every column in the specified valuation-zero basis H0, applies the retained ordinary class boundary, and constructs an annihilator of the point class image. It also enumerates all vectors in the small spaces to check the quotient and unit intersections independently of its rank calculations. The class boundary is the ordinary Cl(K)[3] boundary, not the S-class-group boundary.

## Actual matrices

All displayed matrices have entries in F3. Columns are ordered as stated; the class coordinates are those of the retained source.

For the seed, A has basis (P1,P2,beta1,beta2), and W has basis (P1,P2). The actual images are

    c(A) = [0 2 2 2],       c(W) = [0 2].
           [0 0 2 2]               [0 0]

The functional (0,1) kills c(W). The quotient row is therefore

    (0,0,2,2).

It has rank one. Either beta1 or beta2 represents a nonzero class-supported excess direction. Their difference beta1-beta2 has class image zero and is the already witnessed unit direction. The checker finds exactly nine unit classes in A and exactly three in W, so the known excess has a one-dimensional unit quotient and a one-dimensional class quotient.

For 28042a1, the class order is (P,unit1,unit2). The images are

    c(A) = (1,0,0),         c(W) = (1).

The class image is entirely generated by the rational point. Its quotient rank is zero. The two known excess classes have literal global-unit representatives. This is why the raw class-image rank one in this example does not demonstrate any class-supported excess.

## The seed refutation does not need a complete class-group computation

The explicit class-coordinate matrix uses the retained cached source and does not recertify its class group. An existing independent argument authenticates the quotient dimension without that completeness assumption. It is copied, with its source hash, as inputs/seed_unit_exhaustion_review.md; its hypotheses remain retained.

The octic point field has signature (2,3), and its quartic fixed field has signature (2,1). The norm-one global-unit lattice therefore has rank two. The real embeddings exclude nontrivial cube roots of unity. Its reduction modulo cubes is exactly the anti-invariant unit space, of dimension two.

The prior packet gives literal global units representing P1 and beta1/beta2. The retained four-class independence certificate makes those two unit classes independent. They consequently exhaust the entire anti-invariant unit space. Every class in A is anti-invariant, so

    A intersect U = span(P1,beta1-beta2),
    W intersect U = span(P1).

All four actual source elements have ideal valuations divisible by three. The ordinary ideal-class exact sequence then gives

    dim c(A) = 4-2 = 2,
    dim c(W) = 2-1 = 1,
    dim c(A)/c(W) = 1.

This argument uses exact field signatures, the two literal unit witnesses, full-ideal valuation assertions, the covering-to-cohomology identification, and the four-class independence input. They were retained and scoped in the earlier packet; this run does not independently redo their arithmetic. It does not require the cached fifteen-generator source to exhaust the entire Selmer group or class group.

For 28042, the vanishing of the class-supported image on the supplied span is even simpler: A is generated by W and two literal units. Thus c(A)=c(W) on that span, whatever additional classes an unfinished class-group certification might reveal. The displayed absolute class-coordinate rank remains at its saved source grade; it is not needed to prove that this quotient image is zero.

## Consequence for the research question

The mirror result and the seed result differ in a way the proposed rule excludes: local admission rejects the mirror's class direction but preserves a genuine class-supported excess direction in the seed. Hence a rule that tries to remove all ideal-class-supported contributions by local admission is false on this tested panel. Any useful upper-control construction must handle both the admitted unit contribution and the class contribution after rational-point images have been removed.

This identifies a necessary distinction for a future mechanism. It supplies no upper bound on either contribution. The seed coefficient 3 is a bad prime, so this example does not refute a separately stated theorem restricted to good coefficient primes p>=5. The 28042 curve has good reduction at 3 but different rank; its supplied unit-supported excess does not establish a general unit-only theorem either.

No class group was newly certified, no new seed coefficient prime was eliminated, and FIN, ORDER9 and Q36 are unchanged. The sibling joint_kernel/ remained sealed and untouched.
