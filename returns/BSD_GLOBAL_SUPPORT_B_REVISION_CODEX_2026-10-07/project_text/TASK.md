## A precise sufficient construction, not a required format

For example, construct a fixed finitely generated abelian group G with
an independently proved rank bound rank_Z G<=1, and arithmetic injections

    X_p -> G/pG

for every coefficient prime outside one fixed finite set. Prove these
maps and their local conditions; do not define them by dimensions.
Outside the torsion support of G, the target dimension is at most one.
Retained even parity would force X_p=0. A fixed finite G is stronger but
unnecessary. The fixed group need not be explicitly computed if its
finite generation and rank bound are independently proved.

Alternatively construct a fixed nonzero integer D from arithmetic data
and a realization proving D annihilates X_p for p outside a fixed finite
set. Or construct an integral global element and a realization law with
independently bounded exceptional support. These are sufficient shapes,
not claims that such an object already exists.
