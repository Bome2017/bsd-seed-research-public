## 2. The published rank-zero input applies to the relative character

Primary source: Kato, *p-adic Hodge theory and values of zeta functions of modular forms*, Astérisque 295 (2004), printed pp.234–236, Theorem 14.2(2), Corollary 14.3 and the immediately following remark.

Kato defines the chi-part by the kernel of the ideal I_chi in Z[G]. ## 3. New arithmetic consequence: the relative Selmer group is finite

Put S=Sel(E/F), taking the full torsion Selmer group, and W=E(F) tensor Q/Z. Then W=(Q/Z)^2 and its G-action is trivial. Kato's corollary and the actual nonzero twisted value give

    S_rel := ker(N_G:S->S) is finite.                 (A)

This is an arithmetic conclusion independent of the BSD-normalized analytic ratio. It would remain valid if that ratio had not been assembled.

The point contribution at 5 must be kept. The Kummer sequence and the trivial G-action on W give the exact sequence

    0 -> W[5] -> S_rel -> ker(N_G:Sha(E/F)->Sha(E/F)) -> 0.   (B)

To prove surjectivity in (B), lift a norm-zero Sha class to s in S. Its norm lies in W. Since W is divisible, choose w in W with 5w=N_G s. Then s-w is norm-zero and lifts the original Sha class. The kernel is exactly W[5]. In particular W[5]=(Z/5)^2 is unavoidable in the norm-zero Selmer group; it is not new relative Sha.

## 4. Canonical splitting of the actual Sha groups

Let res and cor be restriction and corestriction on Sha. They preserve local triviality and satisfy

    cor res=[5],               res cor=N_G.

Sha is torsion. The retained vanishing of the 5-primary parts means multiplication by 5 is an automorphism of both base and extension Sha groups, without assuming either group finite. Consequently res is injective and

    e_0=(1/5) res cor

is an idempotent on Sha(E/F). Define

    T=ker(cor:Sha(E/F)->Sha(E/Q)).

Then

    Sha(E/F)=res Sha(E/Q) direct-sum T,               (C)
    T=ker(N_G|Sha(E/F)),       T[5^infinity]=0.

The inverse decomposition sends x to ((1/5)cor x, x-e_0x). This is an actual group decomposition, not one modulo finite errors or only after tensoring with Q. Formula (B), together with (A), proves T finite and

    #S_rel=25 #T.

Thus the new relative component is finite, while the original unknown base component survives intact. In particular,

    Sha(E/F) finite  iff  Sha(E/Q) finite.            (D)

Only after either finiteness statement has been established may one write

    #Sha(E/F)=#T #Sha(E/Q).                           (E)

No conclusion #T=19^4 follows from this argument. Equating that integer with the relative analytic quotient is an additional arithmetic leading-term theorem, not supplied by Kato's nonvanishing/finiteness corollary.

For each coefficient prime ell!=5, (C) could also be obtained directly by averaging in Z_ell[G], without the retained 5-primary vanishing. Those vanishings permit the clean global torsion-group statement above.

## 5. A concrete rank-zero abelian fourfold

Let R=Res_{F/Q}(E_F), with diagonal map j:E->R and norm map n:R->E. Define

    B=ker(n).

Over Qbar, n is addition E^5->E, so its kernel is connected and isomorphic to E^4. Thus B is an abelian fourfold over Q, not merely a formal character constituent. Since R(Q)=E(F)=E(Q), n on rational points is multiplication by 5. The retained absence of rational 5-torsion gives

    B(Q)=0.                                         (F)

The map

    phi:E x B -> R,     (P,b) -> j(P)+b

is an isogeny whose kernel is E[5], embedded by P->(P,-j(P)); hence its degree is 25. An explicit complementary map is

    psi:R -> E x B,     x -> (n(x), 5x-j(n(x))).

Both composites are multiplication by 5. Therefore for ell!=5 these maps induce genuine isomorphisms on ell-primary Selmer and Sha groups. Shapiro's lemma, including its local versions, identifies Sel(R/Q) and Sha(R/Q) with Sel(E/F) and Sha(E/F). The resulting decompositions are

    Sel_{ell^infinity}(E/F)
       = res Sel_{ell^infinity}(E/Q) direct-sum Sel_{ell^infinity}(B/Q),

    Sha(E/F)[ell^infinity]
       = res Sha(E/Q)[ell^infinity] direct-sum Sha(B/Q)[ell^infinity].   (G)

The second summand in (G) is T[ell^infinity]. Prime-to-ell isogeny invariance here follows directly from phi and psi: 5 is invertible on all coefficient and local Kummer groups. No equality of orders is inferred across a 5-isogeny at the coefficient prime 5.

There is nevertheless an unconditional-in-5 finiteness statement for B under the retained inputs. The connected-kernel exact sequence induces

    0 -> B_tors -> R_tors -> E_tors -> 0

on geometric torsion: a torsion point of E can be lifted to a torsion point of R by adjusting any lift using divisibility of B(Qbar). The map R(Q)_tors->E(Q)_tors is multiplication by 5 on Z/2 and is surjective. The cohomology sequence therefore injects H^1(Q,B_tors) into H^1(Q,R_tors). Functoriality of Kummer local conditions and Shapiro give an injection

    Sel(B/Q) -> S_rel.

By (A) the target is finite, and (F) makes Sel(B/Q)=Sha(B/Q). Hence

    Sha(B/Q) is finite.                              (H)

At ell!=5 its component is T[ell^infinity]. At 5 its image lies in S_rel[5^infinity]=W[5], because Sha(E/F)[5^infinity]=0. Consequently

    #Sha(B/Q)[5^infinity] <= 25.                     (I)

This is only an upper bound; no assertion that the 5-primary group is zero or has order 25 is made. It is precisely why B and the prime-to-5 relative Sha summand must not be identified without qualification at 5.

The fourfold is thus an actual finite-Sha rank-zero arithmetic object constructed from the seed and the certified nonzero twists. Its existence does not prove finiteness of the separate elliptic factor in E x B.

## 6. Why the relative factor does not currently bound the seed

The decomposition (C) is compatible with every possible torsion group in the unknown base factor. Even an exact determination of T would leave that factor unbounded. A useful sharper statement concerns direct equivariant maps.

Let A be a G-trivial abelian group on which multiplication by 5 is injective. For every G-equivariant homomorphism h:T->A,

    5h(t)=h(N_Gt)=0,

so h=0. This applies in particular to A=Sha(E/Q), using its retained zero 5-primary component, and to every base coefficient-prime component with ell!=5. Thus direct G-equivariant descent from the new relative group cannot produce a nonzero class or controlling map into the base Sha group.

This lemma excludes that precise map construction. It does not rule out a new correspondence changing the G-representation, an extension or derived construction carrying additional arithmetic, or another independently justified comparison. Such a proposal must specify the new map; the equality of the relative analytic factor with 19^4 does not provide it.
