## Full mod-3 image argument

For 1913b1, the computed discriminant is `-1913^2`. Point counting at 2 gives trace1, so its characteristic polynomial has discriminant2 modulo3. It is irreducible, forcing global irreducibility of the mod-3 representation. Multiplicative reduction at1913 with discriminant valuation2 gives a nontrivial transvection by Tate inertia. This standard local theorem is the arithmetic input behind the transvection claim.

For clarity, the group-theoretic conclusion holds also at3. If a subgroup G of GL2(F3) is irreducible and contains a transvection u, some conjugate v has a different fixed line. Choose those fixed lines as the two coordinate axes. The powers of u give all upper elementary unipotents, and the powers of v give all lower elementary unipotents. These generate SL2(F3). The determinant is the surjective cyclotomic character, hence G=GL2(F3).

The same reasoning applies to the listed Frobenius/transvection witnesses for the other controls. The irreducible quartic 3-division polynomials are recorded as an additional exact check, not as a substitute for this argument. Full image makes the nonzero 3-torsion points one orbit of length eight, matching the intended degree-eight source.
