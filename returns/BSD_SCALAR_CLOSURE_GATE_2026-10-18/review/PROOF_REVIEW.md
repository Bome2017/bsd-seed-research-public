## Exact products and seventh-power saturation

Write gamma_1,...,gamma_60 for the first three translates of the twenty ratios. Their paired later translates are exactly their inverses as field elements. Rational independence of their divisor columns shows that an integer divisor relation among all 120 translates has equal paired exponents. Every such product cancels exactly to one. The entire multiplicative subgroup H therefore contains no ordinary unit except one.

There is no hidden seventh-root workaround within the saturation of this particular subgroup. If x^7=product gamma_j^{a_j} in A^*, reduction of divisors modulo seven and the rank-sixty computation force every a_j to be divisible by seven. Then x/(product gamma_j^{a_j/7}) has seventh power one. The retained field has signature (6,21); a separate exact Sturm check of its field polynomial also gives six real roots (REAL_EMBEDDING_REPLAY.json). Hence its only seventh root of unity is one. Hence x belongs to H. Iteration proves the same for x^(7^n) in H. In this precise sense H is seventh-power saturated in A^*.

This is a finite-family arithmetic conclusion, not a statement about other elements or unrelated units. It does not provide a search bound for constructing those other units. The root proof and report now include this saturation deduction as a finite-family stopping fact.
