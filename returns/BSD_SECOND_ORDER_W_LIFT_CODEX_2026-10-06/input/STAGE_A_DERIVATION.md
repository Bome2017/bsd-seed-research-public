# Tame first-order deformations detecting precisely the excess

Date: 2026-10-06. Status: **WRITTEN_DERIVATION; INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED**.

This is an attempted repair and proof of Stage A, not an import of OpenAI's
generic-fibre obstruction-span lemma. It constructs actual finite Galois
modules over the seed's residual field. The standard arithmetic inputs are
identified below. Neither the full proof nor its seed imports have been
Lean-checked in this return. Literature novelty has not been assessed.

## 1. Statement and scope

Let E/Q be an elliptic curve, p >= 5, k = F_p, V = E[p], and assume the
residual image is GL_2(k). Let S = Sel_p(E/Q) with the actual local Kummer
conditions, and let W be the span of specified rational-point Kummer classes.
For the seed W is the retained saturated plane generated by P1 and P2.

We construct a k-space D of actual tame additive characters, and canonical
linear maps

    b_lambda : S -> S^*                   (lambda in D).

Define, without a complement or a basis of S/W,

    D^W = {lambda in D : b_lambda(kappa(P1)) = b_lambda(kappa(P2)) = 0}.

Then the derived conclusion is

    ker[S -> Hom_k(D^W,S^*), x |-> (lambda |-> b_lambda(x))] = W.       (A)

Equivalently one can use the scalar target

    Hom_k(D^W tensor_k S, k),  x |-> [lambda tensor y |-> b_lambda(x)(y)].

This corrects the scalar target in the prompt: the obstruction of one class
to a Selmer lift is naturally a functional on the dual Selmer space. Killing
two known point obstructions means two **vector equations**, not generally
two scalar equations. They can impose more than two scalar conditions.

No ordinarity is used. In particular the argument, if independently accepted,
covers both ordinary and supersingular good seed primes under the retained
full-image and basis inputs. We assert the seed application on p >= 5, p not
dividing N; no enlargement to a bad-prime theorem is needed for this task.

## 2. The arithmetic directions and local conditions

Fix Sigma containing infinity, p and the bad primes of E. Call a rational
prime q admissible here if q is outside Sigma, q = 1 mod p, and

    rho(Frob_q) - 1 is invertible on V.

These are **deformation primes**, not the trace-two Kurihara primes. We will
use the subfamily with rho(Frob_q) = -I.

For each such q take all additive characters

    lambda_q : Gal(Q(zeta_q)/Q) -> k

whose restrictions at the places of Sigma are zero. View them as characters
of G_Q by inflation. Define D to be their k-linear span, with finite support
in this sum. This definition uses E, p and Sigma, not S/W or its dimension.
Different choices of generator of a degree-p character line give the same D.

For lambda in D let M_lambda be V + epsilon V, epsilon^2 = 0, with action

    g(u,x) = (rho(g)u + lambda(g)rho(g)x, rho(g)x).

Thus 0 -> V -> M_lambda -> V -> 0 is an actual extension, with inclusion
u |-> (u,0) and projection (u,x) |-> x. The action is the scalar twist
rho(g)(1 + epsilon lambda(g)), not a manufactured matrix of desired values.

Equip M_lambda with these local conditions:

* At v in Sigma, lambda is zero, so use the direct sum of the two Kummer
  conditions in the displayed splitting.
* At primes in the ramification support of lambda, use the zero subgroup.
* At all other finite primes use the unramified condition.

These form an exact sequence of Selmerable modules in Morgan--Smith's
sense. Here are the checks, including the middle local condition.

At a new q, H^0(Q_q,V)=0. By the Weil duality V is its Tate dual, so H^2=0.
The local Euler characteristic at q != p gives H^1=0. The same vanishing for
M_lambda follows from its two-step filtration by V. Hence zero is the only
local condition and all local exactness assertions hold. At Sigma exactness
is the direct-sum assertion. At other primes the modules are unramified;
unramified H^1 is computed on the procyclic residue group, whose H^2 is zero.
Surjectivity and the inverse-image condition follow from that residue
sequence and the inertia sequence. Thus both endpoints have precisely their
original Kummer conditions. At good q != p the Kummer condition is unramified.

## 3. A canonical obstruction, including sufficiency

Apply Morgan--Smith, Theorem 1.3, to this exact sequence with its local
conditions. Identify the dual of V with V through the Weil pairing, and
identify (1/p)Z/Z with k. Their Cassels--Tate construction supplies b_lambda.
Its left radical is exactly

    image[Sel(M_lambda) -> S].                              (B)

Consequently D^W has the intrinsic alternative definition: the directions
in which both specified rational-point classes admit Selmer lifts to
M_lambda. No unknown Sha class or chosen quotient basis enters this definition.

The extension for lambda+mu is the Baer sum of the extensions for lambda
and mu. The local conditions agree with that sum: at Sigma they split, at
new ramified primes all three endpoint cohomology groups vanish, and
elsewhere they are the unramified conditions. Morgan--Smith Proposition 4.4
therefore makes lambda |-> b_lambda k-linear. In particular D^W is a subspace.

We will prove the stronger spanning fact needed for (A):

    Sym^2(S^*) is contained in span{b_lambda : lambda in D},           (C)

where a symmetric tensor is viewed as a bilinear form. Equality with the
whole image is unnecessary. We do not invoke an alternation theorem from
the dyadic or induced setting.

## 4. Direct cochain evaluation of b_lambda

Take cocycles x,y representing two classes of S. Their scalar Weil cup
product is locally zero because the Kummer condition is its own annihilator
under local Tate duality. Brauer--Hasse--Noether, with the field Kummer
sequence, gives a global primitive

    du = x cup_e y,     u in C^1(G_Q,mu_p).

All cochains are continuous with finite coefficients. They factor through
finite quotients, so they are unramified outside some finite set. Enlarge
Sigma to a finite Pi **for this proof only**, including those ramified primes.
It suffices to use directions in D that are also locally zero at Pi. This
does not change the prior definition of D or introduce unknown data into it.

Let lambda=lambda_q be one such direction and choose q outside Pi. Write
gamma for its Frobenius, A=(rho(gamma)-1)^(-1)x(gamma). On the unramified
local cochains x=dA. We use the inhomogeneous differential convention

    d u(g,h) = g u(h) - u(gh) + u(g).

For Morgan--Smith Definition 3.2 take the global lift f=(0,x). Then

    df = (lambda cup x,0),
    eta = -lambda cup u,
    d eta = lambda cup x cup_e y.

At q a cocycle lifting x is (lambda cup A,x): the differential of
lambda cup A is -lambda cup dA. The resulting local scalar cocycle in that
definition is

    lambda cup (u - A cup_e y).                            (D)

At Pi it is zero since lambda is zero and the direct-sum local lift is
available. At all other primes it is unramified and has zero local invariant.
Thus local reciprocity evaluates (D) as a nonzero normalization scalar times

    z_gamma(x,y) = (u - A cup_e y)(gamma).                  (E)

Normalize the generator lambda_q so that this scalar is 1, or retain it as
a nonzero scalar multiplying the whole form. Nothing below divides by a
possibly zero arithmetic invariant. This is a scalar change of a tame
character line, not an unspecified period unit.

Unlike a merely necessary lift test, (B) already proves that these pairings
capture the entire obstruction for M_lambda with the stated local conditions.
Equation (E) is obtained by substituting in that canonical construction.

## 5. Evaluation tuples and compatible Frobenius conditions

For the spanning proof only, take a basis x1,...,xs of S, choose cochain
representatives and primitives u_ij bilinearly. This is not a basis of S/W
and is not used to define D, D^W or the universal map. Let J=ker(rho).

Central scalar averaging gives H^1(GL_2(k),V)=0: a nonidentity central
scalar of order prime to p has no invariants on V. Inflation--restriction
therefore makes restriction H^1(Q,V) -> H^1(J,V) injective.

The joint evaluation map J -> V^s for the independent x_i is onto. Its
image is a GL_2(k)-submodule. A proper image in the direct sum of s copies
of the simple module V would yield a nonzero map of its quotient to V and
hence a linear dependence among the restrictions, because End_GL2(V)=k.
Here only the semisimplicity of the particular module V^s is used; the group
algebra k[GL_2(k)] is not assumed semisimple.

Put K=Q(E[p]). For the finite Pi let

    R = Q(mu_p, a^(1/p) : a a rational prime in Pi).

Then K intersect R = Q(mu_p). Indeed K/Q(mu_p) has group SL_2(k), perfect
for p >= 5, whereas R/Q(mu_p) is an abelian p-extension. A common quotient
is therefore trivial. Choose gamma with rho(gamma)=-I and trivial action
on R. Both requirements are compatible, since det(-I)=1.

The Frobenius class of gamma gives q=1 mod p and makes every a in Pi a
p-th power modulo q. Consequently a degree-p character of conductor q is
locally zero at every place in Pi. At infinity the odd-order character is
zero automatically. In particular these are directions in the D defined
before any basis was chosen.

## 6. The commutator calculation gives symmetric matrix units

For v,w in J put c=[v,w]. Since J fixes mu_p, its action on R is through
the abelian group Gal(R/Q(mu_p)); therefore c fixes R. It also fixes V.
The restrictions x_i|J are homomorphisms, so x_i(c)=0.

The primitive equation on J reads

    u_ij(vw)=u_ij(v)+u_ij(w)-e(x_i(v),x_j(w)).

It follows directly, including the inverse terms, that

    u_ij(c)=-e(x_i(v),x_j(w))+e(x_i(w),x_j(v)).            (F)

Replacing gamma by gamma c leaves x_i(gamma) and hence all A_i unchanged.
It changes z_gamma by (F). Both Frobenius prescriptions can be realized at
good rational primes by Chebotarev in one finite Galois quotient recording
rho, R, the cocycles and the primitives. Prime and root markings allow the
specified representative, rather than merely an unnamed conjugacy class.
All excluded sets at this step are finite; no effective prime bound is used.

Take e1,e2 in V with e(e1,e2)=1. Joint surjectivity of the x_i evaluations
lets us prescribe their tuples on v,w independently.

* For the diagonal i, prescribe x_i(v)=e1, x_i(w)=e2 and all other entries
  zero. Formula (F) gives -2 E_ii.
* For i != j, prescribe x_i(v)=e1, x_j(w)=e2 and all other entries zero.
  It gives -(E_ij+E_ji).

These span all symmetric matrices since p is odd. Each is a difference of
two forms b_lambda after normalizing their tame character generators. This
proves (C) using actual arithmetic directions. It does not require every
matrix unit to come from a single auxiliary prime.

## 7. Removing precisely W

Containment W in the kernel in (A) follows by the definition of D^W and
bilinearity. Conversely let x not lie in W. The annihilator of W in S^*
contains a functional phi with phi(x) != 0. The rank-one symmetric form

    (z,y) |-> phi(z)phi(y)

lies in the image in (C). Hence it equals b_lambda for an actual finite
linear combination lambda in D. It kills W in its first variable, so
lambda lies in D^W, but b_lambda(x) != 0. This proves (A).

The functional phi is an existence witness in the proof. No complement or
quotient basis is selected to define the arithmetic map. A finite list of
directions can be extracted after (A), by finite dimensionality of S/W.
This supplies no computable bound on that list, no bound on the conductors,
and no uniform control of its size as p varies.

## 8. What has changed, and what has not

The supplied OpenAI lemma worked over a generic residual parameter field,
using nontrivial old characters and strict/opposite Selmer spaces. Its
denominators cannot simply be specialized to the seed. The proof above
instead uses the seed's V and Kummer conditions, with a different output:
symmetric forms on S suffice to separate S/W.

This is also not the earlier receiver's split-prime evaluation. That map
detects restriction of a class. Here primes have Frobenius -I, all residual
local cohomology at the new prime is zero, and the detecting value is a
secondary cochain expression. The generalized Cassels--Tate theorem ties
it to actual first-order lifting.

The proof remains compatible with any dimension of S/W. Separation is not
an upper bound. In particular it does not prove that these excess classes
are absent from the original S. Nor does a first-order Selmer lift of P1,P2
give flat point sections in a family over a DVR. Those are Stage B and C
obligations, treated separately in NEXT_ARITHMETIC_STEP.md.
