# Scope controls

These are elementary examples checking the logical boundaries of the
written proof. They are not computed seed Kato classes or counterexamples
to BSD. No finite sample is used to prove the placement theorem.

1. **Saturation is necessary for integral placement.** In H=Z_p² take
   W'=pZ_p e₁⊕Z_p e₂ and δ=e₁. Then W'⊗Q_p=H⊗Q_p but δ∉W'.
   The finite Kummer injection excludes exactly this failure for the full
   Mordell–Weil lattice. A pair of merely independent unsaturated points
   would not justify integral coordinates.

2. **Integral does not mean primitive.** With H=W=Z_p², the element
   p e₁ lies in W and is nonzero, but reduces to zero modulo p.
   Equation (8) therefore supplies no unit claim.

3. **The zero branch gives no ambient rank bound.** Take H=Z_p³,
   W=Z_p e₁⊕Z_p e₂, δ=0. The conditional statement
   δ≠0⇒rank H=2 holds, and δ∈W, but rank H is three. Consequently the
   case-split argument cannot itself prove primary finiteness.

4. **Iwasawa weak order leaves all three behaviors.** In Λm the elements
   U m, pU m and U²m have divided augmentations m, pm and zero.
   Once a derivative is placed in W, the same nonzero, nonprimitive,
   and zero possibilities remain uncontrolled.

These controls can be verified directly from the displayed formulas.
No synthetic test count is presented as arithmetic evidence.
