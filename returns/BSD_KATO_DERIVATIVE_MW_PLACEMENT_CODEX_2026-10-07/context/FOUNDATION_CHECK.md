## 3. The integral lattice and the finite part of the tower

Let L_φ be the actual integral image lattice obtained from the modular
parametrization, as in BKS I (2.1.1). Initially make all integral statements
in L_φ, rather than silently declaring its coordinate generator primitive
in T_pE. It is a full Galois-stable lattice in V_pE.

Residual irreducibility proves homothety. Scale L_φ into T_pE so that it is
contained there but not in pT_pE. Its nonzero reduction is a Galois-stable
subspace of E[p], hence equals E[p]. Nakayama gives the scaled lattice
equal to T_pE. This produces a Galois-equivariant integral lattice
isomorphism L_φ→T_pE. Applying it to the cohomology and the source class
simultaneously preserves U-divisibility and rational nonvanishing.

The required power of p has not been computed here. Thus this argument
does NOT prove a unit comparison between two independently normalized
numerical Kato classes. Such an assertion would be inappropriate.
For the weak-order theorem the actual image lattice, or its simultaneously
transported class, suffices; no primitive-generator claim is needed.

The Δ-projection has denominator p−1, a Z_p-unit. Restriction and
corestriction between Q_n and Q(μ_(p^(n+1))) have composites p−1 on the
invariant part. Thus the passage to Q_∞ preserves the relevant lattices
up to this explicitly known unit. There is no degree-p division here.

## 4. Derived control, with the H² term retained

    𝕋 = T ⊗̂_(Z_p) Λ,

sequence of compact G_(Q,S)-modules

    0→𝕋 --U--> 𝕋 →T→0.                                 (F5)

The last map is augmentation. The inverse-action convention makes it
Galois equivariant. Applying continuous derived cohomology gives

    C_Iw --U--> C_Iw →RΓ(Z[1/S],T).

Shapiro identifies C_Iw with the inverse-limit complex over Q_n, where
transition maps are corestriction. One can use simultaneous finite
coefficient and finite field-level quotients; their cohomology groups are
finite, so the resulting countable inverse systems satisfy the
Mittag–Leffler condition. Odd-p global cohomological dimension two gives
no higher-degree term. These statements involve full Galois cohomology,
not a claim that a Selmer condition commutes with specialization.

    0→M/UM→H¹_S→X[U]→0,

## 5. Away-from-p comparison at (U)

Kato §8.2 interprets cohomology on a ring through j_* at the primes not
inverted. The preceding return uses S-cohomology, with all bad primes
inverted. These are not interchangeable by notation.

In degree one, Kato Lemma 8.5 shows that cyclotomic universal norms belong
to the j_* group. Applying it at each level gives the same Iwasawa H¹.
For the Z_p tower one may first adjoin μ_p and then descend using the
prime-to-p trace in §3. This comparison does not use finite Sha.

For the degree-two length comparison it suffices to compare the local
cones at (U), where p has become invertible. For ℓ≠p, their rational
ramification term is built from H¹(I_ℓ,V_pE). Arithmetic Frobenius on it is:

| Reduction type | Rational inertia-cohomology term | Frobenius factor at augmentation |
|---|---|---|
| Good | V_pE(−1) | 1−a_ℓ/ℓ+1/ℓ |
| Split multiplicative | Q_p(−1) | 1−1/ℓ |
| Nonsplit multiplicative | unramified quadratic twist of Q_p(−1) | 1+1/ℓ |
| Additive, potentially good | 0 | 1 |
| Additive, potentially multiplicative | 0 after ramified quadratic invariants | 1 |

For the multiplicative row this follows from the Tate-curve inertia
extension: its one-dimensional quotient acquires the tame-cohomology
twist −1. For potentially good additive reduction finite nontrivial inertia
has no invariant quotient of this two-dimensional realization; positive
finite-group cohomology disappears over Q_p. The ramified quadratic
character removes the potentially multiplicative invariant as well.

Thus the factors are e_ℓ=#E^ns(F_ℓ)/ℓ, all nonzero. Over the local DVR
Λ_(U), a local relation matrix with invertible augmented determinant is
invertible. The local cone therefore contributes zero length there.
Finite integral inertia errors are killed after p is inverted.
Equivalently the local characteristic factors, whose augmentations are
these nonzero e_ℓ, are (U)-units.

For this seed the only extra primes are 2,3,2243,4547,5843. For example
