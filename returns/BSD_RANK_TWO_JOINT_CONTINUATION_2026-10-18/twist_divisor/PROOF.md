# The changed rank-two twist has no faithful valuation source

Let A be the actual degree48 nonzero7-torsion point field of E=[0,-1,1,-22826,2411880], the -47 twist of11a1. The field is irreducible, its exact point has order7, and the exact automorphism sigma3 sends that point to3T. The maximal-order data were computed by nfinit, separately from any class-group approximation.

The actual scalar action on primes above S={7,11,47} has cycles of lengths only1 and3. Consequently sigma3^3 acts trivially on each prime divisor space. On either faithful eigenspace sigma3 acts by3 or5 modulo7, whose cubes are-1. An element in such an eigenspace must therefore satisfy x=-x and is zero. This proves the entire faithful S-divisor source is zero, not merely that a sampled valuation vector vanishes. In particular the primitive incidence divisor kernel allowance is t_D,chi=0 without needing a nonzero target row.

The exact factorizations and permutations are in ACTUAL_DIVISORS.json. The ramification/residue pairs are (6,1),(7,6) at7; three(7,2) and one(1,6) at11; three(2,1), one(2,3), three(2,6) at47. Every decomposition satisfies sum(e*f)=48. The local traces are a11=-1 and a47=0. This agrees with nonsplit multiplicative reduction at11 and quadratic additive inertia at47, but the proof uses the actual prime permutations.

This does not bound ordinary units or Cl(A)[7]^chi. Since valuation vectors cannot survive, the class term in the remaining virtual-unit sequence is the ordinary class group; replacing it with an S-class group would change the source and could erase genuine class-supported directions.
