# Exact central-character exclusion of the first capture

Let sigma act by scalar 3 on the nonzero 7-torsion point field. It has order6 and sigma^3 is scalar -1. The faithful character projector used in the experiment is represented modulo7 by weights (6,2,3,1,5,4).

For a field element a with sigma^3(a)=epsilon a, epsilon in {1,-1}, the three paired weights sum to7. Therefore

    product_{j=0}^5 sigma^j(a)^{(6,2,3,1,5,4)_j}
      = (a sigma(a) sigma^2(a))^7 epsilon^(1+5+4)
      = (a sigma(a) sigma^2(a))^7.

This is an exact seventh-power identity, independently of whether a is an ordinary unit. It excludes any class-supported or unit class generated by these elements from the faithful character component.

The original, unmodified GP checked all 1424 complete exported algebraic polynomials against the exact original field and the independently constructed central matrix. The output was [1424,0,0] for fixed, negated and mixed respectively. Thus the entire captured family is excluded; this does not assert anything about all field elements or all units.

The scalar and NF bindings are inherited from twist_source/field.gp and nf.gp. The patched library is only a source of candidate polynomials; the exact parity audit loads neither it nor its internal relation vectors.

Evidence is preserved in captured_generators.gp.gz and harvest.gp.err.gz. Their raw and compressed hashes are in CAPTURE_COMPRESSION.json. For replay, follow REPLAY.md: decode and hash-check an external temporary working copy, and run with the original GP binary. No extraction is required to inspect the proof itself.
