# What the continued experiment would bound

This uses the actual nonzero 7-torsion point algebra and faithful scalar character of the preceding joint-bound experiment. It does not replace the source with a space spanned by the elements that happen to have been found.

Let A be the actual degree-48 point field of either rank-two control, and let chi be the retained faithful scalar character. Put

    U = (O_A^* / O_A^{*7})^chi,
    V = {a in A^*/A^{*7}: every prime valuation of a is 0 modulo 7}^chi.

The ordinary virtual-unit sequence is

    0 -> U -> V -> Cl(A)[7]^chi -> 0.

It is the ordinary class group, not its S-class quotient. Character projection is exact because the scalar group has order six, invertible modulo seven. The retained equivariant Dirichlet calculation gives dim(U)=4. These claims retain the point-field and full-image hypotheses of the preceding proof.

Let L comprise authenticated global incidence-norm residue rows and the necessary coefficient-seven Kummer-local row. Write r_U=rank(L|U). Choosing any virtual-unit lift of an ordinary class defines a class obstruction in the quotient of the row target by L(U); the obstruction is independent of the lift. If c is the complete class dimension and r_C is the rank of this compatible class obstruction, then

    dim ker(L on V) = (4-r_U)+(c-r_C).

When only lower bounds on the ranks or upper bounds on the dimensions are known, the corresponding statement is an upper bound. Global and local rows must use the same unit correction when a class lift is adjusted. Separate corrections cannot be subtracted twice.

If the valuation layer has a possible kernel, its dimension must also be retained. The new actual divisor calculations give zero such allowance for both 655a1 at S={5,7,131} and the -47 twist of 11a1 at S={7,11,47}; their arguments are written in the respective divisor folders. Thus these rank-two tests isolate exactly the unit and ordinary class terms.

An authenticated collection of units need not generate all units to prove r_U is at least the rank of its displayed image. This is why two genuinely rejecting unit directions would already imply a whole-unit upper bound of two. Conversely, zero rank on a searched collection gives no upper bound on the whole kernel. A complete unit basis would not by itself remove the class term either.

The rational-point plane is a plane in the complete Selmer evaluation source under the retained injection. It need not lie in U. Treating its two directions as ordinary units requires checking their class-group images, not merely knowing the Mordell-Weil rank.

## Exact finite-family exclusion

For the 655a1 tangent source, all principal ideals are factored, their scalar translates give the complete divisor matrix, and the entire integer kernel is computed. Each basis product is then expanded in the actual number field and equals +1 or -1. Every multiplicative product from that kernel is therefore a sign. Since signs are seventh powers, this particular ordinary-unit family has zero image in U. This conclusion is stronger than the local zero tests, but applies only to this finite factor family. It does not exclude other units.

## Endpoint scope

Even a successful complete-source bound on a rank-two control would be an experiment about this method, not a transfer theorem for the seed. A cross-prime result still needs a uniform arithmetic bound on the joint unit kernel and compatible ordinary class contribution. None is obtained by replacing a BNF timeout with a class-zero assumption or by importing a known Sha answer into the producer.
