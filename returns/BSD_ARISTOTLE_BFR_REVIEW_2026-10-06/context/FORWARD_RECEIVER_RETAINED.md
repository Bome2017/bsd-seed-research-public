# A finite class-group receiver for the full residual excess

**Grade: DERIVED, written proof from explicitly retained seed inputs and standard
Kummer/class-field theory. Same-worker checks only. No novelty claim.**

**Endpoint status: no uniform class-group bound, no fixed annihilator, no new
primary conclusion.** This is a comparison construction, not a proof of FIN.

## 1. Inputs and statement

Let E/Q be the seed in its minimal model

    y^2 = x^3 - x^2 - 66020924*x - 65074271436.

For each good prime p >= 5 use these retained inputs:

- V=E[p] has image GL_2(F_p);
- W=E(Q)/pE(Q) has dimension two, generated by the Kummer classes of the
  saturated rational basis P1,P2;
- p does not divide #E(F_p);
- p divides none of the finite Tamagawa factors (their product is 16).

The non-anomalous input holds at all good p>=7 by rational 2-torsion and
0<#E(F_p)<2p; the only possible multiple p in this range is odd. At p=5 the
count is 8. The last input is used for unramifiedness, NOT an assertion that
all bad-prime decomposition-group cohomology vanishes. In particular no
additional exclusion of 19,59,487 is required for the forward construction.

Define

    d_p = 1-p^2-66020924*p^4-65074271436*p^6,
    F_p = Q(sqrt(d_p)),
    R_p = (p^(-2), sqrt(d_p)*p^(-3)) in E(F_p).

Choose Q1,Q2,Q3 with p*Q1=P1, p*Q2=P2, p*Q3=R_p, and put

    L_p = Q(E[p], sqrt(d_p), Q1,Q2,Q3),
    Gamma_p = Gal(L_p/Q),
    A_p = Cl(O_(L_p)[1/p])/p,
    B_p = Hom_(Gamma_p)(A_p,V).

All fields sit inside one chosen algebraic closure. Choices of the Q_i do not
change L_p, since its definition includes E[p]. No unknown Selmer class,
Sha class, L-value, or C_p is used to define L_p or A_p.

**Proposition.** For every good seed p>=5 there is an injective F_p-linear map

    Sha(E/Q)[p]  -->  B_p.                                     (1)

The injection is induced by restriction of Selmer cocycles. It is independent
of the auxiliary pair a,b in the supplied assembly. Equivalently, restriction
injects each X_(p,a,b) into B_p via the assembly's isomorphism X_(p,a,b)=Sha[p].
Both ordinary and supersingular p are covered. No finiteness of Sha, and no
nonzero localization of W at p, are assumed.

The field is Galois and

    Gamma_p = (V + V + V*epsilon_p) semidirect
              (GL_2(F_p) x C_2),
    [L_p:Q] = 2*p^6*(p^2-1)*(p^2-p),                          (2)

where epsilon_p is the nontrivial character of F_p/Q. Isomorphisms in (2)
depend on choices of bases/division points; the field and map in (1) do not.

## 2. The explicit quadratic point supplies the missing local line

Substitution proves that R_p lies on E. The integer d_p is negative and is
1 modulo p^2. Thus F_p is a genuine quadratic field and p splits in F_p.
Both embeddings F_p -> Q_p send sqrt(d_p) to a p-adic unit.

At either embedding, R_p lies in E_1(Q_p), and its formal parameter is

    t(R_p)=-x(R_p)/y(R_p)=-p/sqrt(d_p),
    v_p(t(R_p))=1.                                           (3)

For p>=5 the formal logarithm identifies E_1(Q_p) with p Z_p. In particular
pE_1 corresponds to p^2 Z_p. Because p does not divide #E(F_p), reduction
shows E(Q_p)/p = E_1(Q_p)/pE_1(Q_p), a one-dimensional F_p-space. Equation (3)
therefore shows that R_p generates that local Kummer line.

This is not a uniform non-Wieferich assertion about P1 or P2. R_p changes
with p according to the explicit formula and is forced to be locally primitive.
Its Galois conjugate is -R_p, which generates the same local line.

A common arithmetic source of these auxiliary points is the fixed affine curve

    U^2=1-T^2-66020924*T^4-65074271436*T^6,
    (T,U) |-> (T^(-2),U/T^3), T!=0.

Only the specialization T=p is used. The existence of this fixed source does
not give a bound on the class groups of the fields of its division points.

## 3. Why the new point does not kill an unknown rational Selmer direction

Put K_p=Q(E[p]) and K'_p=K_p F_p. The abelianization of GL_2(F_p) for p>=5 is
its determinant F_p^*. Hence its only quadratic subfield is the quadratic
subfield of Q(mu_p), which is ramified at p. F_p is split at p, so

    F_p cap K_p=Q,
    Gal(K'_p/Q)=G0=GL_2(F_p) x C_2.

The central element (-I,+1) of G0 acts as -1 on both V and V*epsilon_p.
The scalar cohomology lemma gives

    H^i(G0,V)=H^i(G0,V*epsilon_p)=0  for all i.                 (4)

For completeness, the lemma follows because inner conjugation induces the
identity on group cohomology, whereas a central element acts through its
coefficient action. Thus -1 and 1 act identically; 2 is invertible.

The classes of P1,P2 restrict injectively to independent Kummer homomorphisms
over K'_p by (4). R_p is anti-invariant under F_p/Q. Its Kummer class therefore
defines a class r in H^1(Q,V*epsilon_p) (restriction to F_p and the order-two
invariants identify this group with the corresponding anti-invariant part).
The class r is nonzero, by its nonzero localization at p from (3).

Let P be the translation image of G_(K'_p) in

    V + V + V*epsilon_p.

It is a G0-submodule. This direct sum is semisimple as a G0-module: it is a
sum of simple modules. The two indicated simple types are not isomorphic,
since the C2 factor acts differently. If P were proper, a nonzero map from
one of its missing simple quotients to V or V*epsilon_p would give either a
linear dependence of the two restricted point classes or the vanishing of r.
Both are impossible. Thus P is the whole six-dimensional translation module.

The three cocycles together embed Gamma_p into P semidirect G0. The kernel
contains all of P and the projection reaches all of G0; its image is therefore
the whole semidirect product. This proves (2).

Now apply inflation-restriction to 1 -> P -> Gamma_p -> G0 -> 1. Since P
acts trivially on V and (4) applies,

    H^1(Gamma_p,V) = Hom_(G0)(P,V) = F_p^2.                  (5)

The two generators in (5) are precisely the two point cocycles. In particular
there is no extra V-valued inflation class contributed by the anti-invariant
point r: Hom_(G0)(V*epsilon_p,V)=0.

Applying inflation-restriction to G_Q -> Gamma_p therefore gives

    ker[H^1(Q,V) -> H^1(L_p,V)] = W.                          (6)

This establishes exactly which global classes were killed. It is not an
assumption that a field extension preserves every unknown class.

## 4. The restricted Selmer classes are unramified everywhere

Because L_p contains E[p], H^1(L_p,V)=Hom_cont(G_(L_p),V). For a rational
Selmer class s, its restriction is Gamma_p-equivariant.

At a finite place q!=p, the p-part of the Tamagawa factor is trivial. The
residual Kummer condition is therefore the unramified condition (Dainobu,
Proposition 2.8, or the corresponding local Kummer argument). Thus s|_(I_q)
is a coboundary. Restricting to inertia over L_p, where the action on V is
trivial, makes that coboundary zero. This remains true even when L_p/Q is
ramified at q. It uses no unjustified bad-place local acyclicity.

At p the local Kummer group has dimension one and is generated by the local
class of R_p. At a chosen place of L_p above p, the point Q3 is rational,
so that local Kummer class restricts to zero. Hence every local finite class,
and in particular s, restricts to zero on the entire decomposition group.
This holds at every place above p because p splits in F_p and -R_p has the
same spanning property.

At archimedean places there is no odd-primary obstruction; L_p is in fact
totally imaginary because it contains F_p.

Consequently res(s) factors through the everywhere-unramified abelian
exponent-p extension of L_p, with all places over p split. Global class field
theory identifies its Galois group with

    Cl(O_(L_p)[1/p])/p = A_p.

Combining this factorization with (6) and the Kummer exact sequence proves (1).

## 5. What upper bound would now suffice

Let s_p=dim Sha(E/Q)[p]. From (1),

    s_p <= dim B_p.

With retained even parity, a theorem dim B_p<=1 cofinitely would suffice for
the uniform step. This is a SUFFICIENT bound, not an equivalence: B_p can
contain classes not descending from the original Selmer group.

There is also a divisibility consequence. A basis of the image in B_p gives
an equivariant surjection A_p -> V^(s_p). Indeed a proper submodule of the
semisimple V^(s_p) would yield a linear dependence of the coordinate maps,
since End_(Gamma_p)(V)=F_p. Hence

    p^(2*s_p) divides #Cl(O_(L_p)[1/p]).                       (7)

In particular a nonzero Sha[p] forces p^4 to divide this class number, by
parity. Neither a bound excluding (7), nor any useful uniform annihilator,
is proved here.

## 6. Limits

- Finite A_p for each p is NOT one fixed finite group.
- The field degrees grow like 2p^10. No class-group computations are proposed.
- The fixed curve providing R_p does NOT imply compatible class-group bounds.
- This construction is not the earlier exact m_p strict-Selmer identity over
  Q(E[p]); it is a forward injection over a different, larger field.
- It removes the two known rational directions and forces local splitting at
  p independently, including when both P1 and P2 are locally p-divisible.
- It does not evaluate C_p, shorten a Kato witness, or prove the uniform step.
- The new derivation requires independent mathematical review. The exact
  checks test the displayed elementary ingredients, not global class groups.
