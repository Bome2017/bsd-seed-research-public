# Rank-mechanism screen: natural torsion functions and the odd scalar character

2026-10-18. Written deductions; not a uniform arithmetic rank bound. This subtask supplies an exact rejection test for a proposed explicit-unit construction, and fixes the numerical rank threshold that any replacement must meet. It does not modify prior source files.

## 1. What would suffice

Let p be odd and assume the retained full-image/incidence framework. Put d=(p+1)/2, let r be a proved rank lower bound for the actual incidence norm on the complete ordinary chi-unit space, t_D the actual primitive divisor-norm cokernel dimension, and k_C the kernel dimension of the actual ordinary p-class norm from source to companion in the matched characters.

The preceding exact class-map sequence gives

    dim K <= d-r+t_D+k_C.

Hence a whole-source upper bound of three requires

    r >= (p-5)/2+t_D+k_C,

and an upper bound of two requires

    r >= (p-3)/2+t_D+k_C.

The class term is not dropped. A fixed number of nonzero norm witnesses cannot meet these thresholds as p grows, unless separate arithmetic first shrinks the source or gives more independent norm rows. On the seed's retained rank-two/parity domain, a whole-source upper bound of three suffices to force Sha[p]=0 because Sel_p injects into K and its quotient by the point plane has even dimension. This is a consequence of the retained parity input, not a new parity theorem.

## 2. Parity kills the simplest proposed explicit sources

Write tau for the scalar automorphism T -> -T of the nonzero-p-torsion point field A. In A^*/A^{*p}, the descent character chi satisfies tau=-1. If f is any nonzero source element with

    tau(f)=f or tau(f)=-f,

then [tau(f)]=[f], since -1=(-1)^p. Thus [f] is in the plus part. Applying the odd character projector gives

    e_chi[f]=0.

This proof is exact over the number field; it is not inferred from the finite checks. It also applies after norms between compatible torsion fields, since those norms commute with tau.

For a generalized Weierstrass model let w=2y+a1*x+a3. The negation law gives x(-T)=x(T) and w(-T)=-w(T). Consequently every nonzero expression in the multiplicative group generated by

* x(aT)-x(bT), for a not congruent to plus/minus b;
* w(aT), and ratios w(aT)/w(bT);
* any homogeneous parity-eigen division-function value,

has trivial odd-character projection. Even when such elements are actual S-units, they furnish zero norm-rank witnesses. This excludes the indicated homogeneous multiplicative family only. Sums or shifts such as (w-c)/(w+c) are not covered: tau inverts those, so they can survive the parity test.

There is a related moduli qualification. A genuine weight-zero modular function depending only on the isomorphism class of (E,T) takes equal values at (E,T) and (E,-T): the map [-1] is an isomorphism between the marked elliptic curves. Such a specialized function is also killed by the odd projector. This statement concerns invariant functions with no extra rigidifying datum. It does not exclude functions on a larger rigidified moduli problem, sections with separately justified trivializations, Kato cohomology classes, or the shifted fixed-model functions just mentioned.

## 3. Concrete exact control

check_parity.py reads the sealed actual 389a1 torsion table at q=1831 and independently implements the curve law. It checks scalar multiplication on its 24 nonzero 5-torsion points and uses

    e_chi = 4*sigma_1 + 2*sigma_2 + 3*sigma_3 + sigma_4 mod5.

Four w-values, four nonzero x-differences and six w-ratios give 14 candidates. For every candidate at every torsion point, its projected value has fifth-power residue 1: 336 exact checks. The same script checks cancellation of projector weights in +/- pairs at 13 primes from3 through43. These are controls of the parity calculation, not actual global unit authentication or seed upper bounds.

The earlier completed control has an actual compact unit with nontrivial projected norm. Therefore the rejection here does not say all unit constructions vanish; it shows that its successful nonhomogeneous arithmetic cannot be replaced by this much simpler homogeneous torsion-coordinate family.

## 4. Scope of the affine norm: cohomology, then local Selmer admission

A fresh reading of Schaefer--Stoll's long version, Corollary7.9 and Proposition7.10 (printed pp18--19), confirms that scalar compatibility plus the actual affine incidence norm being a global pth power characterizes the image of H^1(Q,E[p]). The source with ramification restricted to S corresponds to H^1(Q,E[p];S). Selmer additionally imposes the local Kummer conditions. Thus the full global norm kernel is a legitimate containing space for Selmer; equality with Selmer cannot be assumed merely from the norm calculation. A sufficiently small containing-space bound is nevertheless enough for the rank-two seed.

Reference: https://mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/papers/p-descent-long.pdf . The ftp-host retrieval failed, but the ordinary HTTPS host was freshly readable. No claim is made that the new arithmetic threshold is in that paper.

## 5. Incidence compositions do not fix the rank

The identities vu=p and uv=p leave all diagonal Smith possibilities

    u=diag(p*I_s,I_(d-s)), v=diag(I_s,p*I_(d-s))

for 0<=s<=d. They therefore do not prove the required rank lower bound. The existing general_character/PROOF.md supplies the stronger G-stable lattice countercontrol preserving the total geometric index. Neither countercontrol is claimed to arise from units of an elliptic torsion field.

The source-independent construction now being tested by the parent task is a shifted parity-breaking family with its finite-ideal support checked before any rank claim. Its results belong to that separate experiment. This note proves no new whole-source bound, no cofinite assertion, and no new seed-prime result.
