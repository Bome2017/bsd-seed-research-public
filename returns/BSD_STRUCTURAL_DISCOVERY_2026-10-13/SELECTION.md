# Selection and historical collision check

The request was a bounded discovery experiment concerning the arithmetic
that distinguishes genuine seed excess from rational-point classes. It was
not authorization to substitute another individual-prime certificate.

## Candidates examined before production

1. **Two tame primes, full quadratic augmentation. EXCLUDED as a repeat.**
   The September 29 W-independence and mod-p^k returns already contain full
   composite-conductor calculations, including six C25 x C25 cases. A new
   copy of that calculation would not test a new relation.
2. **One tame and one cyclotomic variable. OPEN, not run.**
   The historical TAME_WILD_CORRECTION explicitly leaves the mixed coefficient
   uncomputed. A feasible 389a1/5/101 test was specified. Its successful
   determinant-shape comparison would still leave a pointwise scalar; it was
   not selected as the main experiment in this turn. No composite numerator
   or new mixed-coefficient result is reported.
3. **Actual seed 3-descent, unit/class filtration. SELECTED.**
   The record already contains two explicit seed cubic classes and two
   point classes, with independent exact verification. The old search tests
   local conditions, norm, and additivity and obtains a four-dimensional
   subspace. The present intervention holds those classes fixed and asks
   which part of their quotient is visible to valuations, global units and
   ideal classes. It does not repeat the local-only-versus-global rank test.

## Why this is a discovery test

The independently given cubics are the positive-excess cases for the actual
seed. Their lower-bound certificate does not depend on class groups or an
analytic BSD quotient. The tested claim is that ideal classes separate both
excess directions from rational points. The sealed alternatives allow that
claim to fail, partly or completely. Its answer determines whether a future
class-group-only source bound would be missing an actual unit direction.

This is a calculation of arithmetic structure, not a demonstration that the
structure has a uniform bound. The full-image, good-prime source comparison
remains a distinct research problem. No claim of literature novelty or an
exhaustive corpus search is made.
