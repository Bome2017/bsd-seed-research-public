## What this teaches the next experiment

A class-group-only exclusion would miss a genuine unit-type excess direction already present on this seed. A ramification-only exclusion would miss both. The next independently controlled source bound would have to account simultaneously for (i) units satisfying the full coefficient/additivity constraints modulo point units and (ii) the appropriate ideal-class quotient modulo point images. The explicit norm-one unit is now a concrete specimen on which those constraints can be tested. No claim is made that a bound for these components at all good coefficient primes has been found.

Coefficient3 is a bad prime for the seed and lies outside the p>=5 good-prime Kato/receiver domains discussed elsewhere. No ordinary/full-image theorem is transported from3 merely because this experiment succeeds. The classical exact Kummer source filtration is the common structure; its ranks and local conditions may change with the coefficient prime.
