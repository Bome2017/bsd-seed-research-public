# What the experiment measures

Let K be the seed's degree-eight 3-torsion-point field. Let A be the
four-dimensional subspace of K*/K*^3 generated by the two certified point
images and the two certified cubic images. Let W be the point plane.
The retained explicit-cover certificate proves independence. Identifying
A/W with the full Sha[3], rather than a two-dimensional subgroup, additionally
uses the project's separate upper bound. No L-value is used in the experiment.

For this experiment take all finite prime ideals of K and define
v:A -> direct_sum_P F_3 by valuations modulo 3. Put A0=ker(v), W0=W cap A0.
There is an exact sequence

    0 -> A0/W0 -> A/W -> v(A)/v(W) -> 0.

Indeed, a coset of W has trivial image in v(A)/v(W) exactly when subtracting
an element of W gives a representative in A0. Thus a ramification test on
one chosen representative is not intrinsically a test on its Sha coset.

For a in A0, its principal fractional ideal is I^3. The ideal I is unique
because the fractional-ideal group is free abelian. Define c(a)=[I] in Cl(K)[3].
Multiplying a representative by a cube changes I by a principal ideal, so c
is well defined and linear. Its kernel consists of classes represented by
global units: if I=(b), then a/b^3 is a unit, and the converse is immediate.
This proves the usual ideal/unit exact sequence here without importing a
Sha-finiteness assumption.

Let U=A0 cap (O_K* / O_K*^3), and UW=W cap U. Then

    0 -> U/UW -> A0/W0 -> c(A0)/c(W0) -> 0.

To check the middle kernel, c(a)=c(w) for some w in W0 exactly when a-w is
represented by a unit. Therefore the three numbers

    dim v(A)-dim v(W),
    dim U-dim UW,
    dim c(A0)-dim c(W0)

are nonnegative and sum to dim(A/W)=2. This is a filtration; a canonical
direct-sum decomposition is not asserted.

Deleting one allowed ramification prime repeats the first exact sequence
with only its valuation rows. No local-solubility condition is changed and
no new Selmer computation is implied. The intervention asks how existing
classes enter a specified global source, not whether they remain Selmer.

## Discovery criterion

The candidate claim is that the known seed excess is separated from its
point plane by the unramified class-group part. It passes in its strongest
form only if the valuation and unit quotients both have dimension zero,
while the class-group quotient has dimension two. Other patterns distinguish
a unit contribution, a ramified contribution, or an ineffective class-group
detector. The arithmetic output, not dimension of Cl(K)[3] alone, decides.

This calculation can guide a source construction or a necessary restriction
on candidate classes. It does not bound class groups or units in the varying
p-torsion fields, and does not imply any cofinite Sha statement. The p=3
source has to be compared with those fields explicitly before transfer.

## Evidence boundaries

Exact ideal factorization and valuation identities do not require a complete
class group. If class coordinates use the saved GRH-conditional bnf object,
conclusions depending on its class-group completeness retain that condition.
No conditional classification may upgrade the independent exact covering
certificate or be reported as a new proof of the seed's Sha order.
