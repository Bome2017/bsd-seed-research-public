## The old failed hypothesis has a precise repair

The historical Gras screen stopped because the full torsion Galois group has order divisible by the coefficient prime. That objection applies to using the theorem with that acting group over Q. It does not apply to every subextension.

Under full GL2(F_p), let k be the projective field Q(P^1(E[p])), of degree p+1. Choose a nonzero point P and a nonzero covector lambda with lambda(P)=0. Their point and covector fields A,B have compatible embeddings into the torsion field K. Their compositum L=A(mu_p)=AB is fixed by the order-p unipotent subgroup in the Borel stabilizing the line of P. Hence

    Gal(L/k) = (F_p^*)^2,

of order prime to p. It contains mu_p, so the prime-to-p premise in Gras's reflection theorem is satisfied on L/k.

These compatible embeddings are not the incidence pair lambda(P)=1 used to evaluate the affine norm. The latter compositum has a different degree. Atp3 the two degrees are16 and24 respectively. The program check_group_scope.py checks these groups atp3,5,7. For generalp, k is fixed by all scalar multiplications; it is NOT A^{+/-1} unless p=3.

## What the primary theorem actually gives

Freshly downloaded and visually read: Gras, *Theoremes de reflexion*, JTNB10(1998), Theorem7.7, printedp450. It bounds a reflected class-group **rank difference** by a component of p-primary units, then by an archimedean term. It is not a bound on class-group length and it does not discard the primary-unit correction.

Atp3 the common quartic field has signature(2,1). Each degree-eight point/dual field has signature(2,3). For the two reflected nontrivial quadratic characters, the archimedean allowance is2. Thus the immediate consequence is only

    r_A^- <= r_B^- + 2,       r_B^- <= r_A^- + 2.

Adding ordinary anti-unit rank2 yields at best a crude valuation-zero source bound r_B^-+4. It does not reach the rank-two seed target. The exact primary-unit term could be smaller, but computing it requires genuine local arithmetic at3, and allowing ramification at the rational setS requires the corresponding S/T ray-class form. The ordinary unramified formula must not be silently reused there.

For generalp the relevant archimedean character allowance grows withp, so this immediate reflection inequality is not itself the missing small uniform estimate. This is a limitation of the inspected application, not a no-go theorem for reflection or derived refinements.

The source URL is https://www.numdam.org/article/JTNB_1998__10_2_399_0.pdf. The downloaded file and p450 image are retained with hashes. ## Exact mirror distinction

V^* is the residual representation of E^(-3) atp3 because det(V) is the quadratic mod3 cyclotomic character. It is not the Cartier dual of V: that dual is V^* tensor mu_3, isomorphic to V by the Weil pairing. A direct local Tate pairing between the two mirror Selmer spaces cannot be asserted merely from the point/covector construction. The incidence norm maps are actual field maps and avoid this invalid shortcut.
