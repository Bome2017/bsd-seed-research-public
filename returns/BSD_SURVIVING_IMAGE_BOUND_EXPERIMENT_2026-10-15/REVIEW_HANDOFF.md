# Review handoff

Review the independent arithmetic bound before judging the status label. The critical passages are:

1. The retained ordinary-unit index s_A=1+c_B-c_A, including the real determinant of the incidence map and cancellation of the plus fields.
2. The exact lattice-index sign when ordinary units are enlarged to S-units. S is defined by the same rational primes, with every prime above S retained.
3. Projection of the actual global kernel to Cl(A,S)^-[3]. The upper bound adds its rank, not its length; using length only weakens the bound.
4. The actual primitive divisor matrices, as opposed to a matrix on a guessed S-unit subgroup. The new certificates give exact weak-approximation valuations and norms.
5. The1913 reverse identities. They refute the boundary-is-point shortcut only under the retained membership and rank inputs, which are not freshly recertified.

The producer of the independent bound reads curve/field/S/torsion geometry and cached class-group data. It does not read local admission rows or use Sha values. Historical data files can contain additional fields which the producer does not consume; inspect the code's accesses rather than interpreting file presence as proof of independence.

Endpoint acceptance requires a new bound on the seed's actual excess. This packet does not meet that threshold. It does meet the narrower experimental objective of constructing and evaluating an independent upper bound on complete containing sources, with both units and classes retained, and using a positive control to reject an invalid simplification.
