# A companion S-class upper bound at coefficient three

Grade: written deduction under the retained integral incidence identities, ordinary-unit index formula, source embedding and certified number-field inputs. No novelty claim. This bounds the entire containing global norm kernel, before local admission; it is not a rank computed from that kernel.

Let A be the nonzero 3-torsion point field and B its dual affine-line field for a full-GL2(F3) representation. Let tau be the internal scalar -1. Let S be one finite set of rational primes and use **all** primes above S in each field and in each plus subfield. All lattices below are tensored with Z3 and projected to the minus eigenspace. The projector (1-tau)/2 is integral over Z3.

Write U_A for ordinary units, U_AS for S-units, D_AS for the primitive free divisor lattice on primes of A above S, and Lambda_A for the valuation image of U_AS in D_AS. Write c_A, c_AS for the Z3-lengths of the minus ordinary and S-class groups, and r_AS for dim_F3 Cl(A,S)^-[3]. Use corresponding B notation.

The incidence maps u:A->B and v:B->A mean the actual multiplicative incidence norms; on divisors and units we use their induced additive lattice maps. On minus components the retained integral identities are

    vu=3 Id,       uv=3 Id.

They follow by counting incidence fibers and cancelling pairs P,-P. They imply injectivity, equal lattice ranks, and cokernels killed by3. In particular, the length of such a cokernel equals the dimension of the mod3 kernel. No Smith form on an unsaturated subgroup is substituted for a primitive lattice index.

## 1. The independent divisor index

Define

    t_D = length_Z3 coker(u:D_AS -> D_BS).

The divisor lattices here are freely generated by differences of primes interchanged by tau. They are not the valuation images of a guessed set of S-units. The new producer computes u on these primitive generators by exact ideal approximation: an element pi_i has prescribed valuations delta_ij at every prime above S, and pi_i/tau(pi_i) realizes the prime difference. The other prime divisors of pi_i are irrelevant for this restricted map, since a rational-prime-preserving norm cannot transfer them into S.

## 2. Passing from ordinary units to S-units

There are exact sequences of Z3 lattices and finite modules

    0 -> U_A -> U_AS -> Lambda_A -> 0,
    0 -> Lambda_A -> D_AS -> H_A -> 0,

where H_A is the minus subgroup of Cl(A) generated by primes above S. Hence

    length H_A = c_A-c_AS.

The same sequences hold for B, and u is compatible with all their maps. Taking determinants on the free lattice sequences gives

    s_AS = s_A + length coker(u:Lambda_A->Lambda_B),

where s_A is the ordinary-unit norm index and s_AS is the S-unit norm index. Comparing each Lambda with its primitive D lattice gives

    length coker(u:Lambda_A->Lambda_B)
      = t_D + (c_A-c_AS) - (c_B-c_BS).

The signs can be checked directly in rank one: replacing the source lattice by 3^a Z3 and the target by 3^b Z3 changes the norm determinant valuation by a-b.

The retained ordinary-unit comparison is

    s_A = 1+c_B-c_A.

Its proof uses the literal incidence maps, their real determinant, arithmetic equivalence of the vector and covector fields, and the analytic **number-field** class-number formula. It does not use BSD or Sha. At3 the two plus fields are isomorphic: projective covectors correspond equivariantly to their kernels, which are projective vectors. The ordinary plus class contributions therefore cancel. Because S is chosen by the same rational primes, this isomorphism also matches the plus S-class groups; a different choice of primes would not justify the same simplification. The retained proof is bound as inputs/ordinary_index_proof.md.

Combining the equations yields

    s_AS = 1+t_D+c_BS-c_AS.                       (1)

All c terms in (1) mean **minus lengths**, not total class numbers and not 3-ranks.

## 3. Bounding unit and class contributions together

Let A(S,3) denote the multiplicative classes with valuations divisible by3 outside S. Its exact sequence is

    0 -> U_AS/3 -> A(S,3)^- -> Cl(A,S)^-[3] -> 0.

Let K_A be the kernel of the actual incidence norm on A(S,3)^-. Projecting K_A to the final class group gives an image of dimension at most r_AS. The kernel of that projection is the norm kernel on S-units modulo cubes. A cube root of an S-unit in B is again an S-unit; thus this kernel has dimension s_AS. Therefore

    dim_F3 K_A <= s_AS+r_AS
      = 1+t_D+c_BS-(c_AS-r_AS)
      <= 1+t_D+c_BS
      <= 1+t_D+v3(#Cl(B,S)).                     (2)

No assertion that a class direction can be repaired is required. The bound permits every class direction to survive, but its cost cancels against the source-class denominator in the independent unit index. This is precisely the feature missing from a bound that controls only admitted units.

If the retained Schaefer--Stoll source embedding places Sel_3(E/Q) in K_A for this S, then

    dim_F3 Sel_3(E/Q) <= 1+t_D+v3(#Cl(B,S)),
    dim_F3 Sha(E/Q)[3] <= 1+t_D+v3(#Cl(B,S))-rank E(Q),

assuming E(Q)[3]=0, as supplied by full residual image. No Sha finiteness or analytic BSD order enters this implication. A negative right side would signal inconsistent inputs, not a negative group dimension.

This is an independent arithmetic upper certificate because its inputs are the prime-decomposition incidence map and the companion S-class group, together with source eligibility and Mordell--Weil rank. It does not define its input using the rank of a Selmer matrix or the size of its surviving image.

## 4. Executed bounded test

The saved certified fields for389a1 and its -3 twist were used. S={3,389}. Their ordinary class groups are trivial and C3, respectively; every prime above S has zero ideal class, so the S-class groups are unchanged. The literal affine-line field was independently matched to the saved companion field by an exact field inclusion of equal degree.

The forward primitive divisor norm matrix is

    diag(1,-1).

Thus t_D=0. Its reverse matrix, in compatible divisor bases under the canonical double dual, is 3 times its inverse, diag(3,-3), so the reverse t_D is2. These are obtained from the divisor norm and its integral composition, not solved backwards from a known Selmer dimension.

Using total companion S-class lengths, (2) gives

    389a1:      dim K_A <= 1+0+1 = 2,
    mirror389:  dim K_B <= 1+2+0 = 3.

The producer does not read point columns, norm-kernel columns, local Kummer rows, or any Sha order. Its only source-record fields are the curve, field polynomial, rational set S and torsion point needed to construct the incidence map. The certified BNF objects are reused; no new BNF computation or certification is performed.

After these outputs were sealed, they may be compared with the separately computed complete norm kernels, of dimensions2 and3. The independent bound is sharp on this pair. The retained two independent rational points then exhaust the389 upper bound. This reproves an already settled control at3, not a new seed prime.

## 5. A positive-excess control without a companion class computation

A separately sealed extension applied the same primitive divisor calculation to1913b1. Its saved point-field BNF is certified and has trivial class group. No companion BNF was available or constructed. The actual divisor matrix is the one-by-one matrix (-1), so the divisor map is injective modulo3.

For any class in the global norm kernel, its valuation vector modulo3 is therefore zero: its norm is a cube and the divisor map has no mod3 kernel. With Cl(A)[3]=0, the ordinary ideal-class sequence shows that this class has a global-unit representative. The complete ordinary antiunit rank is2 by the field signatures. Consequently

    dim_F3 K_A <= 2

without calculating a companion class group, a Selmer kernel or a local admission matrix. This argument uses the **ordinary** source class group, not just Cl(A,S): a class killed by S can remain relevant after all valuations are zero.

The independent upper bound2 allows genuine excess when the rational rank is zero, as it is for this control. Its previously certified two genuine excess classes attain the bound. They are used only in this subsequent comparison, not to produce the upper bound.

## 7. What remains unbounded

The companion class term in (2) is a genuine number-field invariant, not an automatically small quantity. The mirror illustrates this: the independent global bound is3 and local arithmetic is still needed to remove its three candidates. The seed's retained genuine class-supported excess prevents replacing the class term by zero in general.

The deduction here is specifically the p=3 minus component. For larger coefficient primes the relevant scalar character and its inverse must be kept distinct, and the whole minus component is larger than the descent component. The p3 constant1 and class-number formula have not been promoted to a uniform bound for those character components. Neither current computation estimates companion class terms across p. FIN, ORDER9 and Q36 remain unchanged.

Ordinary cup products of two locally admitted Kummer classes cannot supply an additional zero condition: those local Kummer images are already isotropic. A Cassels--Tate row requires the secondary covering/lift data documented in the prior SECONDARY_REPAIR_DEFECT.md, and a nonzero row on genuine Sha does not exclude that class.
