## Concrete algebra controls retained

Over a DVR O, the two-term complex with differential
diag(pi^3,pi^3,pi,...,pi) has integral H1=0, yet its mod-pi^3 H1 is
(O/pi^3)^2 plus an arbitrary number of residue-field summands.

Over R=Z_p[[T]], the module
R + (R/(T))^2 + (R/(T+p))^(2e) has rank one, doubled torsion, mu=0,
and no finite submodule, but augmentation torsion F_p^(2e). This is an
abstract countercontrol to a coarse inference, not a seed arithmetic example.

The relevant old factor-base matrix is an equivariant Hom-space problem.
Do not count the two vector coordinates of E[p] as two independent
multiplicity directions. A bound on raw divisor count is not a bound on
that Hom-space kernel.

## Collision procedure for a candidate

Search the exact proposed object, its map and its normalization in the
register and current returns. Record the matching source file and first
failed step. State what your construction changes. If it merely supplies
an equivalent name, a general implication, or an already available local
operation, do not spend the run developing it further. If the implication
is repaired by new arithmetic, the candidate remains admissible even if
its final criterion is equivalent to Sha vanishing.

This task does not require novelty claims. A legitimate existing theorem
that closes the seed obligation under checked hypotheses is sufficient.
