# How to carry the research forward in this run

This is a discovery workflow, not a claimed new theorem or a prescribed winning
method. No particular candidate is asserted to have passed its arithmetic gate.

## 1. Choose a construction, not a theorem-shaped label

Use the record to locate a specific source element, map, family, or estimate
that might supply upper control. Consider distinct possibilities if necessary,
but select one for sustained work. For each considered mechanism write briefly:

- The actual source: an element, cycle, class, integral lattice, arithmetic
  function, or independently bounded object, with its definition and domain.
- The operation to perform on it, including the first presently unproved
  equation. “Apply an Euler system” or “prove multiplicity one” is not an operation.
- Which source data are already controlled independently of Sha and which are
  not. A newly named unknown scalar does not supply independent control.
- The precise old failure, and what changed input or argument could avoid it.
- The implication from a successful construction to FIN, conditional ORDER9,
  Q36, or a concrete missing step of that mechanism.

Consult the relevant wall card and underlying proof, not just its headline.
If the headline overstates the proof, use the narrower proved scope and record
the correction. Do not reject a family merely because its name appears in the
stop register. Do not claim to have read unavailable raw evidence.

## 2. Perform the missing arithmetic operation

Build the proposed element or map, derive the proposed identity in one named
normalization, or work directly on the estimate. Keep the integrality factors,
coefficient-prime dependence, source grading and exceptional sets explicit.
If a denominator or local case obstructs the operation, use the same kind of
repair that succeeded in BFR, then immediately return to the control mechanism.

Do not turn an open global problem into a mandatory prerequisite for every
attempt. An alternative realization can bypass an unfinished comparison, but
its own integral source and specialization must be proved. Conversely, do not
declare an integral unit merely because two rational realizations agree.

## 3. Try to preserve two excess directions

For a receiver-based proposal, test whether the new identities remain
compatible with two independent elements of B_p. Exhibit the compatibility if
you can. Then identify which additional *arithmetic property of the real
source* would exclude it and attempt to prove that property.

An abstract countermodel only refutes deductions from the assumptions it
satisfies. It need not realize this seed or its class group. A finite-field
control is not a seed computation. Failure to construct a countermodel is not
a proof of the proposed theorem.

Principal relations vanish on every true receiver functional. Therefore do
not ask for a nonzero relation minor “on B_p.” For a relation-rank argument,
define the ambient candidate space and impose the actual rows there. Prove
its kernel contains B_p, then prove any new bound on that kernel. Establish
the arithmetic rows and independence; coordinate count or a nonzero raw row
does not establish codimension at most one.

Known balanced-tail, Weil-cup and norm-division failures remain relevant at
their exact scopes. Repair requires a new arithmetic relation or estimate,
not another combination of the already imposed rows. If the proposed source
reproduces an uncontrolled C_p or equivalent witness, record the identity and
seek a different source of control; do not merely rename it again.

For another endpoint, use the corresponding check: is the proposed ORDER9
bound independent of assumed BSD, or does the proposed Q36 comparison add
actual arithmetic information beyond rewriting the analytic derivative?

## 4. Use partial results rather than stopping at them

A complete cofinite theorem would be a major success. A useful intermediate
theorem can instead remove a specific missing source, comparison, integrality,
or independence step in a concrete mechanism. Explain how the mechanism uses
it, which hypotheses are now discharged, and why the remaining task is not
just the old task with changed notation.

If the result is only better coverage or another presentation, keep it as
supporting material and continue the selected construction while resources
remain. If a proposed partial bound improves a p-dependent quantity by a
constant, do not call it an eventual uniform bound unless the additional
iteration or growth estimate is established.

You may change methods. Do not force the receiver, a fixed rational scalar,
ordinary Hida theory, or any other previously favored architecture. A new
arithmetic route to Q36 or conditional ORDER9 is admissible. A fresh bounded
calculation is justified only by the identity it distinguishes, not as a
replacement for the infinite-prime problem.

## 5. Formalize what the mathematics now warrants

Reuse the accepted Lean sources. Formalize the new finite arithmetic,
linear-algebra or other tractable proof steps when this helps verify the
construction. A missing Mathlib implementation does not require abandoning a
rigorous written arithmetic proof. Separate written imports, formalized
statements, external computation and conjectures.

The original formalization/build/axiom contract still applies. Do not introduce
the missing arithmetic claim as a field of a structure and report its abstract
consequence as a concrete seed theorem.

## 6. Return an explicit change in mathematical knowledge

Alongside PROOF.md, ASSUMPTIONS.json, checks and the original return files,
write `SEED_DELTA.md` with:

1. The exact quantified statement now proved and its prime domain.
2. The independently controlled arithmetic input newly used or constructed.
3. The displayed implication to the selected seed obligation.
4. The actual before/after: which previously unresolved premise is discharged,
   or which previously allowed excess configuration is now ruled out.
5. The first remaining arithmetic equation, with the attempted construction
   already carried as far as this run supports.

An honest useful partial theorem is acceptable. A scoped unsuccessful attempt
is also a legitimate outcome to report truthfully; it is not to be disguised
as progress through the upper bound. Do not substitute a new task brief for
the requested construction work. Preserve candidate failures so the next run
can repair or bypass their actual first failed implications.
