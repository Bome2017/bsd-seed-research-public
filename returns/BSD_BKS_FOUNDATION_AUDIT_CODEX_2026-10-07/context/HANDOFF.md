multiplier and an independently controlled fixed nonzero rational scalar.
For the analytic route the candidate scalar is q_E, whose rationality is
still a separate obligation.

Kato source. Attempt to prove a comparison into the rank-two point line

the independent nonvanishing/integrality that would make the conditional

assume regulator unitness is necessary, or claim the −23 computation is

is a routine implementation or that all other routes are exhausted.
