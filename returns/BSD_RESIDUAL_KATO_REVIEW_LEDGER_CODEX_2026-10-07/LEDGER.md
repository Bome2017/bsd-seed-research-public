## Normalization guard

Do not replace (A_p,B_p) by p^(−r)(A_p,B_p) merely because both coordinates
are divisible by p^r. The matched integral inequality is attached to the
specified Euler-system source, not to every rational rescaling of its
augmentation.

There are two separate issues:

1. Divisibility of the augmented coordinates need not lift to divisibility
   of the Iwasawa class. As an elementary illustration, in M=R m with
   R=Z_p[[U]], y=(p+U)m has augmentation p·aug(m), but y/p is not in M.
   Dividing the coordinates therefore need not define an integral family.
2. Even if y/p is integral, the known inequality for M/Rz does not
   automatically become the stronger inequality for M/R(z/p). If
   z=p^r U m, the original quotient has height-one (p)-length r; the
   divided source U m has length zero there. The old bound cannot remove
   that allowance without another justified arithmetic comparison.

These are explanations of the guard, not seed Kato computations or new
BSD theorems. A genuinely different integral Euler-system source can be
used only after its own source comparison is justified. Multiplication
by an R-unit is harmless; multiplication by p^(−1) is not a unit change.
