# Sources and scope

The actual supplied UPPER_CONTROL.md, all three new Lean sources, and its eta
program were read. Prior RECEIVER_DESCENT.md and LOCAL_EXACTNESS.md were read
for the historical comparison. The prior BFR review is retained. This review
does not claim to have reread all 5,180 historical files; the included reading
ZIP was verified byte-identical to its previously published version.

Primary source statements checked in this review:

* Darmon, Diamond, Taylor, *Fermat’s Last Theorem*, Theorems 2.18 and 2.19,
  PDF pages 61-62 (one-based): finite-module Selmer/dual-Selmer size formula.
  https://www.math.mcgill.ca/darmon/pub/Articles/Expository/05.DDT/paper.pdf
* Milne, *Class Field Theory*, III section 1 (unramified unit cohomology and
  relative Brauer invariant), and IV Example 2.14(e), printed page 130 / PDF
  page 139: localization over all places.
  https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf
* Dainobu, arXiv:2304.05035, Proposition 2.8 and its elliptic specialization:
  finite coefficients require the p-unit Tamagawa hypothesis in the
  away-from-p finite/unramified comparison.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.05035

Serre's local representation theorem is retained from the supplied, already
reviewed local-exactness dossier; it was not independently reread in this
turn. Brown's general cohomology tools are standard imports; the particular
Sylow and no-lift computations were reconstructed explicitly. The literature
summaries of Prasad-Shekhar and Ray-Weston in the submission are not promoted
by this review: they are not used to prove the accepted comparison.
