# Re-derivation of the submitted finite-error example

This is a written algebra check, not a new seed result, formal verification, or another run of the 288-case program. Let a be a positive integer and R=Z_p[[U]]. (For a=0 the finite summand is zero.)

For

    D = [[p^a, U, 0],
         [  0, 0, U]],

the signed maximal minors are

    Delta=(U²,-p^a U,0)=U eta,
    eta=(U,-p^a,0).

Indeed D eta=0. Conversely, D(x,y,z)=0 forces z=0 and p^a x=-Uy. Coprimality of the irreducibles p and U gives x=Uv, y=-p^a v. Thus eta is an R-basis of the actual kernel. Its coordinates have unit gcd, but their ideal (U,p^a) is proper for a>0.

The column image is generated by (p^a,0), (U,0), and (0,U), so

    coker D = R/(p^a,U) direct_sum R/(U).

The first summand is finite and is absent from the height-one characteristic factor, which is U. At U=0, the cokernel is Z_p/p^a direct_sum Z_p, so its torsion has length a.

On the other hand, eta(0)=(0,-p^a,0) has content a, and

    [U]Delta=(0,-p^a,0)=eta(0).

Taking y=eta and z=U eta gives g=1, d=U, d1=1. The full height-one inequality is an equality in this abstract model, and the specialized torsion length equals the derivative content a. The finite error is therefore retained exactly in eta(0).

This does not realize a Selmer complex of the seed or assign a to a seed Sha group. In particular it supplies no arithmetic upper estimate for m_p-w_p.
