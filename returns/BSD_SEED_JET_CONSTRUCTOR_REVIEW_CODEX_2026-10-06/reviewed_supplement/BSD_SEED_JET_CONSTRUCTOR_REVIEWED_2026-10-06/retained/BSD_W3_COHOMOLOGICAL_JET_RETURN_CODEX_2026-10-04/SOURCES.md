All results here retain the hypotheses and grades stated in the individual proof files. Source hashes are in SOURCE_BINDINGS.json; retained project copies and hashes are in CONTEXT_BINDINGS.json. No source PDF is redistributed in this return.

## Primary texts read

* [Ochiai, A generalization of the Coleman map for Hida deformations](https://www.math.titech.ac.jp/top/~ochiai/coleman.pdf), Proposition 5.1, with Theorem 3.13 and (MW). Used for the integral quotient map and its pseudo-null error. The input's character matching is checked against this representation-level statement.
* [Kings–Loeffler–Zerbes, Rankin–Eisenstein classes and explicit reciprocity laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02888), Proposition 11.2.1, finite-free case. Used for perfect Galois complexes and derived base change. The residual/minimal-complex argument in this return supplies the actual local freeness.
* [Ochiai, On the two-variable Iwasawa main conjecture](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X06002223), Compositio 142 (2006), pp.1157–1200. Theorem 6.11 and Corollary 6.17 supply the integral optimized arithmetic Euler system; Theorem 2 records its one-sided bound with the transvection condition. The proof and source normalization, rather than an abstract alone, were read.
* [Ochiai, Iwasawa Main Conjecture for p-adic families of elliptic modular cuspforms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.06427), v3, March 5, 2019. Theorem 4.12 and its proof give the optimized family construction and integral gluing; Definition 4.4 specifies the coefficient representation and norms; Theorem 4.5 records the integral Euler-system bound and all its image hypotheses. The full main-conjecture equality is not used.
* [Kato, p-adic Hodge theory and values of zeta functions of modular forms](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_2004__295__117_0.pdf), Astérisque 295 (2004). The retained index argument cites 13.4(3), 14.2, 14.13–14.16. The printed p.242 reciprocity statement was visually checked to prevent silently changing the old fixed normalization. The present construction does not claim its new Euler system has unit ratio to that old class.
* [Nakamura, Zeta morphisms for rank two universal deformations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13647), v2. Proposition 3.15, equation (42), Theorem 3.16, Proposition 3.17 were read for the actual integral modular-cohomology construction and weight compatibility. This is a supporting source, not an invocation of the universal theorem with unverified local residual hypotheses.
