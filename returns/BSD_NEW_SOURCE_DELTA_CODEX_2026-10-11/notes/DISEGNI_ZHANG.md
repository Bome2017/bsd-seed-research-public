# Disegni–Zhang source check

New check of a previously title-only item, not a new release. Primary source: [arXiv:2410.08401v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08401v2), revised March 3, 2026. The historical register already mentions the general first-derivative scope; E17-145 lists the paper itself as title-only UNCHECKED.

Read: introductory Theorems A–D, Remarks 1.2.2–1.2.4, §§1.3.5–1.4.1, §7.3 integrality qualification. Printed pages 8,12,13 visually inspected. This is not a review of all 150 pages.

Theorem D gives an exact first-derivative height identity with the explicit local factor e_p and a canonical invariant functional. Its hypotheses include ordinarity, an unramified CM extension F/F0 (hence F0≠Q), splitting above p and 2, and local representation restrictions. Remark 1.2.3 retains Hypothesis 11.1.2 on tempered cohomology. Theorem A rationalizes a normalized central Rankin–Selberg value, not the seed’s second derivative. Section 7.3 permits clearing a p-power depending on p; it is not integral unit control across coefficient primes.

For this project, §§1.3.5–1.3.6 offer a precise normalization template for the CM comparison. Applying their theorem directly to the seed over Q fails its base-field hypothesis. No map from that tensor motive’s first derivative to the seed’s rank-two derivative, and no seed integral normalization bound, has been supplied. Keep this as a technique reference, below the new theta-class and nonordinary-source leads. The Oxford recording has not been identified with this paper’s precise theorem.
