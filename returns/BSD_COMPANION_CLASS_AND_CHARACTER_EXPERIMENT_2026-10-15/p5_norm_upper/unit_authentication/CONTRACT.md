# Exact compact-unit authentication

Authenticate only literal unitness of the thirteen free BNF candidates, independently of GRH/completeness. Use the saved number field maximal order and compact products. Factor each distinct factor ideal, reconstruct that ideal exactly, and sum its finite valuations with the full integer exponents. Require every resulting valuation vector to be zero. Do not expand huge unit powers; do not invoke BNF correctness or a known class number.

Bound: 60 seconds process wall time, GP alarm 58 seconds, observed RSS 1GiB, PARI stack maximum 1,000,000,000 bytes. No BNF or class certification. A timeout gives no authentication claim. Save exact factor valuation and exponent matrices for replay.
