# Certification terminology correction

The contract phrase "bnfisintnorm/certification" was an editing error. `bnfisintnorm` tests norm equations and does not certify a class group. The intended test is PARI `bnfcertify`, with its exact hypotheses and result recorded. An uncertified `bnfinit` remains conditional. This correction was made before any class-group result was accepted; the original sealed contract is retained.
