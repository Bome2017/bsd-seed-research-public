# Exact joint-evaluation model, sealed before enumeration

This is a theoretical count, not a new seed computation or a statistical acceptance rule.

For independent Kummer classes consisting of r point directions and s known admitted excess directions, under full GL2(F3), the conditional Frobenius distribution at completely split good auxiliary primes should be uniform on 2-by-(r+s) matrices over F3. The local signature quotient rank is rank(full matrix) minus rank(point columns).

Predicted counts by quotient rank 0,1,2:
- r=0,s=2: (1,32,48) of81.
- r=1,s=2: (73,608,48) of729.
- r=2,s=2: (4177,2336,48) of6561.
- r=2,s=0: (81,0,0) of81.

A proof of joint evaluation surjectivity and rank invariance under conjugation is required separately. The exact asymptotic densities do not make a finite prime sample complete, do not supply error probabilities for deterministic primes, and do not bound the number of unlisted Selmer classes.
