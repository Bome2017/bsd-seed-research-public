# Fixed-ancestor test and the scope of the construction

No fixed ancestor has been constructed. The normalized residual object
required by the brief has not yet been proved to arise from M_p, so this
return does not claim to have reached the admissible ancestor-search stage.
The observations below correct the logical test and examine whether the
newly constructed objects themselves already provide it.

## A necessary correction to the brief

For a nonzero element a of a fixed finite free Z[1/D]-module M, its image
in M/pM is nonzero for all but finitely many p. In a basis, at least one
coordinate is a nonzero rational number with fixed denominator support;
only primes dividing its numerator or D can kill a. This is the valid
finite-support argument.

The brief also permits a fixed finitely generated Z-module element whose
reductions **map to** the desired residual object. Nonzero alone is not
sufficient in that form:

* A torsion element is zero in M/pM for all but finitely many p.
* A nonzero reduced element can lie in the kernel of every proposed
  realization. For example, M=Z^2, a=(1,0), and maps
  (x,y) mod p -> y have zero image at every prime.

Thus this version needs an element nonzero in the torsion-free quotient
and an independently proved non-killing statement for the realization
maps. A suitable sufficient form is a fixed map of finite free integral
modules whose characteristic-zero image of a is nonzero, with the mod-p
maps its reductions outside a fixed denominator set. Another is proved
injectivity on the reduced line generated by a. Without that extra arrow,
the proposed ancestor does not prove cofinite unitness.

This is a correction to the success criterion, not a nonexistence theorem
for ancestors.

## What the cofinite Hida construction actually provides

The fixed integer D in COFINITE_HIDA_CONTROL.md separates the seed's
tame weight-two eigensystem from the other eigensystems. It proves that
each admitted ordinary family is a power-series ring and that its symbol
specialization is primitive. It does not construct a common critical
symbol for the different weight-three forms g_p.

In particular the equal-looking rings Z_p[[T]] for different p are not
themselves a common integral Hecke module, nor does T=p identify their
coefficients. The finite tame Hecke spectrum defines D, but the moving
weight-three/p-nebentypus critical functional has not been realized as a
single map on that spectrum's integral module.

The fixed seed modular-symbol class is a possible source one could try
to realize. Merely reducing it gives a weight-two class. In this return
there is no proved map carrying it to the **second divided** weight-three
critical specialization with a non-killing property. The period comparison
and the order-two specialization are precisely the missing arrows; they
are not supplied by freeness of the family symbol module.

The elementary power-series control is decisive about what was not proved.
Even over Z_p[[T]], F(T)=T^4 is nonzero, primitive as a power series, and
has F(p) divisible by p^4. Rank-one family control alone cannot establish
the needed valuation bound. This is an algebraic control, not a proposed
Hida L-function for the seed.

## No blanket p-dependence obstruction

The changing character omega^(-1) does not prove that a fixed ancestor
cannot exist. Many p-dependent realizations arise from fixed integral
objects. This return therefore does not issue
`W3_NO_FIXED_ANCESTOR_FOR_THIS_CONSTRUCTION` simply from character or
level variation. Nor is the absence of a constructed ancestor a proof
that the uniform BSD step is impossible.

No claim of W3_FIXED_ANCESTOR, W3_COFINITE_BOUND, cofinite primary
vanishing, FIN, ORDER9 or Q36 is made.
