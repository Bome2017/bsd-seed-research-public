The [relative-class reduction](returns/BSD_GENUINE_RELATIVE_CLASS_METHOD_2026-10-20/REPORT.md) gives zero new closed rank after its residual ideals are accounted for. Those are bounded outcomes for specified families, not impossibility theorems for class methods.

The research target is a sufficiently small bound on the **relevant primary character contribution**, together with complete unit/local accounting and control across coefficient primes. The existing whole-relative-class estimate is genuine but too coarse. Further results must control this contribution rather than rename the missing quotient, discard unknown generators or infer completeness from finite relation searches.
