# Correction precedence and scope

This edition preserves failed attempts because they locate tested first failed steps. Inclusion means evidence is disclosed; it does not mean every assertion in an earlier file remains accepted.

1. **Corrected Lane A governs.** Use [the correction review](returns/BSD_ARISTOTLE_EXCESS_A_CORRECTION_REVIEW_CODEX_2026-10-07/REPORT.md). The proposed identification between the DLRV ideal and classical Heegner classes was withdrawn. The retained -23 twist certificate was already in the record; lack of full replay files is not a proof it was false. The direct nonexceptional specialization is excluded for the seed; broader higher-Fitting methods are not universally disproved.
2. **Receiver equality supplies a target, not a bound.** The [global exactness review](returns/BSD_RECEIVER_GLOBAL_EXACTNESS_REVIEW_CODEX_2026-10-06/REPORT.md) supersedes older scope limitations as specified there. No equality with Sha proves Sha vanishes.
3. **Finite-order lifting preserves excess.** The [order-three audit](returns/BSD_ORDER3_EXCESS_AUDIT_CODEX_2026-10-06/REPORT.md) and [freeze](returns/BSD_DEFORMATION_FREEZE_CODEX_2026-10-06/REPORT.md) retain the short excess summands. Further point lifting alone is not an upper bound.
4. **Kato normalization is source-specific.** Integral source comparisons do not authorize division of the actual class by unknown p-content. Keep the stated lattice, smoothing, local factors and cohomology complex together.
5. **Valuation law is not an independent vanishing predictor.** The [mechanism review](returns/BSD_VALUATION_MECHANISM_REVIEW_2026-10-13/REPORT.md) explains the observed quotient using arithmetic theorems. A seed valuation inferred from an already certified Sha component cannot be fed back as an independent proof of that component.
6. **Tame-law observations are not an all-prime identity.** Use the [status correction](returns/BSD_TAME_IDENTITY_STATUS_CORRECTION_CODEX_2026-10-07/REPORT.md). Matching finite residues does not establish a fixed global scalar or Q36.
7. **Relative and detector calculations retain their kernels.** The [direct-base review](returns/BSD_DIRECT_BASE_CONTROL_REVIEW_CODEX_2026-10-12/REPORT.md) and [local-detector review](returns/BSD_LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION_DETECTOR_REVIEW_2026-10-13/REPORT.md) distinguish detection of supplied classes from an upper bound on undiscovered ones.
8. **Class relation accounting must be complete.** The [latest relative method](returns/BSD_GENUINE_RELATIVE_CLASS_METHOD_2026-10-20/REPORT.md) keeps every private ideal column and unit kernel. Its zero net rank is not repaired by relabeling or combining the same rows. The [analytic bound](returns/BSD_CLOSED_CLASS_CONSTRUCTION_2026-10-20/REPORT.md) includes unknown classes but is too coarse.

A newer timestamp alone does not override a mathematical statement. The cited correction must identify the statement and its changed scope. Reports labelled written/conditional remain so; exact finite algebra checks are not seed arithmetic computations. No corpus-wide novelty assessment or human certification accompanies this export.
