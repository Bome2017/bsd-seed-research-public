## 2. The bridge's own steps, re-derived

**§2: no finite submodule.** Assume `μ = 0`, let `H = X_tors(Z_p)`, and `Y = X/H`, which is `Z_p`-free of rank `2d`.
Then `char Y = char X`, so `v_p det(T − a | Y) = v_p(F(a)) + v_p(F(ι a))`. Theorem 7.1 (equality) and §1 give
`length X/(T−a)X = 2v_p(F(ιa)) = 2v_p(F(a))`. The sequence `0 → H/(T−a) → X/(T−a) → Y/(T−a) → 0` is exact because
`det ≠ 0`. Hence `H/(T−a)H = 0`, and `H = 0` by Nakayama. **Correct.** One value of `a` suffices.

**§3: balanced τ-ranks.** Take any `τ`-equivariant free resolution `F_1 → F_0` of `W = X/pX` over `F_p[[u]]`, with
`τu = −uτ`. Its Euler characteristic `[Tor_0] − [Tor_1]` is intrinsic, because equivariant resolutions can be compared
equivariantly (2 is invertible). The canonical resolution gives `(w^+ − w^-)([1] − [sgn])`. Directly: the sequence
`0 → W[U]⊗sgn → W⊗sgn → W → W/UW → 0` is exact and equivariant. The arithmetic resolution gives
`Tor_1 ≅ Sel_p(E/K)`, `Tor_0 ≅ Sel_p(E/K)^∨`. Their `±` dimensions agree because two identifications are
`τ`-equivariant:
* the Weil pairing `E[p] × E[p] → μ_p`, which is `G_Q`-equivariant;
* `Σ_v inv_v`, which is invariant under the field isomorphisms `K_v → K_{τv}`.

So `w^+ = w^-`. **Correct.** BLV never states `τ`-equivariance of the Λ-adic modules. I checked it condition by
condition:
* **at `p`:** Rubin's `E(m_∞)^±` is cut out by `log_χ = 0` for `χ ∈ Ξ^∓_p`. Conjugation by `τ` sends `χ` to `χ^{-1}`
  up to Galois action on values, which preserves `n_χ`. For `p` split, the definitions at `𝔭` and `𝔭̄` are symmetric.
* **at `N^-`:** the Tate filtration is `G_{Q_ℓ}`-stable.
* **at `N^+`:** `H^1 = 0`.
* **at `S_aux`:** the condition is relaxed on the compact side and strict on the discrete side.
* **on the Λ-adic module:** `𝐓 = T ⊗ Λ(ε_∞^{-1})^ι` carries the semilinear operator `t⊗λ ↦ τt⊗ι(λ)`, compatible with
  `Ad(τ)`.

The Λ-adic presentation is the inverse limit of the finite-level Poitou–Tate sequences. Those groups are finite, so the
limit is exact (Mittag-Leffler).

**§4: rank two forces `Sha = 0`.** `uX = TX`, and `(X/uX)^+ = X^+/uX^-`. By control and `p` odd, `(X/TX)^+` is
`Sel_{p^∞}(E/Q)^∨`. If `d = 2`, the surjection `X^+ ≅ Z_p^2 ↠ Z_p^2` is an isomorphism, which kills both `uX^-` and
`Sha(E/Q)[p^∞]^∨`. **Correct.** The step uses only `rank E(Q) = 2`.

**`M_1 = 0`.** When `μ = 0`, `rank X^+ = d` and `X^+ ↠ Z_p^2`, so `d ≥ 2`, which forces `b̄_1 = 0`. When `μ > 0`,
`b̄_1 = 0` trivially. **Correct.** This is the Selmer-side form of the functional-equation parity. Consistency check:
for `(r^+, r^-) = (0,0)`, `d = 1` would force a cyclic, nonzero `Sha`, which Cassels–Tate forbids. That matches `λ` being
even when the sign is `+1`.
