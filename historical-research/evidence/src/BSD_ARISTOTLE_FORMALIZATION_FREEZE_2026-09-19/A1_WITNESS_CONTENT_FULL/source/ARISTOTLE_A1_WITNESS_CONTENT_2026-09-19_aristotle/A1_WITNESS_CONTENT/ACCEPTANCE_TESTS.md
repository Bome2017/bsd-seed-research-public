## 2. Procedure (`bash scripts/acceptance.sh`)
1. Frozen-file check (above).
2. Clean build of this package (`rm -rf .lake/build && lake build`). It must end with `Build completed successfully`.
3. Informational scan for `sorry` / `admit` / `axiom` tokens in non-frozen Lean files. Comments may mention them.
4. **Authoritative:** `lake env lean WitnessContent/Acceptance.lean` prints `#print axioms` for all 20 targets. A target is **CLEAN**
   iff its axioms are a subset of {`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`}. `sorryAx` or any other axiom means NOT clean.
5. `scripts/level.py` computes the level:
   - **A1_FULL** — all 11 CORE and 6 FULL targets are CLEAN;
   - **A1_CORE** — all 11 CORE targets are CLEAN;
   - otherwise **BELOW_A1_CORE**, and `FIRST_NOT_CLEAN_TARGET` names the first failure in CORE-then-FULL order.
   The 3 STRETCH targets are reported separately and never affect the level.
