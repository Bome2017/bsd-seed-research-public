## A. Lift Ota leading terms to characteristic zero

The exact finite-stage obstruction is proved in Algebraic Lemma A: the group exponent kills every positive augmentation quotient. Rationalizing theta_S at fixed S loses its leading term. Any finite selection of S sees finitely many primes. Taking a p-adic limit can retain a Q_p line, but does not produce a Q-line with uniform primitive comparison. This rules out the naive lift, not a derived or geometric construction.

We tried the weaker goal that only valuations should descend. The resulting WC theorem needs a nonzero element of J_E and no matching unit parts. This is meaningful before FIN. It is a smaller sufficient target than LINE, not a proof that it is easier.

## B. Does BKS give a common scalar after one or several finite primary groups?

No such arrow is in the source. Hypothesis 2.2 explicitly includes finite p-primary Sha. The determinant descent in Theorem 7.8 and its image lattice are over Z_p. Reading its statement and the definition of its augmentation map supplied no comparison between distinct primes. Lemma U remains existing theory under its hypotheses; it was not reproved as a research accomplishment.

Finiteness at 3 and 5 therefore does not identify a rational scalar shared by other primes, nor define the required MW realization at those primes. A fixed rational rank-two exterior line is already available from P1,P2; the missing object is a suitably normalized zeta element in it, with uniform arithmetic control.

## C. Integral zeta elements, Fitting ideals, and determinant modules

An ideal over Z could provide enough finite-support information without any common scalar's unit parts. Thus “an element is always necessary” overstates the algebra. What is missing is construction of a **nonzero** ideal with the required arithmetic comparison independently of FIN.

The complex over product Z_p in WC §4 is a counterexample to obtaining this solely from perfectness, local rational acyclicity, and finite local defects. The distinction between a perfect Z-complex and a perfect profinite complex is load-bearing. Giving the Sha defect a globally finitely generated torsion Z-model would already prove the desired finiteness; merely naming that model is circular.

## D. H9 and the older point tower

The compatible Hom/Jacobian-valued point tower is real, but its audited specialization does not hit the seed's strict line. H9R23 further excludes ordinary characteristic-zero coefficient correspondences on the moving constituent, even after fixed denominator clearing. FIN does not alter the representation-theoretic Hom-vanishing.

The later coefficient gate excludes a nonzero strict vector with algebraic-over-Q coefficient ratio. Lemma C shows why this does not exclude a rational **exterior** source: contraction by log_p naturally produces the transcendental ratio. This is useful type-level clarification, with no constructed arithmetic comparison or bounded-content result.

## E–F. Can LINE be weakened to finite support?

Yes, rigorously: WC requires one finite-depth witness for each good prime p>=7, primitive at all but finitely many primes, with their depths bounded by the valuations of one integer A. It omits all cross-prime unit coherence. The proof of the implication is now complete; its arithmetic antecedent is not proved.

The decisive attempted implication remains unproved:

    rational modular-symbol source + norm relations
       -> J_E contains a nonzero integer.

The known uniform denominator bound does not prove this; the integral maps n->p^2 n refute that abstract inference. The known BKS diagrams are local and cannot be used as a cross-prime descent theorem. The H9 tower has neither the required realization nor uniform primitive comparison.

## Weakest sufficient statement, with scope stated

There is no canonical “weakest theorem” among all possible proof languages. Within the currently usable Ota architecture, WC is the weakest explicit sufficient condition isolated here: **nonzero J_E**, plus finiteness at its finite excluded set. For tail vanishing alone, it suffices that h_p=0 outside an explicitly finite set; finite-depth witnesses at the remaining good primes give their finiteness.

For the seed, the excluded 2-,3-,5-parts are retained as solved; 2243,4547,5843 remain open under this route. They remain subnodes of FIN. A genuinely global theorem may absorb them.
