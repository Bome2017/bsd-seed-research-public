# Could one object close FIN and FORMULA?

Possibly. This session does not establish a viable construction or classify the frontier as one executable comparison short of BSD.

## Required interfaces

A useful Z_E needs more than characteristic-zero existence:

1. A finitely generated integral lattice with a nonzero Z_E, constructed without assuming global FIN.
2. Arithmetic realization maps whose integral content is controlled outside a finite explicit set. For an Ota realization, this means witness depth bounded by the content of Z_E; integral maps alone are insufficient.
3. Arithmetic treatment of the exceptional primes. For a tame Ota construction these include 2243,4547,5843; their disappearance must be a theorem of the global construction.
4. A real realization comparing Z_E with L''/(2 Omega Reg), strong enough to give bounded rationality or bounded algebraicity.

The rational line generated by (P1 wedge P2)^tensor2 is already present. Naming it, or naming its real vector q times that generator, does not construct Z_E with these properties.

## A one-sided common certificate would suffice

Here is an exact reduction that avoids demanding every equality of a full conjectural framework.

**Proposition.** Assume the retained p=3 group of order 9. Suppose a theorem constructs a positive rational B and proves:

- q=4B;
- for every prime p, Sha[p^infinity] is finite and its order has p-adic valuation at most v_p(B).

Then the current certified interval proves full BSD for the seed.

**Proof.** The second condition implies v_p(B)>=0 for all p, hence B is a positive integer. The interval q in [35.99427,36.00573] gives 8.9985675<=B<=9.0014325, so B=9. The same condition makes Sha[p^infinity] zero away from primes dividing 9. Every remaining primary group is finite, so Sha is finite and its order divides 9. The known 3-primary group has order 9; thus #Sha=9 and q=4 #Sha. RANK is retained as closed. QED.

This proposition permits one-sided arithmetic bounds; exact p-adic unit equality at every prime is unnecessary. Its second bullet must be proved from the object, not assumed as a restatement of FIN. Its first bullet is an archimedean comparison. Neither bullet has been obtained from an existing seed object in this session.

If instead the common object gives only WC and q rational of small denominator, it proves the good-prime part of FIN and q=36. It still requires exceptional-primary finiteness and order control; the two conclusions cannot be silently composed into FORMULA.

| A hypothetical global perfect Z-complex for the Selmer defect | Would force finite support if rationally acyclic | None automatically | Construction/arithmetic identification is the missing theorem |
| Rational exterior MW line | Exact and integral already | Known real regulator | Missing distinguished zeta element and its arithmetic/analytic comparison |

The strict-line algebraicity obstruction is not a reason to reject the exterior line: Algebraic Lemma C gives its correct p-adic contraction. But the extracted scalar is still c_p, and neither its content nor its archimedean meaning is known.

## Decision

Keep the common-object possibility open. Prefer an attempt supplying both interfaces if an actual construction appears. Do not replace the two open branches by “one theorem remains” merely by conjoining their targets in a definition. The current evidence does not establish that such a combined theorem is easier than separate FIN and rationality proofs.
