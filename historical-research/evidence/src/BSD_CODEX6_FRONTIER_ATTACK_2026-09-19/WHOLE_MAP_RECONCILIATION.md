## Material corrections, with severity and scope

**REPAIRABLE — numerical state.** “Reg is only PARI/non-Arb” missed Lane 3's actual interval algorithm. It did not invalidate the rank or primary-Sha conclusions. The new certificate supplies an independently checkable directed computation and a proved tail bound. In B.6, the printed bound z in [0.3514,7.0587] loses an exponent: z(1/2)=705865053470641, about 7.06e14. Its reported M=34.19 is consistent with that larger value. We correct the successor account without altering the source.

**REPAIRABLE — order shorthand.** FIN alone does not imply L_alg=36. Use L_alg=4 #Sha under FIN, and specialize to 36 only with #Sha=9. The primary data and Cassels–Tate square condition permit #Sha=9t^2. The master DAG itself already included the order hypothesis; the correction is to abbreviated R2 wording.

**REPAIRABLE — ideal versus element.** An ideal over the profinite integers always exists for arbitrary local data, possibly with no nonzero integer in it. Local perfectness does not imply finite support. Conversely, a nonzero global ideal with a valid one-sided arithmetic comparison can suffice for FIN; identifying all scalar unit parts is unnecessary. The real comparison still has to be proved to get FORMULA.

**REPAIRABLE — finite quotient scope.** Positive augmentation quotients of finite group rings vanish on rationalization. This does not prove that completed, derived, or geometric objects have no rational realization. Inverse limits can produce Z_p/Q_p lines. The exact finite-stage lemma replaces the broader phrase “no rational line.”

**REPAIRABLE — finite tests.** Finite computations cannot prove an unbounded global LINE assertion. They can refute a specified scalar or an internal compatibility. Finitely many compatible residue requirements alone need not refute the existence of an unspecified rational scalar: the Chinese remainder theorem supplies integer approximants. A failed sufficient detector is also not a negative Sha theorem.

**UNVERIFIED, carried — source seams.** p=3 R1/R2, the exact Nekovar/Benois versus MTT unit dictionary, and historical publication/import qualifications remain labelled. No absence of a located source is promoted to a contradiction. Whole-archive independent rebuild is outside this bounded audit.

Lemma U is known under its hypotheses and should receive no new proof budget. Ota supplies the local detector implications. The new exact reconstruction implication is elementary and its numerical premise is closed.
