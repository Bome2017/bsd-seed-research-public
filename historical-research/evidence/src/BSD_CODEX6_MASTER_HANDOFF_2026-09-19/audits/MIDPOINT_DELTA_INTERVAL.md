## 2. The two existing integral forms

Put \(c=2\pi/\sqrt N\), \(t=\sqrt N y\),
\(r(t)=R_{\rm mid}(it/\sqrt N)\), and
\[
\phi(t)=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{a_n}{n}e^{-cnt}.
\]
The established zeroth half-ray moment is zero: \(\phi(1)=0\).
This uses \(L(f,1)=0\) and the already checked Fricke sign, not a vanishing
first half-ray logarithmic moment. Consequently
\[
\boxed{\Delta_{\rm mid}
=c\int_1^\infty f(it/\sqrt N)[r(t)-r(1)]\,dt
=\int_1^\infty\phi(t)r'(t)\,dt.}
\tag{1}
\]
The second equality follows from \(\phi'(t)=-cf(it/\sqrt N)\),
with zero endpoints. It is the existing Eichler-primitive integration-by-parts
representation. Subtracting \(r(1)\) is essential for a useful coefficient-tail
bound and changes neither the period nor its normalization.

Both integrals are computed after \(t=e^u\), \(0\le u\le24\). They use
different integrands: \(ce^u f[ r-r(1)]\) and \(\phi\,\partial_u r\).
They share the coefficient certificate and residual evaluator. Thus the
cross-check is between mathematical representations, not independent software.

\[

\[

\left[\beta_j k_n+\epsilon_j+
\beta_j e^{4\pi n2^jv}E_1(4\pi n2^jv)\right],
\quad

\frac{2\sum_{a\mid n}a\log a}{\sigma_1(n)}.

Then, with \(p=4547\), \(M=N/p^2\),
\[

\tag{2}

Here the last identity is only an evaluation acceleration of the existing
residual. Its proof is included to justify the error bounds. Write
\(a=\varphi'(0)\), \(b=\varphi''(0)\) for the scattering derivatives.
The level-one inversion gives
\[

\]
\[
F''(v,0)-F''(1/v,0)=b(v^{-1}-v)+2a\log v(v^{-1}+v).
\]
Substitute into \(H(v)=\sum_j[\beta_jF''(2^jv,0)/8-
\epsilon_jF'(2^jv,0)/4]\). All elementary terms vanish because
\[
\sum\beta_j2^{\pm j}=0,\quad
\sum2^{-j}(\beta_jj\log2-\epsilon_j)=0,
\quad\sum2^j(\beta_jj\log2+\epsilon_j)=0,
\quad\sum\epsilon_j=\sum j\epsilon_j=0.

The reflected indices are \(j\mapsto3-j\), proving (2).

## 4. Exact compression of the finite coefficient sum

The stream is grouped into 37130 consecutive bins \([l,l+b-1]\), with
\(b=\min(4096,1+\lfloor l/64\rfloor)\), except the last truncated bin.
Set \(n_0=l+(b-1)/2\), \(h=(b-1)/2\), \(\eta=1/128\).
Then \(h/l\le\eta\). Store the eight **exact integer** moments
\[
M_k=\sum_{n=l}^{l+b-1}a_n(2n-2n_0)^k,\quad0\le k\le7.
\]
Their signed 128-bit computation is safe: using \(|a_n|\le2n\),
the sum of absolute values is at most
\(2T\cdot4096\cdot4095^7<2^{127}\).
The binary moments and their SHA-256 are retained.

For \(z=cn_0t\), the finite approximation is
\[
F(t)=\sum_{\rm bins}e^{-z}\sum_{k=0}^7 C_kz^k,
\quad C_k=\frac{(-1)^kM_k}{(2n_0)^k k!}.
\tag{7}
\]
Its exact primitive is
\[
\Phi_F(t)=\sum_{\rm bins}e^{-z}\sum_{j=0}^7 Q_jz^j,
\quad Q_7=C_7/n_0,\quad Q_j=C_j/n_0+(j+1)Q_{j+1}.
\tag{8}
\]
In particular \(\Phi_F'=-cF\); this is not a separately fitted primitive.

Taylor's theorem bounds a bin's error by
\[
S_l e^{-clt}(cht)^8/8!,\qquad S_l=\sum_{n\text{ in bin}}|a_n|.
\]
Let \(A_T=2\sqrt T(1+\log T)\), so
\(\sum_{n\le T}|a_n|/n\le A_T\), by partial summation of
\(\sum_{n\le x}d(n)\le x(1+\log x)\).
Integrating the Taylor remainder gives the direct compression error
\[

=4B_R\eta^8(65/64)A_T.
\tag{9}
\]
The factor 4 includes both the approximation and possible omission of
compressed bins at a panel-specific cutoff.
For the primitive cross-check, use \(\log t\le t\) and
\(\sum d(n)n^{-3/2}=\zeta(3/2)^2\le9\). This gives
\[
\varepsilon_{{\rm comp},1}
=\frac{81B_1}{c}\eta^8(65/64)^2.
\tag{10}
\]

Residual-series errors in the two integrals are, respectively,
\[

## 5. Infinite coefficient tails and finite-panel cutoffs

\(x\log x-x\le D(x)\le x(1+\log x)\), followed by partial summation,

\[

\ge\sum_{n>T}\frac{|a_n|}{n}e^{-kn}.
\tag{12}
\]
The same calculation applied to \(\sqrt x e^{-kx}\), decreasing when
\(kT>1/2\), gives
\[
G_T(k)=e^{-kT}\left[
2T^{3/2}+\frac{\sqrt T(\log T+2)}k
+\frac{\log T+4}{2\sqrt T k^2}+\frac1{T^{3/2}k^3}\right]
\ge\sum_{n>T}|a_n|e^{-kn}.
\tag{13}
\]
For (13), bound \(\log(T+s)\le\log T+s/T\) and
\(\sqrt{T+s}\le\sqrt T+s/(2\sqrt T)\) in the resulting integral.
All its uses here have \(kT\ge cT>11\).
