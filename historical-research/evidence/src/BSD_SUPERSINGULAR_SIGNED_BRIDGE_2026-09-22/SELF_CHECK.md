# Checks on the signed minimal-lambda derivation

This is an internal proof check, not an independent audit or a formal replay.

1. **Source version.** BLV arXiv:2306.17784v2, 2026-02-05, was opened directly. Its inert minus case has both exact local control and main-conjecture equality. The inert plus exceptional case is never used.
2. **Character scope.** Theorem 7.1 starts with an arbitrary Z_p-algebra morphism Lambda -> O_chi, not only a finite-order character. Thus the specialization gamma -> 1+a, a in pZ_p, in the finite-error argument is allowed. Section 9's finite-order restriction is not imported into section 7.
3. **Finite to infinite coefficients.** Exact control identifies the finite-coefficient groups with the p^k-torsion of the full specialized group. The length in Theorem 7.1 is constant once k exceeds the nonzero specialization valuation, so the union stabilizes. This justifies applying an exact length formula to the full quotient of X.
4. **Finite Lambda errors.** The proof does not infer their absence from the characteristic ideal. It compares exact specialization lengths with the determinant for X modulo its finite Z_p-torsion, then uses Nakayama.
5. **Equivariant presentation.** Proposition 5.13 supplies freeness modulo p, not an unstated integral perfect complex. Finite-level Poitou--Tate followed by inverse limits supplies its cokernel. All finite-level groups are finite, so inverse-limit exactness uses the Mittag--Leffler property. At the base layer Proposition 5.10 plus the equal dimensions in Proposition 5.13 proves the required relaxed-group comparison even though Corollary 5.14 is printed only for n>=1.
6. **Complex conjugation, including the sign.** At augmentation the compact and discrete p-Selmer groups are identified by the Weil pairing (BLV Proposition 5.15); the invariant local Tate pairing is natural under conjugation. Their duals have the same +/- dimensions. In contrast, the canonical resolution u-U of X/p has the *sign-twisted* source. Keeping this sign is exactly what proves balance. A resolution without this twist is not equivariant and would invalidate the argument.
7. **Dual conventions.** Poitou--Tate may place the iota-twist of the scalar Pontryagin dual in the cokernel. The proof defines X accordingly. The full characteristic ideal F*F^iota is invariant, augmentation is unchanged, and iota does not change the tau eigenspaces. Inverse-character valuations are compared explicitly through Remark 5.16.
8. **Arithmetic group isolated.** The last surjection is onto the dual of E(Q) tensor Q_p/Z_p in the plus eigenspace. It is not an assertion that the entire K-Selmer group has no excess. The explicit module with u e_i=p^c f_i, u f_i=0 retains a nontrivial minus finite quotient.
9. **No finiteness assumed for seed Sha.** A surjection from a free Z_p-module of rank two to Z_p^2 is an isomorphism. Its entire kernel vanishes, including any finite or free part of the seed's Sha dual.
10. **No arithmetic witness smuggled in.** No psi_f(Gross point) value was calculated. The hypotheses ord_T(Fbar)=2 / M_2!=0 remain open at every supersingular prime. The proof does not claim a positive-density or uniform nonvanishing result.

The most consequential independent-review targets are items 3 and 5--7. All are expanded in SIGNED_MINIMAL_LAMBDA_BRIDGE.md rather than hidden behind a claim of standard control.
