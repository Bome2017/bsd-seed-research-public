# Alternative (c): structural obstruction and its scope

## The proposed universal endpoint

**CONJECTURE — not proved.** Every identity of alternative (a), in an appropriately specified class, has scalar n_Y=32*#Sha(E/Q).

**DERIVED — initial definitional issue.** While total Sha finiteness is unproved, #Sha is not a defined finite rational coefficient. An unconditional scalar identity involving that cardinality must independently establish the finiteness needed to interpret it. Defining a restricted category of constructions is also necessary for a universal factorization claim; the phrase “computed from modular symbols, tame data and E(Q) alone” does not itself supply such a category or a theorem about it.

## Candidate C-1: conditional refined-formula uniqueness as an unconditional obstruction

**DERIVED — conditional uniqueness only.** Suppose independently that total Sha is finite, that the refined residue law w_p=32*#Sha mod p holds at infinitely many primes in the scalar identity's domain, and that a rational n satisfies that identity there. Then n=32*#Sha. Indeed, after clearing finitely many denominator primes, the numerator of n-32*#Sha is divisible by infinitely many primes and hence is zero.

**DERIVED — first failing test for using this as an unconditional endpoint.** K-F. Its cardinality and residue law are additional unproved inputs, not consequences of B.20–B.22 or of Attempt (i). No printed conjecture is promoted to a theorem. The conditional uniqueness proof is elementary and is not claimed as a new obstruction result.

## Candidate C-2: free-part regulator cannot hide an integral torsion determinant

**DERIVED — scoped statement.** In a finitely presented Z_p-module with free rank two, additional cyclic p-torsion factors contribute nonunit factors to the higher Fitting ideals. For example X=Z_p^2 direct_sum (Z/p)^2 has Fitt_2=(p^2). A procedure that retains only the free quotient cannot infer that this ideal is the unit ideal. This is the same precise paired-state obstruction used in the direct attempts.

**DERIVED — limitation.** This does not show that every arithmetic identity factors through that particular presentation or determinant line. Nor do the abstract module models prove a nonexistence theorem for the actual modular symbols of the seed. A universal factorization/comparison theorem is missing.

**DERIVED — first failing test for the claimed vanishing endpoint from the free quotient.** K-S. It cannot distinguish the two paired states. As a limited obstruction to that inference it is valid; as an obstruction to every construction in alternative (a) it is not proved.

## What the current comparison does and does not force

**DERIVED.** Attempt (i) identifies minimum character depth, capped at p-1, with Selmer generator number. It therefore detects excess Selmer at a single p exactly in the shallow range relevant to v2. It does not identify any singleton scalar with the order of total Sha, and does not produce a common scalar in the first place.

**DERIVED — finite-support distinction.** A nonzero rational scalar with the stipulated residue law gives primary vanishing only outside its finite comparison, hypothesis, numerator, and denominator exception sets. It proves no finiteness at the excluded primes. Conversely, finiteness of total Sha alone supplies no residue law for the scalar. Neither direction is strengthened here.

## Outcome

**DERIVED.** No universal obstruction of v2 alternative (c) is proved. The valid obstructions are restricted to filtered projection/unit-norm shortening, inference from rational-point local separation, independent twist idempotents, and completed compatibility alone. Their precise scopes are recorded in the corresponding attempt files. They do not rule out an independent uniform arithmetic bound or a new comparison theorem.
