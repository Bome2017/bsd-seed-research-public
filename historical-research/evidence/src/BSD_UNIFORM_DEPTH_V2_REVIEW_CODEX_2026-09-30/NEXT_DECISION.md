# What to do after the two Pro returns

## Decision

There is no construction ready for a new proof session in either return. That is a statement about the supplied work, not impossibility of the desired theorem. The uniform return adds a valid comparison; the Q36 return adds closure corollaries to an already-retained obstruction. Neither supplies an independent upper bound or an endpoint arithmetic identity.

## Concrete work now

1. Finish the already-running v0.2.6 bank and check its actual final changes. Preserve this new comparison as a separately dated addendum so its scope and proof are not silently attributed to the earlier synthesis. The approved release work and its push approval remain separate.
2. Correct the Q36 collision record using the preceding review: M2 was already retained, and was omitted from the brief. Do not relaunch the same literal Mellin construction under different terminology.
3. Keep prime production frozen. More settled primes or a paid bad-prime computation cannot supply the missing all-but-finitely-many-primes argument. They remain valid finite arithmetic tasks if that scope is later deliberately chosen.

These steps preserve and clarify existing work. They do not move the mathematical BSD endpoint, and should not be advertised as doing so.

## What would justify the next research attempt

A candidate needs an explicit new arithmetic input from which an upper bound follows. Sufficient examples, with a stated prime domain, are:

- a normalized character-value estimate strictly below depth four, obtained without feeding in an already-short unit witness;
- a construction proving a two-prime Kurihara quantity is a unit;
- an independent bound on the full Selmer generator number;
- another independent argument giving eventual primary vanishing.

Being equivalent to the desired conclusion is not a reason to reject a proof. The issue is whether the proposed proof introduces a new valid argument. Depending on p is also allowed. Separate mechanisms may cover different prime sets whose union has a proved finite complement. A fixed global object, rationality of q, and exact 288 are not mandatory prerequisites.

Before committing a full session, identify the new formula, source theorem, construction or inequality, and the earlier failed step it changes. For an intermediate lemma, explain the new role it will play; it need not prove FIN on its own. Abstract paired-state examples can reject deductions from selected structural inputs, but cannot rule out a theorem using arithmetic not represented in those examples.

## The norm inequality is not yet that construction

The new condition 0<|Norm(2048 chi(theta_K))|<p^4 is mathematically sufficient. It is not supplied by the report and is stronger than merely controlling the p-adic depth, since it also constrains every prime-to-p factor of the norm. No proposed estimate here achieves it. Assigning another worker only “prove this inequality” would hand over the missing theorem, not a worked-out approach. Do not treat its elementary formulation as evidence that an analytic norm campaign is now feasible.
