=== WHY ellmoddegree CANNOT ANSWER THIS, regardless of feasibility ===
PARI ellmoddegree returns  R = deg(phi) / c^2   (by design, see its own help).
Put k = val_7(c), m = val_7(deg phi), v = val_7(R) = m - 2k.
CNS Thm 1.2 gives k <= m = v + 2k, i.e.  0 <= v + k, i.e.  k >= -v.
That is a LOWER bound on k. An upper bound on k is exactly what we need, and R does not give one.
  v >= 0  =>  k >= -v is vacuous (k >= 0 anyway): NO information.
  v <  0  =>  k >= |v| > 0: tells us 7 | c. Bad news, still no upper bound.

Sage modular_degree WOULD give deg(phi) itself - but its docstring makes correctness
'subject to ... Manin's conjecture: the Manin constant is 1'. Circular. Also heuristic.
