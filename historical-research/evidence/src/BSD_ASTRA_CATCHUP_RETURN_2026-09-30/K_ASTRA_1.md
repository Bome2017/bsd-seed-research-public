# K-Astra-1: pre-registered failure test

A concrete way the argument could yield only #Sha[3^infinity] <=81 is to use a regulator normalization whose valuation differs by two, or to fail to match the characteristic-series leading coefficient to the gamma-normalized regulator in Jones's formula. An unaccounted integral index/cokernel of order 3 in the Coleman map or local-condition comparison could likewise weaken the valuation upper bound by one, giving only <=27 before any square-order refinement.

Test these before accepting route K: (i) Coleman cokernel zero integrally, (ii) primitive Selmer conditions and no missing 3-divisible Euler factor, (iii) the exact gamma-normalized Jones identity.
