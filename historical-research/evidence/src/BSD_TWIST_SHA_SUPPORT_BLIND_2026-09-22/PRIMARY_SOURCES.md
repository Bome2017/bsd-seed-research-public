# Load-bearing sources

Read at source. Do not rely on this packet's descriptions of any of them.

## For Stage I.A — the p-part of Sha at analytic rank zero
* **Kato**, Euler systems for modular forms: the divisibility bounding Sha at good ordinary non-Eisenstein p.
  Check its hypotheses against this curve; the Eisenstein exclusion matters.
* **Skinner–Urban**, Iwasawa main conjecture for GL(2): the other divisibility. Hypotheses again.
* **Kolyvagin** / Gross-Zagier-Kolyvagin at analytic rank 0: what it bounds, and whether the bound is effective.
* **Keller** and **Keller–Stoll** on the p-part of strong BSD at analytic rank 0/1, including **ordinary Eisenstein
  primes** and p-converse theorems; and Keller-Stoll's complete-verification method for computing Sha exactly.
  arXiv:2312.07307, arXiv:2107.00325, and the finite-support write-up.
* **Mazur**, rational isogenies of prime degree - for the reducibility/Eisenstein analysis.
* Visibility and congruence criteria (Cremona-Mazur and successors) for producing or excluding Sha elements.

## For Stage I.B — the eigenspace question
* **Bertolini-Darmon**, *Derived p-adic heights*: Thms 2.7, 2.18 (esp. (2) and (5)), 2.23, Cor 2.24, Rmk 2.21,
  Lemma 3.1. Note Lemma 3.1 sits in a chapter with a standing Sha-finiteness assumption; check whether its proof
  uses it.
* **Bertolini-Longo-Venerucci**, *The anticyclotomic main conjectures for elliptic curves*, arXiv:2306.17784,
  Math. Ann. 395 (2026): Hypothesis 1.1, Definition 1.3, Theorem A, and the definition of Char_p(f).
* **Bertolini-Darmon**, Annals 2005: Theorem 1's setup and Corollary 3.
