## 2. Exact full-2-Selmer consequence

For `[2]=hat(phi_q) o phi_q`, the standard isogeny-Selmer comparison is
exact at the displayed terms:

\[
0\longrightarrow
\frac{E'_q(\mathbf Q)[\widehat\phi_q]}
     {\phi_q(E_q(\mathbf Q)[2])}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Sel}^{(\phi_q)}(E_q/\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Sel}_2(E_q/\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Sel}^{(\widehat\phi_q)}(E'_q/\mathbf Q).
\]

Here `E_q(Q)[2]={O,(0,0)}` is the kernel of `phi_q`, while
`E'_q(Q)[hat(phi_q)]={O,(0,0)}`.  Therefore the first nonzero term has order
`2`.  Under `H_SEL(q)`, the middle isogeny-Selmer group has order `4` and the
last group has order `1`.  Exactness gives

\[
 \#\operatorname{Sel}_2(E_q/\mathbf Q)=4/2=2,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Sel}_2(E_q/\mathbf Q)\simeq\mathbf Z/2\mathbf Z.
\]

The Kummer exact sequence is

\[
0\longrightarrow E_q(\mathbf Q)/2E_q(\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow \operatorname{Sel}_2(E_q/\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow \Sha(E_q/\mathbf Q)[2]
\longrightarrow0.
\]

The torsion calculation below gives `E_q(Q)_tors = Z/2Z`.  Zhai's theorem
independently gives analytic and algebraic rank zero.  Hence

\[
 \#(E_q(\mathbf Q)/2E_q(\mathbf Q))=2,
 \qquad \Sha(E_q/\mathbf Q)[2]=0.
\]

Zhai also gives finiteness of `Sha(E_q/Q)`, so

\[
 \Sha(E_q/\mathbf Q)[2^\infty]=0.
\]

This deduction uses the orientation and the order-`2` kernel term above; it
would be wrong to multiply the two displayed isogeny-Selmer orders without
dividing by that term.

## 3. Analytic input and period normalization

The base curve is optimal, has Manin constant `1`, negative minimal
discriminant `-496`, rational 2-torsion, and

\[
 \left|\frac{L(E,1)}{\Omega(E)}\right|=\frac14.
\]

This absolute value is certified by the exact Hecke eigenspace and canonical
integral modular-symbol lattice replay in
`checker/exact_base_modular_symbol.gp`, with the Neron normalization proved
in `evidence/EXACT_BASE_L_VALUE.md`.  It is not reconstructed from decimal
period and special-value fields or from `msfromell`'s numerical scaling.

Because the discriminant is negative, the full Neron real period `Omega(E)`
is Zhai's least positive real period `Omega_E^+`.  Thus its base valuation is
`v_2(L(E,1)/Omega_E^+)=-2`.

For `q in F_62`, one has `q=5 mod 8`, hence `q=1 mod 4`, and

\[
 v_2(\#E(\mathbf F_q))=2=-v_2(L(E,1)/\Omega_E^+).
\]

Zhai, *Non-vanishing theorems for quadratic twists of elliptic curves*,
Asian J. Math. 20 (2016), Theorem 1.5, therefore gives

\[
 L(E_q,1)\ne0,
 \qquad
 v_2\!\left(\frac{L(E_q,1)}{\Omega^+_{E_q}}\right)=0,
\]

and finiteness of `E_q(Q)` and `Sha(E_q/Q)`.  A positive twist preserves the
negative discriminant sign, so `Omega(E_q)=Omega^+_{E_q}` and

\[
 v_2\!\left(\frac{L(E_q,1)}{\Omega(E_q)}\right)=0.
\]

The nonminimal short equation is used only for descent.  Its invariant
differential is one half of the Neron differential on the displayed minimal
`62a1` model; no period in this chain is computed from the short equation
without that scaling.

## 4. Local factors and torsion

For every `q in F_62`, the minimal local data of `E_q` are

| place | reduction | local factor |
|---|---|---:|
| `2` | nonsplit multiplicative `I_4` | `c_2=2` |
| `31` | split multiplicative `I_1` | `c_31=1` |
| `q` | additive `I_0^*` | `c_q=2` |

There are no other bad finite primes.  In particular,

\[
 N(E_q)=62q^2,
 \qquad
 \Delta_{\min}(E_q)=-2^4\cdot31\cdot q^6,
 \qquad
 \prod_p c_p(E_q)=4.
\]

The local reasons are uniform, not sampled:

- `q=5 mod 8` is the unramified nonsquare class in `Q_2`; it twists split
  `I_4` to nonsplit `I_4`.
- `(q/31)=-1` twists nonsplit `I_1` at `31` to split `I_1`.
- At the new odd prime, the normalized `I_0^*` component polynomial is
  `T(T^2-15T+64)`.  Its quadratic factor has discriminant `-31`, and
  `(-31/q)=(q/31)=-1`, so it has exactly one rational root and `c_q=2`.

Moreover,

\[
 E_q(\mathbf Q)_{\mathrm{tors}}\simeq\mathbf Z/2\mathbf Z.
\]

Indeed, the 2-division cubic has only the rational root `0`; a hypothetical
point of order `4` would have `x=+/-8q`, and substitution gives either
`y^2=64q^3` or a negative real value, so no such rational point exists.
There is no odd torsion because `62a1` has no rational `3`-, `5`-, or
`7`-isogeny and quadratic twisting does not change the existence of a
Galois-stable line in odd-prime torsion.

Consequently,

\[
 v_2\!\left(\prod_p c_p(E_q)\right)=2,
 \qquad
 v_2(\#E_q(\mathbf Q)_{\mathrm{tors}})=1.
\]
