## 2. Exact headline Selmer theorem

Frozen theorem (`P/TARGET.md:101` §4):

    Sel^(φ_q)(E_q/Q)  = {1, −31, q, −31q}                    (P/TARGET.md:106–107)
    Sel^(φ̂_q)(E'_q/Q) = {1}                                  (P/TARGET.md:113–114)

for **every** `q ∈ F₆₂`. Both are subsets of `Q*/Q*²`; the ambient group is `Q(S,2)` with
`S = {2,31,q}`, 16 classes, from `Δ(E_q) = −2¹⁶·31·q⁶` and `Δ(E'_q) = 2¹⁴·31²·q⁶`
(`E/MECHANISM_ABSTRACT.md:45–50`).

The four surviving φ-classes are exactly the global Kummer image of `E'_q(Q)[2] ≅ (Z/2)²`
(`P/PROOF.md:36–37`) — i.e. the φ-Selmer group is as small as the rational 2-torsion allows and
the dual Selmer group is trivial.

**Local images, both maps, at ∞, 2, 31, q** (`P/PROOF.md:26–32`):

| place | φ_q image | dual image |
|---|---|---|
| ∞ | both real classes | positive class |
| 2 | `{1,q}` | all unit classes |
| 31 | all four local classes | `{1}` |
| q | all four local classes | `{1}` |
| outside `{2,31,q}` | unramified subgroup | unramified subgroup |

Writing `d = (−1)^s 2^e 31^f q^g`, the φ intersection is `e = 0, s = f`; the dual conditions have
the unique solution `s = e = f = g = 0` (`P/PROOF.md:34–36`).

**The uniformity mechanism** — the point Banwait will want first: the local images depend on q
only through finitely many local square classes. At 2 via `q = 5s²` in `Q₂` (reducing every
`q ≡ 5 mod 8` to the single curve `E₅/Q₂`); at 31 via `(q/31)`; at q via
`(−31/q) = (q/31)` by reciprocity, `q ≡ 1 (4)`. The q-adic cases are instances of
Kane–Klagsbrun Lemma 3.1 (arXiv:1702.02687). Stated at `E/MECHANISM_ABSTRACT.md:276–279` and
`:216`; derived at `P/evidence/DESCENT_DERIVATION.md` §3.1–§3.4, §4.1–§4.4 (assumption ledger §6 at `:486`).
