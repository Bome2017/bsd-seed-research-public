# Part B — minimum unit length and the composite-conductor depth criterion

Every result asserted here is either **THEOREM (imported)** with its source and hypotheses or **DERIVED** with its proof. Source identifiers are in `SOURCE_AUDIT.md`. This review does not construct a new auxiliary conductor or a new prime certificate.

## B1. Match the quantities before comparing the indices

**DERIVED — conventions.** Castella–Sano §1.1, pp.1–3, and Kim §§1.2.2 and 1.4, pp.4 and 6, use the same auxiliary congruences

    ell = 1 mod p, ell not dividing N, a_ell = ell+1 mod p,

and the same p-primary ideal generated by ell-1 and a_ell-ell-1. For a product n, the ideal is the sum of the prime ideals; n=1 has ideal zero. Their real period is the total positive Neron period, and their quantities are the modular-symbol sums weighted by one discrete logarithm for each prime dividing n. Primitive-root changes multiply the individual quantity by a p-unit and do not alter a valuation or the first unit length. No comparison with the old omega_1-normalized singleton scalar is needed.

Let

    rho_p = min {nu(n): delta_n != 0 in its defining quotient},
    r_p   = min {nu(n): delta_n mod p != 0},
    X_p   = Sel_(p^infinity)(E/Q)^dual,
    d_p   = dim_Fp X_p/pX_p.

The first index is the first nonzero quantity at its available p-power precision. The second is the first unit index. These are distinct definitions, not interchangeable readings of a single invariant.

Part A gives M_infinity(delta)=0 and existence of a unit, so both minima are finite. The integer minimum zero is attained; it is not merely an unattained real infimum.

## B2. Derive the exact minimum-unit formula

**THEOREM (imported, S4, Kim Theorem 1.8, p.7).** For E/Q and p>=5, with surjective residual representation and p-unit Manin constant, assume the collection of Kurihara quantities is not identically zero. Then the Selmer corank is rho_p; the higher Fitting ideals at i>=rho_p with i congruent to rho_p modulo two are p^(M_i-M_infinity)Z_p; and the finite part of the Selmer group is a direct sum of pairs of cyclic p-groups, as described by the successive differences of these valuations. The further conclusions that explicitly identify Mordell–Weil rank and Sha's order in clauses (4)–(6) impose an additional Sha-finiteness assumption. Those clauses are not used.

**DERIVED — parity.** The root number +1 is retained. Castella–Sano §1.1.3, pp.2–3, gives vanishing of delta_n when nu(n) is odd. Thus rho_p is even. By Kim's paired finite structure,

    X_p ~= Z_p^(rho_p) direct_sum (direct_sum_(j=1)^s Z/p^(a_j))^2,
    a_j>0,

so d_p=rho_p+2s is also even. This is a statement about the classical Selmer group; it does not replace its corank by the Mordell–Weil rank.

**DERIVED — Fitting threshold.** If M is finitely presented over the local ring Z_p, then Fitt_i(M)=Z_p exactly when i is at least the minimal number of generators dim_Fp(M/pM). This follows either from a minimal presentation or the structure theorem over Z_p.

For an even i<rho_p the Fitting ideal is zero. For an even i>=rho_p, Part A and Kim give

    Fitt_i(X_p)=p^(M_i)Z_p.

At i=d_p this ideal is the whole ring, hence M_(d_p)=0 and some length-d_p quantity is a unit. Conversely, a unit at length i forces M_i=0. Its length is even, so the same formula forces Fitt_i(X_p)=Z_p and i>=d_p. Therefore

    r_p=d_p.

**DERIVED — finite-level interpretation.** The usual Kummer sequences imply

    0 -> E(Q)/pE(Q) -> Sel_p(E/Q) -> Sha(E/Q)[p] -> 0,

and

    0 -> E(Q) tensor (Q_p/Z_p)
      -> Sel_(p^infinity)(E/Q) -> Sha(E/Q)[p^infinity] -> 0.

The first term of the second sequence is p-divisible, so taking p-torsion gives the corresponding short exact sequence. Since E(Q)[p]=0, the two middle terms have the same F_p-dimension. Pontryagin duality gives

    d_p=dim_Fp Sel_p(E/Q).

Combining these calculations proves precisely the requested identity

    r_p=dim_Fp Sel_p(E/Q).

It is an identity, not an independent upper bound.

## B3. Why r_p=2 kills even a hypothetical divisible Sha part

**DERIVED.** Write A_p=Sha(E/Q)[p^infinity]^dual. Dual Kummer gives

    0 -> A_p -> X_p -> Z_p^2 -> 0.

The rank-two quotient is free and hence projective over Z_p, so the sequence splits as a sequence of Z_p-modules. The module A_p is finitely generated because it is a submodule of the finitely generated module X_p. Thus

    d_p=2+dim_Fp(A_p/pA_p).

Nakayama's lemma now gives

    r_p=2 <=> d_p=2 <=> A_p=0
          <=> Sha(E/Q)[p^infinity]=0.

This does not assume A_p is torsion. A hypothetical free part of A_p is killed by exactly the same argument. In particular, no assumption of finite primary Sha, or finite total Sha, has entered the deduction.

The base rank gives d_p>=2, and parity gives d_p even. Consequently r_p<4 is sufficient, whereas mere finiteness of r_p is not.

## B4. Audit the full BH element and its hypotheses

**THEOREM (imported, S5, Bullach–Honnor Hypothesis 1.2 and Theorem 1.4(b), p.2).** Let K/Q be abelian and p>3. Suppose the p-adic image contains SL_2(Z_p); either K contains no primitive p-th root of unity or E has potentially good reduction at p with no p-torsion in the indicated reduction; and, if E is additive at p, p is unramified in K. Then the inverted full Mazur–Tate element theta_K^(MT,#) belongs to Fitt_0 over Z_p[Gal(K/Q)] of the Pontryagin dual of the classical p-primary Selmer group over K. No finite-Sha hypothesis is included in this assertion.

**DERIVED — application.** For a good p>=5 and a real cyclic degree-p K/Q of conductor m prime to pN, Part A's group-theoretic consequence supplies large p-adic image. Real K contains no primitive p-th root of unity. The additive-reduction clause is vacuous, and p is also unramified by the conductor assumption. Thus the printed theorem applies. The reference to a finite-Sha integral Selmer object in BH's introductory discussion is not a hypothesis of its classical-Selmer conclusion.

**DERIVED — normalization.** Use exactly BH Definition 2.2, p.8:

    theta_K^MT = projection_(Q(mu_m)/K)
                 sum_(a in (Z/m)^*) ([a/m]_E^+ + [a/m]_E^-) sigma_a,

with Omega^+=integral_(E(R)) |omega_E|. Since K is real, the minus part cancels between a and -a after projection. Keep the full sum; do not replace it by a sum over representatives without its factor of two. For the seed, this total period is c_infinity*omega_1=2*omega_1. Such a factor of two is a p-unit for the primes at issue, so it does not alter lambda-valuation, but its existence should not be concealed in an exact-value comparison.

The conductor in this statement is the conductor of K. Using an element at an artificially enlarged conductor requires BH's norm relations, Proposition 2.4, p.9, with their Euler factors retained. A primitive faithful character value can be related to an L-value by BH Remark 2.3(c), but only with its Gauss sum and period normalization. Bare complex nonvanishing is not a depth bound.

## B5. Prove the strict character-depth test

**DERIVED.** Fix G=C_p, a generator gamma with chi(gamma)=zeta_p, and put

    R=Z_p[G], t=gamma-1,
    A=R/pR=F_p[t]/(t^p),
    Y=Sel_(p^infinity)(E/K)^dual,
    d=dim_Fp(Y/(p,t)Y).

Here d is the minimal number of generators of Y/pY over the local Artin ring A. It is not the Z_p-rank of Y and not dim_Fp(Y/pY).

A minimal presentation A^b -> A^d -> Y/pY -> 0 has every matrix entry in (t). Every d-by-d minor is therefore in (t)^d. If there are too few columns the Fitting ideal is zero, and the same inclusion holds. Fitting ideals commute with base change, so BH gives

    theta_K^(MT,#) mod p in Fitt_0,A(Y/pY) subset (t)^d.

Every element of R has a unique expression sum_(j=0)^(p-1) b_j t^j with b_j in Z_p. Let lambda=zeta_p-1, with v_lambda(lambda)=1. Since v_lambda(p)=p-1>=4, a p-divisible coefficient cannot contribute at valuation less than four. Terms with degree at least four cannot contribute below four either. At degrees below four the valuations of p-unit terms are distinct. Consequently

    d_0=v_lambda(chi(theta_K^MT))<4

forces the first nonzero coefficient of theta_K^MT mod p to have degree exactly d_0. Inversion sends t to -t/(1+t), so it preserves this degree. The Fitting containment therefore implies

    d<=d_0<=3.

This argument explicitly includes p=5: the threshold is strictly below p-1=4 there.

**DERIVED — restriction.** The image of G_K under the residual representation contains the commutator of GL_2(F_p), because K/Q is abelian. It therefore contains SL_2(F_p), which has no invariant nonzero vector in the standard representation. Thus E(K)[p]=0, and also E(K)[p^infinity]=0. Inflation–restriction makes

    H^1(Q,E[p^infinity]) -> H^1(K,E[p^infinity])

injective. Local Kummer conditions are preserved by restriction, so the same is true for classical Selmer groups. Dualizing and using G-invariance gives a surjection

    Y_G ->> X_p.

It follows that

    d_p=dim_Fp X_p/pX_p
       <=dim_Fp Y/(p,t)Y=d<=3.

By B2–B3, d_p is even and at least two. Hence d_p=2, and

    v_lambda(chi(theta_K^MT))<4
        => Sha(E/Q)[p^infinity]=0.

Composite conductor introduces no extra obstruction in this argument: BH applies to an abelian K of the stated conductor, and the ring used after projection is still Z_p[C_p]. No G2 or Kato auxiliary-prime condition is inserted into this criterion.

## B6. Exact countermodels to the tempting stronger inferences

**DERIVED — primitivity is not quadratic primitivity.** The abstract Z_p-module

    X=Z_p^2 direct_sum (Z/p)^2

has d=4, with Fitt_2=(p^2), Fitt_3=(p), and Fitt_4=Z_p. It is compatible with an even-index pattern M_2=2, M_4=M_6=...=0. Thus M_infinity=0 does not imply r_p=2. This is a module countermodel to an implication, not a claim that this is the Selmer group of the seed at an uncomputed prime.

**DERIVED — why <=4 is insufficient even in the group ring.** Over R=Z_p[C_p] take

    Y=(R/(t))^2 direct_sum (R/(p,t))^2.

Then Y_G=Z_p^2 direct_sum (Z/p)^2 and

    Fitt_0,R(Y)=(t)^2*(p,t)^2,

which contains theta=t^4. Yet a faithful character gives v_lambda(chi(theta))=4 and the base module still requires four generators. This directly refutes any deduction of primary-Sha vanishing from the weaker bound <=4 using just these Fitting and restriction inputs. It is again an algebraic countermodel, not a fabricated elliptic-curve example.

**DERIVED — first missing assertions.** Replacing a first nonzero p-power quantity by a unit omits its valuation; replacing a unit witness of unspecified length by a quadratic one omits an upper bound for d_p; replacing complex L-value nonvanishing by low depth omits a normalized p-adic valuation estimate. None of these omissions is supplied by Part A.

## B7. What is, and is not, authorized

**DERIVED.** The exact outstanding sufficient statement may allow the field, conductor and character to depend on p:

    There is one fixed finite set S such that, for every p outside S,
    some real cyclic degree-p K/Q of conductor prime to pN and some
    faithful chi satisfy v_(zeta_p-1)(chi(theta_K^MT))<4.

Alternatively, one may prove a two-prime unit Kurihara witness or an independent Selmer upper bound. This review supplies none of them. A bound expressed in terms of r_p itself has not crossed the gap. No automatic construction session, broad source screen, new prime certificate, or canonical-state integration is performed.
