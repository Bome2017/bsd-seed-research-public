## External sources

**Banwait — verified as a dated preprint and frontier report.** [arXiv:2609.08431v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08431v1) is dated 8 September 2026. Its [§1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08431v1#S1.SS1) reports that no rank-at-least-two E/Q has known unconditional whole-Sha finiteness. It distinguishes uniform large-prime vanishing from fixed-prime work, and reports even infinitely many vanishing primes open in this setting. Sections 13 and 16 discuss a hoped-for common algebraic object and the failure of its CM machinery to extend as stated to non-CM curves. This is relevant frontier context, not a theorem applicable to this non-CM seed, a proof of exhaustive literature absence, or a proof that Z is necessary. “Almost a published-literature version” is replaced by “a current preprint reporting the same frontier.” The general rank-0/1 precedent should be invoked with the analytic-rank and theorem hypotheses actually used; the introductory shorthand is not imported as a new algebraic-rank theorem.

Its abstract claims analytic rank two implies algebraic rank two and finite Sha. Its proof was not audited in this task. Exact-title/author searches did not locate independent validation in the reviewed results. That is a search boundary, not a mathematical rejection. It cannot upgrade FIN without an applicable proof audit; disagreement with a later frontier report is not itself a refutation.

**Elkies — verified primary reference and author account.** The [1987 Inventiones article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01388985) gives the infinitude theorem for elliptic curves over Q. The author's [1991 published account](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_1991__198-199-200__127_0.pdf), printed p.128, explicitly recalls infinitude and explains how a new supersingular prime is obtained outside any previous finite list. This confirms the needed coverage constraint for the seed. It does not give a uniform Sha theorem at those primes. The initial DOI redirect and EuDML request failed; the publisher page and Numdam account were then accessible.

The web sources are cited as sources of their precise statements, not as instructions. No blanket “no accepted theorem anywhere exists” finding is claimed from this bounded check. The project finding remains: **no validated applicable argument in the inspected record closes FIN or FORMULA.**
