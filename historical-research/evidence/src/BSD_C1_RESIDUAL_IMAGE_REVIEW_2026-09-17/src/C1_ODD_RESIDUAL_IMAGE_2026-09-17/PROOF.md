# Positive residual-image result for the parent Zywina family

Date: 2026-09-17.
Status: author-side mathematical deduction, supplied for independent review. This is not an independently sealed result or a novelty claim. C1's definition, all existing seals, and the current Sha boundary are unchanged.

## 1. Statement and exact scope

Let m,n be positive integers such that

    m, A=m+16n^2, B=m+25n^2

are primes congruent to 11 modulo 24. Let

    E: y^2=x^3-5Ax^2+4ABx.

Then, for EVERY odd rational prime ell,

    image(rho_bar_{E,ell}) = GL_2(F_ell).

For ell != A, the proof needs no isogeny classification: the local representation at A is absolutely irreducible, and a multiplicative prime supplies a transvection. The one remaining coefficient prime ell=A is handled by Mazur's rational prime-isogeny theorem.

This is a statement about each mod-ell representation separately. No simultaneous adelic independence, integral ell-adic lifting, Selmer dimension, Sha finiteness, or BSD leading coefficient is asserted.

## 2. Arithmetic preliminaries

Since B-m=25n^2 is divisible by 24, n^2 is divisible by 24; hence 12 divides n. In particular

    A >= 11+16*12^2 = 2315 > 163.

The primes m,A,B are distinct, and none divides n. For example, m|n would make A=m(1+16m(n/m)^2) composite; the other claims follow similarly or from the differences A-m=16n^2, B-m=25n^2, B-A=9n^2.

The exact invariants are

    c4 = 16A(13m+100n^2),
    Delta = 2^8*3^2*m*A^3*B^2.

At m, c4 is congruent to 25600 n^4 and is nonzero; v_m(Delta)=1. Thus E has multiplicative reduction I_1.
At B, c4 is congruent to 32400 n^4 and is nonzero; v_B(Delta)=2. Thus E has multiplicative reduction I_2.
At A, v_A(c4)=1 and v_A(Delta)=3. The explicit good-reduction calculation below replaces any need to infer the inertia action merely from a Kodaira label.

All these models are minimal at the named primes because the discriminant valuations are below 12. The primes are different from 2,3,5.

## 3. An explicit local irreducibility proof at A

Put q=A, and work first over the maximal unramified extension of Q_q, which contains a fourth root of unity i. Adjoin u with u^4=q. Make the change

    x=u^2 X,  y=u^3 Y.

The equation becomes

    Y^2 = X^3 - 5u^2 X^2 + 4B X.

Its discriminant is Delta/u^12, a q-adic unit. Its special fibre is

    Y^2 = X^3 + 4 B_bar X,

which is nonsingular because q does not divide 2B.

Choose the tame inertia generator sending u to i u. The induced descent automorphism on the special fibre is, up to replacing the generator by its inverse,

    (X,Y) -> (-X,iY).

Its square is [-1]. Therefore, for every odd ell != q, the inertia matrix T on E[ell] satisfies

    T^2=-I.

Good reduction identifies prime-to-q torsion with special-fibre torsion, so this is an assertion about the actual representation. Inertia has determinant one because the ell-cyclotomic character is unramified at q != ell. Consequently T has two DISTINCT eigenvalues i and -i over Fbar_ell: it cannot be scalar (a scalar square root of -I has determinant -1).

The inertia subgroup after this tame degree-four extension acts trivially on E[ell], by good reduction. Thus the original inertia image is precisely the cyclic order-four group generated by T, and wild inertia acts trivially. Tame Frobenius conjugation therefore gives

    F T F^{-1} = T^q.

Because q=A is 3 modulo 4, this is T^{-1}. Frobenius therefore exchanges the two T-eigenlines. A line stable under the entire local decomposition group would have to be a T-eigenline and cannot be stable under F. Thus the local representation, and hence the global representation, is absolutely irreducible for every odd ell != A.

The argument is independent of whether -1 is a square in F_ell. It also applies at ell=3. The only excluded coefficient prime in this local argument is ell=A.

## 4. A transvection at every odd coefficient prime

Fix an odd ell. Use the multiplicative prime m if ell != m. If ell=m, use B instead. These choices always give a residue prime different from ell and a minimal discriminant valuation equal to 1 or 2, respectively, and not divisible by ell.

For multiplicative reduction, split after an unramified quadratic extension if necessary. Tate uniformization describes ell-torsion using mu_ell and an ell-th root of the Tate parameter. The parameter has valuation 1 at m or 2 at B. Over the maximal unramified field, units have ell-th roots; the non-ell-divisible valuation therefore gives nontrivial tame inertia on the root of the parameter. In a suitable basis the residual inertia image contains

    [[1,c],[0,1]], c != 0 in F_ell.

That is a nontrivial transvection. The unramified quadratic twist in the nonsplit case is trivial on inertia and does not change this conclusion.

## 5. The elementary group lemma

Let Gamma <= GL_2(F_ell), ell a prime. If Gamma is irreducible and contains a nontrivial transvection, then it contains SL_2(F_ell).

Proof: choose coordinates making the transvection upper unipotent. Its powers provide all matrices u(a)=[[1,a],[0,1]], since the coefficient field is the PRIME field F_ell. Irreducibility supplies g moving its fixed line <e1> to a different line. An upper unipotent then moves that line to <e2>. Conjugating the original transvection by the resulting element gives a nontrivial lower unipotent. Its powers provide all lower unipotents. Upper and lower elementary matrices generate SL_2(F_ell), by row reduction. This proves the lemma without a subgroup classification.

The determinant of rho_bar_{E,ell} is the cyclotomic character and is surjective over Q. Thus containment of SL_2(F_ell) gives equality with GL_2(F_ell).

Sections 3-5 prove the main statement for ell != A.

## 6. The coefficient prime ell=A

Reducibility of E[A] over F_A would give a rational cyclic isogeny of prime degree A. Mazur's theorem restricts rational prime-isogeny degrees to

    {2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,37,43,67,163}.

But A>163 by Section 2. Thus E[A] is irreducible. Section 4 supplies a transvection at m, and Section 5 completes the proof at ell=A.

Source precision: Mazur's original publisher page and bibliographic record were checked, but its paywalled original theorem text was not retrieved. The precise theorem was read in Banwait, arXiv:2101.02673v3, Theorem 1.2, which explicitly restates Mazur's Theorem 1. It was cross-checked in Michaud-Jacobs, arXiv:2209.03153v2, Theorem 1. No GRH enters Mazur's theorem over Q; the GRH-qualified quadratic-field algorithms elsewhere in Banwait's paper are not used.

## 7. A same-family option without the artificial modulo-7 restrictions

For discussion only, define C60 by

    m == 11 (mod 120), n == 0 (mod 60), m,n>0,
    m, m+16n^2, m+25n^2 all prime.

This is a SUPERFAMILY of C1, not a relabelling or replacement of C1. Indeed, C1's m==491 (mod 840) implies m==11 (mod 120), and 420|n implies 60|n.

All three primes in C60 are 11 modulo 120, hence 11 modulo 24. Zywina supplies rank two, the two rational sections, and Sha[2^infinity]=0. The preceding theorem supplies the full mod-5 image without any reduction test at 7.

At 5, m==1 and n==0; the reduction is

    y^2=x^3+4x,

with 8 points, so a5=-2, good ordinary reduction, and E(Q_5)[5]=0. The same m==1 (mod 5), multiplicative I_1 prime m, and prime-to-5 Tamagawa conditions used on C1 remain. This does not assert every downstream theorem automatically extends: its hypotheses and field identifications still have to be carried explicitly.

Infinitude: take the positive-relative-density set of primes 11 modulo 120, and apply Tao-Ziegler Theorem 1.3 to

    0, 16*(60u)^2, 25*(60u)^2.

The polynomials vanish at zero and are multiples of 120. Hence infinitely many qualifying pairs exist. Zywina's reduced j-invariant argument separates them geometrically: the denominator 9*m*B^2 recovers the distinct primes m and B, and then n>0. Thus there are infinitely many geometrically distinct curves.

One exact example outside C1 is

    (m,n)=(3491,60), A=61091, B=93491.

All three primes are verified by trial division in the checker. This is not claimed to be the smallest C60 example by any global height ordering. Its odd conductor part is 3*m*A^2*B = 3654225778448609283. The retained C1 example (3011,420) has odd conductor part 318222060510286953256323, a ratio about 87083.3.

These are sizes, not runtimes. Neither this calculation nor the larger family authorizes a new number-field, class-group, modular-symbol, or calibration computation. In particular the degree-24 central obstruction still holds; smaller coefficients do not remove it.

## 8. What this changes and what it does not

Changed, conditional only on independent review of this proof:
- all odd-prime residual surjectivity is supplied uniformly on the parent Zywina family;
- the modulo-7 restrictions in C1 were a convenience for its original irreducibility check, not necessary for that property;
- an explicitly specified larger infinite family retains the stated p=5 curve-side inputs.

Unchanged:
- no C1 or C60 bound on c,b,r5, Sel_5, or Sha[5^infinity];
- no valued Stark/Euler-system component or class-group generator coverage;
- no escape from generic-radical saturation or the central norm-channel obstruction;
- no assertion of adelic independence or simultaneous full product image;
- no arithmetic witness, no manuscript, no canonical seal or shared ledger edit.

The next major operation must still identify an evaluated arithmetic input. Repeatedly verifying Galois-image hypotheses is not a replacement for it.
