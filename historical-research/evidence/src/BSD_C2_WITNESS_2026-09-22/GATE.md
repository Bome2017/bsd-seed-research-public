# GATE — binding

## K1. Do not assume the target
**Do not assume the desired unit statement, or any consequence available only under that assumption.** An equivalent
criterion is admissible when it is **proved by an independent argument** — equivalence alone does not make a proof
circular. What supplies no new input is merely restating the leading-term identity: re-deriving, through the same
BLV/BD machinery, a statement already known to follow from (or be equivalent to) the unit statement. Note
`INPUTS.md` §2: only the forward bridge is asserted; the naive converse is false without `rho_p = 4`.

## K2. Route C must not enumerate
Any computational proposal must show explicitly how it avoids materializing the full definite Shimura set
(Eichler mass `5.42e14`). "Compute phi_E on the Brandt module" without a compression argument is not a route.

## K3. Do not reopen (unless you find an actual dependency error)
twist-Sha of `E^{-7}`; Manin constants; the modular degree; anomalous primes (`a_p` is even at every odd good `p`);
A2a / A2b; the Cassels–Tate shortcut; the `D = -7` certificate. These are settled or off the critical path.

## K4. Label every claim
proved theorem (with citation) / identification you proved yourself (with proof) / conditional (on what) /
conjecture / unsupported inference. Nothing conditional may be promoted.

## K5. Failure-to-find is not a theorem
If you cannot locate a result, return the missing statement. Do not turn "not found" into "does not exist". An
infeasible *method* is not an impossibility *result*.

## K6. Out of scope
supersingular primes (a separate lane, A3); the bad primes `2243, 4547, 5843`; `I_3(13)` (a different field,
`Q(sqrt -23)`); any claim of BSD, FIN or FORMULA.
