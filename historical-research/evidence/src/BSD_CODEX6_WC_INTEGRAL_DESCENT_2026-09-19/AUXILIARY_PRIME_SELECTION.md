# What freedom of auxiliary primes buys

For each p, WC may select its own conductor. The support theorem proves that one or two prime factors suffice, with no increase in the best depth h_p. This is the main unconditional use of that freedom obtained here.

Residual surjectivity also gives infinitely many primes with ell=1 modulo p and nonidentity unipotent Frobenius. They have E(F_ell)[p] of dimension one. At higher precision, the retained p-adic surjectivity permits Frobenius congruent to a nontrivial upper triangular unipotent matrix modulo p^k, imposing ell=1 and a_ell=2 modulo p^k while preserving residual cyclicity. Chebotarev supplies eligible primes with these prescribed representation conditions, outside any finite prohibited set.

This chooses the shape of the local group. It does not choose a nonzero Kato derivative or guarantee that its pairing with a local point is nonzero. Ota Lemma 4.10 and Kim Proposition 2.2 concern detection of already specified nonzero cohomology classes.

Likewise, detecting the two known Mordell-Weil basis classes only controls that two-dimensional subspace of Selmer. The sought conclusion concerns the entire Selmer group. When extra Sha classes occur, the required determinant on the full Selmer data need not be primitive. Treating detection of the Mordell-Weil basis as surjectivity/injectivity on full Selmer would assume the missing conclusion.

At ordinary p, the exact positive statement is O1: if Sha[p^infinity]=0, a primitive two-prime Kurihara witness exists, and Kim's localization isomorphism ensures both primes are Ota-admissible. This is useful for interpreting and searching for witnesses, but its antecedent is the vanishing we want to prove. Nonzero witnesses can certify a result when found; this is not a uniform existence theorem.

A uniform content theorem would have to prove that the evaluated weighted-cycle ideal D_{p,k} has index dividing one fixed A for a suitable k at each p, not merely that the eligible-prime set is infinite. Neither Chebotarev nor residual surjectivity alone bounds that index.

Do not assert that all core pairs have the same content, that an arbitrary first pair works, or that a finite collection of pairs covers all p. No pair or new value of h_p was computed in this session.
