# The local Ota regulator has integral index one

Status: proved mathematical deduction, importing standard integral local Tate duality and Ota's construction and reciprocity. Not kernel-formalized. The theorem concerns the local comparison map. It is not a uniform content bound for the global Kato classes.

Fix p in P and an admissible squarefree S. Put F=Q(S), the maximal p-extension in Q(zeta_S), Gamma=Gal(F/Q), K=F tensor Q_p, O=O_F tensor Z_p, T=T_p(E), and R=Z_p[Gamma]. Cohomology over the semilocal algebra K means the sum over its field factors. Let

    L_S = H^1(K,T) / H_f^1(K,T),

with H_f the integral Kummer subgroup. Non-anomalous good reduction will identify the p-adic completion of E(K) with its formal group.

## Lemma L1: the cyclotomic trace is a normal integral basis generator

Set eta_S=Tr_{Q(zeta_S)/F}(zeta_S). Its Gamma-conjugates form a Z_p-basis of O.

Proof. For each prime ell dividing the squarefree S, the elements zeta_ell^a, 1<=a<ell, form a Z-basis of Z[zeta_ell]: replacing 1 in the usual power basis uses the relation 1+sum_{a=1}^{ell-1}zeta_ell^a=0 and has determinant a unit. Tensoring these bases over the distinct prime factors gives the conjugates of zeta_S, up to a permutation by CRT. Over Z_p the tensor product identifies with Z_p[zeta_S]: the natural map is surjective, both modules have rank phi(S), and p does not divide S. Thus these conjugates are a normal integral basis over Z_p. Invariants under H=Gal(Q(zeta_S)/F) have a basis of H-orbit sums. These are exactly the conjugates of eta_S. Invariants are O_F tensor Z_p; equivalently one may use the averaging idempotent since #H is prime to p. QED.

## Lemma L2: Ota's point is a formal-group normal basis generator

The formal logarithm gives log_E/p: Ehat(O) -> O, an integral isomorphism for unramified K at p>=5. On each field factor let sigma be Frobenius. In the coordinate log_E/p, Ota's operator

    c |-> (1-a_p sigma/p+sigma^2/p) log_E(c)

is p-a_p sigma+sigma^2 acting on O. Every residue field degree is a power of p. Consequently sigma-1 is nilpotent modulo p. The constant term of the polynomial sigma(sigma-a_p), expanded in sigma-1, is 1-a_p, which is nonzero modulo p by non-anomalous reduction. The operator is invertible modulo p and hence integrally invertible. It commutes with Gamma. (This also gives a short proof of Ota Lemma 5.5 in the present setting.)

Ota's c_S is the inverse image of eta_S under this operator. Lemma L1 therefore shows that {c_S^gamma} is an integral basis of Ehat(O), or equivalently Ehat(O) is free of rank one over R generated by c_S.

## Lemma L3: the integral pairing is perfect

No residue-field point has p-power order. To see this, E[p](F_{p^d}) is the kernel of Frob_p^d-1 on the etale p-torsion. In the ordinary case its eigenvalue is a_p modulo p, and for d a p-power, a_p^d=a_p in F_p; non-anomalous reduction excludes eigenvalue 1. In the supersingular case the geometric p-torsion has no etale part. Hence reduction contributes no p-primary completion, and E(K) completed at p is Ehat(O). The formal logarithm also shows that E(K)[p^infinity]=0.

Integral local Tate duality, with the Weil pairing, gives a perfect pairing

    Ehat(O) x L_S -> Z_p.

For clarity, at level p^n the finite Kummer subgroup is its own annihilator. Local duality identifies H^2(K,T) with the dual of E(K)[p^infinity], so it is zero here; consequently integral cohomology reduces to H^1(K,E[p^n]) without a torsion correction. Passing through the finite perfect pairings and then to inverse limits gives the displayed integral pairing. Its rational version is Ota's cup-product pairing, and Ota equation (5.7) expresses it as Tr(log_E(c) exp_S^*(x)).

## Theorem L4: index one

Define

    U_S: L_S -> R,
    U_S(x)=sum_{gamma in Gamma} <c_S,x^(gamma^(-1))> gamma.

Then U_S is an R-linear isomorphism. In particular its integral index is exactly one for every eligible p and S.

Proof. Invariance of the pairing rewrites the coefficient at gamma as <c_S^gamma,x>. The c_S^gamma are an integral basis by L2, and L3 identifies L_S with their Z_p-dual. Thus recording these coefficients is a Z_p-linear isomorphism. Reindexing gamma proves U_S(delta*x)=delta*U_S(x); Gamma is abelian. QED.

The map preserves the augmentation filtration I^j L_S exactly and induces isomorphisms

    I^j L_S/I^(j+1)L_S -> I^j R/I^(j+1)R.

Ota Theorem 5.9 supplies the integral Kato class z_S before FIN, and Corollary 5.13 says

    U_S(loc_p z_S)=theta_{S,p}.

Here loc_p means localization followed by the quotient defining L_S. Since theta_{S,p} belongs to I^2, this particular local class belongs to I^2 L_S. Its degree-two class has precisely the same nonvanishing and p-depth as the WC witness.

## The remaining arithmetic map

The unresolved map is therefore the GLOBAL specialization

    z_S in H^1(F,T) -> loc_p(z_S) in I^2 L_S -> I^2 L_S/I^3 L_S.

The index-one theorem eliminates denominators or saturation loss in U_S. It gives no primitivity assertion for that global class or for its degree-two image. Even an integral primitive group-ring element can lie in I^3 and have zero degree-two class. The global source and localization vary with p and S; no finite integral source with a proved global image-content bound has been extracted from these local isomorphisms.

This is why an integral regulator comparison can be completely perfect while the FIN question remains open.
