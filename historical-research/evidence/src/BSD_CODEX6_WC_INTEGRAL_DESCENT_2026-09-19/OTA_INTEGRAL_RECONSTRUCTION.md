The proof of 6.4 expands the Kato class using the derivative operators and applies the local/global Selmer argument. It does not exhibit a nonzero fixed integer determinant for all p. It permits vanishing of every rank-two coefficient when the relevant Selmer obstruction persists. Integral construction and arithmetic nonvanishing are distinct steps of the proof.

## Six requested questions

1. **Integral characteristic-zero source?** Yes. The actual theta_S are rational group-ring elements with a fixed power-of-two denominator bound for these conductors. H3 realizes their p-primary quadratic parts from fixed integral homology. They are not characteristic-zero elements of their degree-two quotients: those quotients are torsion and rationalize to zero.
2. **Uniform Fitting/congruence ideal?** No such controlling Z-ideal is obtained from Ota's theorem or proof. UC isolates the evaluated cycle ideals that would have to be controlled. Kim's ordinary structural formula describes related p-local defects, and therefore reinforces the FIN-equivalence issue.
3. **One integer determinant?** The local regulator determinant is a unit, by L4. The global localization/degree-two image is the missing arithmetic factor. No nonzero determinant over Z bounding it at all p was produced.
4. **Tame norm relations?** They give T3, an exact reduction to one or two auxiliary primes. Their augmentation factors a_ell-2 may vanish modulo p and are not generally invertible. They do not compare the content for unrelated p.
5. **Finite list of tame levels?** No. At a fixed level S the positive-degree augmentation groups have support only among prime divisors of #G_S. A finite list has finite support and cannot supply a nonzero witness for every p in P.
6. **One source with uniformly bounded saturation?** The fixed source and maps are constructed in H3. The needed bound after evaluation by z_E is precisely UC, unproved. Replacing it by bounded denominators or perfectness of the local regulator is invalid.

The new positive theorem is the integral isomorphism L4; its full proof and its import of local Tate duality are recorded separately. It is not relabelled as the uniform global theorem requested by WC.
