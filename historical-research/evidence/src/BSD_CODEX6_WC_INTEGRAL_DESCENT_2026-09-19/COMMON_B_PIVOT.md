## Precise common-B theorem

Assume a pre-FIN construction produces B in Q_{>0}, proves q=4B, and proves for each prime p that Sha[p^infinity] is finite with

    length_Zp Sha[p^infinity] <= v_p(B).

Then every v_p(B) is nonnegative, since the left side is a nonnegative integer. Writing B in lowest terms therefore shows B is a positive integer. The retained interval

    35.99427 < q < 36.00573

places B between 8.9985675 and 9.0014325. Thus B=9. The primary groups vanish away from 3, and the 3-primary group is finite; hence Sha is finite and its order divides 9. The retained 3-primary theorem supplies order 9, with its inherited source qualifications. Consequently #Sha=9 and q=36=4#Sha, and the retained RANK theorem completes the three-part architecture.

Rationality of B is a genuine hypothesis. A bare positive real B does not carry the rational prime valuations used in this proof. Individual primary finiteness is also explicit; no finite order is assigned to a potentially infinite primary group.

## Test against the object constructed here

The fixed homology functional z_E is a period functional for the weight-two newform. Its weighted evaluations give critical-value modular symbols. Its value on the path to 0 vanishes because L(E,1)=0. There is no theorem here identifying a rational functional or determinant of those data with L''/(2 Omega Reg). The real Neron-Tate regulator is already known, but that does not identify a leading-term zeta coefficient.

L4 gives exact local control of the conversion from a Kato localization to theta. It does not bound every primary order by a common rational B and does not provide an archimedean derivative comparison. Thus the current object supplies neither of the two new common-B interfaces. No common B was constructed, and the one-sided certificate is not announced as an executable alternative route.
