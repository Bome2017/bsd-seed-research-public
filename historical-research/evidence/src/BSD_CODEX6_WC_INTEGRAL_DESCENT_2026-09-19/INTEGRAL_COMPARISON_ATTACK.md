# Integral comparison attack and the remaining arithmetic lemma

The attack constructs a fixed finite integral source and proves the local Ota comparison has index one. The unresolved integral index is the index of selected logarithm-weighted cycles AFTER evaluation by the fixed elliptic period functional. This is specific arithmetic data; it is not supplied by a denominator bound.

## One explicit remaining lemma: UC

Use H, z_E and c_E from HOMOLOGY_SOURCE_THEOREM.md. Fix p in P and k>=1, and let R_k=Z/p^k. Consider all squarefree S with one or two factors, each a good prime ell not dividing pN, ell=1 mod p^k, and E(F_ell)[p] cyclic. Choose the quotient discrete logs modulo p^k. The integral cycle is

    C_{p,k,S}=sum_a binom(log_ell(a),2) C_{a,S}                 if S=ell,
    C_{p,k,S}=sum_a log_ell(a) log_m(a) C_{a,S}                if S=ell*m.

Logarithms mean integer representatives modulo p^k. Their cycles modulo p^k H are independent of changing these representatives: for the binomial coefficient this uses that 2 is invertible modulo p^k. Generator changes need not fix each cycle; every such cycle still realizes an admissible coefficient. Use the fixed choices throughout.

Let F_{p,k} be the free R_k-module on this explicitly defined set of conductors/cycles (finite linear combinations). Define

    Psi_{p,k}: F_{p,k} -> R_k,
    [S] |-> z_E(C_{p,k,S}) mod p^k,
    D_{p,k}=image(Psi_{p,k}).

Although F_{p,k} may have infinite rank, D_{p,k} is a principal ideal of the finite chain ring R_k, generated by finitely many of its displayed values. The source M_Z of the actual witnesses remains the fixed finite lattice above; F_{p,k} organizes the selected cycle tests, not a falsely finite replacement for their whole family.

**UC (UNPROVED arithmetic assertion).** There exists A in Z minus {0} such that for every p in P there is some k>=1 for which

    D_{p,k} != 0  and  A * (R_k/D_{p,k})=0.                 (UC)

Both clauses matter. If k<=v_p(A), the second clause alone says nothing.

This is a uniform cokernel-annihilation assertion for a fully specified map with a rank-one target and the distinguished functional already evaluated. It has no vague universal object and does not use FIN to define its source or target.

## Theorem U1: UC is equivalent to the exact WC antecedent

Fix a proposed nonzero A. If UC holds at p, write D_{p,k}=p^d R_k with 0<=d<k. The annihilation clause says d<=v_p(A). Some displayed generator of this ideal has depth d: otherwise all generators lie in p^(d+1)R_k. That value is c_E times a nonzero top-support coefficient of t(p,S), possibly reduced to a smaller modulus. Its nonzero reduction has the same valuation as the original coefficient, so h_p<=d<=v_p(A). Hence A belongs to J_E.

Conversely, if A belongs to J_E, the support theorem gives a top-support coefficient at a one- or two-prime S with depth d<=v_p(A), nonzero modulo p^e, where e is the relevant singleton exponent or the minimum of the two exponents. Take k=e. Both primes, if present, are 1 modulo p^k. Reducing the chosen logs to p^k realizes exactly this coefficient up to the p-unit c_E. Thus D_{p,k} contains p^d times a unit, is nonzero, and R_k/D_{p,k} is killed by p^d, hence by A. QED.

The theorem is an exact concrete reformulation, not a proof that the arithmetic task is easier. ORDINARY_CONVERSE_GATE.md proves that its depth-zero ordinary instances are equivalent to primary Sha vanishing. The cross-prime part is finite support of precisely those defects.

## What was actually proved toward UC

1. H3 constructs every specialization from a fixed finite free Z-lattice and an odd-prime primitive source element.
2. T3 reduces the cycle tests to one or two auxiliary primes, using the actual norm relations.
3. L4 proves that Ota's local cup-product regulator is an integral isomorphism, with index one for every p,S. Thus local regulator denominators are not the missing index.
4. Theorem O1 identifies the ordinary depth-zero test with Sha[p^infinity]=0, including the auxiliary-prime cyclicity check for primitive witnesses.

None proves that the evaluated cycle ideal D_{p,k} has a uniform nonzero integer annihilator for suitable k. A fixed Hecke congruence denominator does not do this: z_E is an f-eigenfunctional, so taking the Hecke span of these cycles multiplies its values by integral Hecke eigenvalues and cannot enlarge their image ideal. Saturation by division by p changes the cycles and requires a new arithmetic realization theorem.

## Finite-depth converse: a bounded separate transfer issue

If an exact equivalence for the entire ordinary part is desired, one additional precise task is to prove that Kim's two-prime finite-depth minimum can be attained with E(F_ell)[p] cyclic at both primes. A sufficient statement is: for each ordinary p with finite Sha[p^infinity], there is such a pair with a nonzero Kurihara number of valuation at most length_Zp Sha[p^infinity]. This is not needed for U1 or O1 and is not asserted proved here. It would clarify the equivalence; it would not establish the uniform bound.
