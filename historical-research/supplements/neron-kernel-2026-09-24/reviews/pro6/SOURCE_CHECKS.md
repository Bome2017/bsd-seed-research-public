# Primary-source bindings

These references support the mathematical imports, not a novelty claim.

1. Chan-Ho Kim and Kazuto Ota, *On the quantitative variation of congruence ideals and integral periods of modular forms*, arXiv:1905.02926v4.
   - https://arxiv.org/html/1905.02926v4
   - https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.02926v4
   - Relevant items: full Hecke definitions in section 2; Definition 2.12; section 6.1 pairing (6.1); Assumption 6.3; Theorem 6.5; Remark 6.6(1); Corollary 6.7.
   - The supersingular use retains the explicitly stated low-weight crystalline extension; it is not inferred from section 6.2's ordinary mu-invariant discussion.

2. Amod Agashe, Kenneth Ribet, William Stein, *The modular degree, congruence primes, and multiplicity one*.
   - https://www.math.fsu.edu/~agashe/math/moddeg3.pdf
   - Relevant items: Theorem 2.1 (printed p.3), perfect Hecke/q-expansion duality (p.7), Theorem 3.6 and Remark 3.7 (p.8).
   - Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 3.6 were additionally inspected in rendered PDF screenshots. The optimum quotient, rather than an unspecified parametrization, is used for d.

3. Kestutis Cesnavicius, Michael Neururer, Abhishek Saha, *The Manin constant and the modular degree*, arXiv:1911.09446v3.
   - https://arxiv.org/html/1911.09446v3
   - https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.09446v3
   - Relevant items: section 4.2 slash expansion; Proposition 5.14 (printed p.39); Proposition 6.2 and Example 6.3; Theorem 6.12 (p.46).
