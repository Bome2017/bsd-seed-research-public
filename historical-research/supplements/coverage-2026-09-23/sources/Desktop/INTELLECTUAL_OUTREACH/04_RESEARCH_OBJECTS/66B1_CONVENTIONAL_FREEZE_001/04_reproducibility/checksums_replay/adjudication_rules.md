# Stage-I adjudication rules

Scope: supplied original packet and publicly located primary sources only. No prior-conversation retrieval and no other model's reconstruction.

A mismatch in a local Kummer image defeats the stated Selmer theorem. A factor-of-two mismatch defeats the affected analytic-to-arithmetic comparison, not an independently valid descent. Failure of the proposed finite-field mechanism redirects the point-count proof unless the family itself is contradicted. An exact existing uniform theorem defeats a novelty claim, not its mathematical conclusion. A bounded search without such a theorem does not establish novelty.

The quotient-torsion lower bound does not establish the isogeny Selmer upper bound. The kernel in the isogeny-to-2-Selmer sequence must be computed on this curve. An undefined period is not to be fixed by making a BSD formula work. BSD(2), rank zero, Sha[2] vanishing, and strong BSD are separate claims.
