## Three things not to misread

- **`LEAN_FORMALIZED_66B1 = NO`.**  Two conventional investigations agreeing is not kernel
  certification.  The 62a1 Lean development is a separate artifact about a different curve.
- **`PRIOR_ART_BLOCKER = UNRESOLVED`.**  BSTW contains a stronger printed statement that explicitly
  includes 66b1.  The supportable claim is only that a bounded audit did not locate a source-complete
  proof of this exact normalized calculation.
- **Preregistration was partial.**  The signed-character iff, its proof, the period conversion, the
  model derivation and the Selmer *lower* bound were preregistered.  The uniform local *upper*
  bounds — and therefore the Sha and BSD conclusions — were post-freeze Stage-II work.  The first
  model also disclosed having seen a sample-size figure from the withheld README.
