## What was predicted versus what was only established later

- **Preregistered in L1:** the signed-character iff and its proof, the period conversion, the model
  derivation, and the structural Selmer **lower** bound.
- **Not preregistered — post-freeze Stage II only:** the uniform local **upper** bounds, and hence
  the actual Sha/BSD conclusions.

L4 states this explicitly and it must not be smoothed over.

## Independence qualifications recorded by the referee — preserved verbatim in substance

- The first model **disclosed** having seen the withheld README's inventory (38 family primes below
  4000).  The original design packet also supplied `q = 5`, `N₅ = 4`, and proposed the Selmer shape.
  **Claims of an entirely numerical-evidence-free or hypothesis-free preregistration would be
  inaccurate.**
- Self-recorded timestamps (freeze 16:59:12.719650 UTC, first retained count-program open
  16:59:30.666740 UTC) are internally consistent with the claimed order but **do not independently
  authenticate historical access order**.
- The referee's own `PROOF_CHECKPOINT_BEFORE_COMPUTATION_REVIEW.json` records completion of the
  proof audit before reviewing the count programs, but numerical metadata and prior-conversation
  claims were already visible; **it is not a claim of complete metadata blindness**.
