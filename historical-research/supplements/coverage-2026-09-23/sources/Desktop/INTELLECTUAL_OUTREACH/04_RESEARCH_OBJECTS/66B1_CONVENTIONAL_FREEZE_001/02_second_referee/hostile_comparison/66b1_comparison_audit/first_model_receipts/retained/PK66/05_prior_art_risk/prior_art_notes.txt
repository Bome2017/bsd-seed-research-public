== the positive-discriminant flag, from the four-curve theorem-chain document ==
### Arrow A: analytic rank zero and the exact 2-adic analytic valuation

**Source.** Shuai Zhai, *Non-vanishing theorems for quadratic twists of
elliptic curves*, Asian Journal of Mathematics 20 (2016), Theorems 1.5 and
1.7.

**Use.** Theorem 1.5 applies to `62a1` and `141c1` (negative discriminant);
Theorem 1.7 applies to `66b1` and `105a1` (positive discriminant).  The common
point-count condition is exactly `v2(#E(F_q))=2` after the period
normalization is handled correctly.

**Conclusion.** `L(E_q,1)!=0`, exact `v2(L/Omega+)` equal to 0 (negative
discriminant) or 1 (positive discriminant), and rank-zero/Sha finiteness via
Kolyvagin.

**Boundary.** Neither theorem proves `Sha[2]=0` or BSD at 2.  The paper's
"easy classical 2-descent" sentence is not a source-complete proof for these
four curves.  See `ZHAI_PRIOR_ART_AUDIT.md`.
