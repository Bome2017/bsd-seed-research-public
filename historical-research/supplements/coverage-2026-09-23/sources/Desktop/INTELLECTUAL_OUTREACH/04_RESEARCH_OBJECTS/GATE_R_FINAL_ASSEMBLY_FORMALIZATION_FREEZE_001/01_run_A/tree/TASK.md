programme.  Two of them describe an *earlier design* for the rank argument and
are **not** the specification for this job:

ingredients in the first one is a forecast, not a theorem, and it has since been
superseded.  **The specification is the five signatures in
`RequestProject/GateR/Assembly.lean` and nothing else.**  Do not treat a frozen
comment as a requirement, and do not add a target because a comment mentions one.
The comments are not repaired, because the sources are frozen.

## What may not be used

**Constructs.**  `sorry`, `admit`, any new `axiom`, `native_decide`,
`ofReduceBool`, `implemented_by`, `unsafe`, `extern`, `trustCompiler`.  The
accepted axiom footprint of each of the five theorems is exactly a subset of
`{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`.

**Weakenings of the endpoint.**  `Module.finrank`, `Group.rank`, `AddGroup.rank`,
or any natural-number rank proxy.  A statement about a group other than
`(P01.Ecurve ε m q).toAffine.Point`.

**Premises that would make the result conditional.**  `Module.Finite ℤ`,
`Module.Free ℤ`, `AddGroup.FG`, a `Finite` or `Fintype` instance or hypothesis on
the point group, Mordell–Weil finite generation, or any assumed torsion, rank,
or height hypothesis at the family boundary.  The five signatures carry exactly
`Params`, `H41` and the prime instance; nothing may be added to them.

**Out of scope entirely.**  Canonical or logarithmic heights.  Cohomological
`Sel₂`, `H¹`, Ш.  Any L-function, analytic rank, or BSD statement.  Widening
`m ≥ 3`, changing `ε`, relaxing the conditions in `Params` or `H41`, or enlarging
the family in any way.  Redefining `R1.height`, `R1.heightQ`, or any frozen
declaration.
