# Constraints applied; no new obstruction theorem

This session adds no new no-go theorem. The following retained results
were used to reject unsupported compression inferences.

| Constraint | Proved obstruction retained | Exact scope |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | A fixed integral source can have distinguished images divisible at every prime | Source finiteness/integrality alone does not bound evaluated image content |
| N2 | A finite free complex over \(\prod_p\mathbf Z_p\) can have finite nonzero defects at infinitely many primes | Profinite perfectness does not imply finite prime support; this does not exclude a genuinely identified rationally acyclic integral model |
| N3 | Finite primary groups at every prime can have infinite support | Pointwise finiteness/nonvanishing does not establish a global tail |
| N4 | \(h_p=0\iff\Sha[p^\infty]=0\) on the specified ordinary regime | No extension asserted to supersingular primes, positive depth for the exact Ota set, or a global equivalence of WC and FIN |
| N5 | A fixed finite tame level has positive augmentation quotients supported only at primes dividing its exponent | A finite list of such levels cannot supply nonzero graded witnesses at every remaining prime; inverse limits are not excluded |
| B1 | The retained primary and pairing data plus FIN allow \(9t^2\) | FIN does not determine \(t=1\) |
