# Exact base algebraic L-value certificate

The load-bearing input to Zhai Theorem 1.5 is not inferred from decimal
`special_value/real_period` data or from PARI's numerically normalized
`msfromell` routine.  The script `checker/exact_base_modular_symbol.gp`
instead uses the exact integral modular-symbol lattice.

## Exact calculation

In the plus part of the weight-2 new modular-symbol space at level `62`, the
exact Hecke conditions

```text
a_3=0, a_5=-2, a_7=0, a_11=0
```

isolate a one-dimensional rational eigenspace.  The eigenvalues are also
recomputed by exact finite-field point counts on `[1,-1,1,-1,1]`.
`mslattice` computes the canonical integral structure using rational and
integer cohomology.  Its primitive plus generator `ell_plus` satisfies

\[
 |\ell_+([0]-[\infty])|=\frac12.                              \tag{1}
\]

No call to `msfromell`, `lfun`, `ellperiods`, or `bestappr` occurs.
The defining integral-cohomology construction is documented in the official
[PARI modular-symbol specification](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/html-stable/Modular_symbols.html).

## Exact Neron normalization

For the normalized newform, write

\[
 F=x^+\Omega_E^+ + x^- i\Omega_E^-.
\]

The PARI modular-symbol specification states both

\[
 x^+([0]-[\infty])=L(E,1)/\Omega_E^+
\]

and that, when `E(R)` has one connected component, the canonical lattice is
generated by `x^+-x^-` and `2x^-`.  Its intersection with the plus line is
therefore exactly `Z*(2x^+)`.  Here `Delta_min=-496<0`, so there is one real
component.  The bound `opt_man` row

```text
62 a 1 [1,-1,1,-1,1] 1 1
```

certifies that this is the optimal curve and its Manin constant is `1`, so
the period is Neron-normalized without another scale factor.  Equation (1)
now gives, without choosing the irrelevant sign,

\[
 \left|\frac{L(E,1)}{\Omega_E^+}\right|=\frac14,
 \qquad v_2\left(L(E,1)/\Omega_E^+\right)=-2.                 \tag{2}
\]

The source containing that `opt_man` row is bound by SHA-256 in
`evidence/SOURCE_BINDINGS.tsv`.  The sign is numerically positive, but only
the exact nonzero value and valuation in (2) are load-bearing for Zhai.

## Claim boundary

PARI's `msfromell` uses numerical period scaling followed by rational
reconstruction and is deliberately not used here.  The certificate combines
an exact Hecke eigenspace, the exact canonical integral lattice, the exact
one-component lattice normalization, and the bound Manin constant `1`.
It establishes the base valuation needed by Zhai; it does not compute any
twist Selmer group or prove BSD.
