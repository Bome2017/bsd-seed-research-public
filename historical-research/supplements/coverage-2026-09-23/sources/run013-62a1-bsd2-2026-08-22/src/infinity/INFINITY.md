# Archimedean local condition

Status: proof complete; independent review PASS.

For the dual covering

\[
 n^2=du^4-15qu^2v^2+\frac{64q^2}{d}v^4
\]

with `q>0` and `d<0`, every term on the right is nonpositive and at least
one is strictly negative for a nonzero projective pair `(u:v)`.  Hence the
covering has no real point.

This excludes

\[
 -1,-2,-q,-2q
\]

from the dual Selmer group.  The identity covering is globally soluble.
The four retained `phi_q` classes are also globally soluble, so their
archimedean local condition is automatic.
