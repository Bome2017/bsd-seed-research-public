## 1. Base curve and descent model

Let `E` be the optimal curve with Cremona label `62a1` (LMFDB `62.a4`):

\[
  \mathcal E:\quad v^2+uv+v=u^3-u^2-u+1.
\]

The rational change of variables

\[
  X=4(u+1),\qquad Y=8v+4u+4
\]

identifies it with

\[
  E_1:\quad Y^2=X^3-15X^2+64X.
\]

For a positive odd prime `q`, define

\[
 E_q:\quad y^2=x^3-15q x^2+64q^2x
\]

and

\[
 E'_q:\quad y^2=x^3+30q x^2-31q^2x
             =x(x-q)(x+31q).
\]

The map with kernel generated by `(0,0)` is, away from the kernel,

\[
 \phi_q(x,y)=
 \left(\frac{y^2}{x^2},
       \frac{y(64q^2-x^2)}{x^2}\right):E_q\longrightarrow E'_q.
\]

The normalized dual, away from its kernel, is

\[
 \widehat\phi_q(x,y)=
 \left(\frac{y^2}{4x^2},
       \frac{y(-31q^2-x^2)}{8x^2}\right):E'_q\longrightarrow E_q.
\]

Both maps extend over their kernels and satisfy
`widehat(phi_q) o phi_q = [2]`.

## 2. Prime family

Write

\[
 a_q(E)=q+1-\#\widetilde E(\mathbf F_q).
\]

The frozen family is

\[
 F_{62}=\left\{
 q\ \text{prime}:\
 q\nmid372,\
 q\equiv5\pmod8,\
 \left(\frac q{31}\right)=-1,\
 v_2\!\left(\#\widetilde E(\mathbf F_q)\right)=2,\
 a_q(E)\ne0
 \right\}.
\]

The descent proof may establish the Selmer identities under a larger local
signature, but the frozen theorem must retain these literal fields.

## 3. Selmer orientation

All squareclasses lie in `Q*/Q*2`.  The notation is fixed by the coverings:

\[
 C^{(\phi)}_{q,d}:\quad
 n^2=d u^4+30q u^2v^2-\frac{31q^2}{d}v^4,
\]

\[
 C^{(\widehat\phi)}_{q,d}:\quad
 n^2=d u^4-15q u^2v^2+\frac{64q^2}{d}v^4.
\]

Membership in the corresponding Selmer group means local solubility at every
place of `Q`.  This convention prevents reversal of the two isogenies.

## 4. Frozen theorem

For every `q in F_62`, prove

\[
 \operatorname{Sel}^{(\phi_q)}(E_q/\mathbf Q)
   =\{1,-31,q,-31q\}
\]

and

\[
 \operatorname{Sel}^{(\widehat\phi_q)}(E'_q/\mathbf Q)
   =\{1\}.
\]
