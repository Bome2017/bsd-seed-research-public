# 14 — CM Chow-model test for the H9R23 approximants (2026-09-16)

Question (operator): can both Scholl motives M(theta(chi Psi_{l_n})) and M(theta(chi^{-1} Psi_{l_n})) be identified, in CHOW motives over
F = K_chi, with the same explicit CM motive M(Psi_{l_n}), so that Z_n = (iso) o id o (iso)^{-1} is an algebraic correspondence with
uniformly bounded p-denominator?  Three checks. Sources: CH extract (local), Flapan–Lang arXiv:1708.03145v2 and Qiu arXiv:2105.12561v2
(fetched today, text extracted to the scratchpad; line numbers below index those extracts). [V] verbatim, [D] derived.

## Check 1 — CM-character shape: YES
[V] CH l.1088-1092: Psi_T(sigma) = (1+T)^{l(sigma)}, sigma|_{K_{p_frak^infty}} = gamma_p^{l(sigma)}, K_{p_frak^infty} the p_frak-ramified Z_p-extension;
    l.1096-1098: p^b = order of the p-part of the class number, v in Z_p with v^{p^b} = eps_cyc(gamma_p^{p^b}); l.1305-1307: at an arithmetic
    point the CM slots are theta series of (chi psi_x^{-1}, chi^{-1} psi_x^{-1}) with psi_x = Psi_T|_x.
[D] At a crystalline point of weight k, 1+T = v^{k-1}, so Psi_T|_x = (v^{l(.)})^{k-1} =: Psi_alg^{k-1}. Psi_alg is a character of
    Gamma_{p_frak^infty} (unramified outside p_frak) with Psi_alg^{p^b} = eps_cyc on inertia at p_frak, i.e. the p-adic avatar of an algebraic
    Hecke character Psi_0 of K of infinity type (1,0) and conductor a power of p_frak, up to a character of order dividing p^b; that
    ambiguity dies in Psi_alg^{phi(p^n)} for n > b. Hence, for n > b,
        Psi_{l_n} = Psi_0^{phi(p^n)},   a power of ONE fixed algebraic Hecke character of type (1,0), conductor p_frak^m, p not dividing D_K.
    Any two type-(1,0) characters of K differ by a finite-order character, so Psi_0 = lambda_A . eps for the Hecke character
    lambda_A of any CM elliptic curve A/H (H the Hilbert class field; over K if h_K = 1) and a finite-order eps.
    => the approximants' CM slots are theta(chi Psi_0^{phi(p^n)}) and theta(chi^{-1} Psi_0^{phi(p^n)}); over F = K_chi both restrict to Psi_0^{phi(p^n)}.

## Check 3 (taken before 2, because it settles the MODEL) — uniform p-denominator of an explicit CM Chow motive: YES for the model
[D] Let A be a CM elliptic curve with End(A) (x) Q = K and lambda_A its Hecke character. The graph Gamma of sqrt(-D) in End(A) is an
    algebraic cycle on A x A; composition of graphs is composition of endomorphisms exactly in CH, so e_lambda := (Gamma - iota-bar)/(iota - iota-bar)
    is an idempotent in CH^1(A x A) (x) K with denominator sqrt(-D), a p-adic unit (p split, p not dividing D_K). Then
        M_CM(lambda_A^N) := e_lambda^{(x) N} h^1(A)^{(x) N} subset h(A^N),      M_CM(Psi_0^N) := M_CM(lambda_A^N) (x) M(eps^N)
    (Artin motives are Chow motives) is a Chow motive over H (over K if h_K = 1) with K-coefficients, realization Psi_0^N, and
    denominator (sqrt(-D))^{-N}: p-adic UNIT for every N. No symmetric group enters (tensor power of a rank-one piece). So
        sup_n v_p(denom(projector of M_CM(Psi_0^{phi(p^n)}))) = 0.
[V] Flapan–Lang (FL l.842-935, Lemma 3.1 and (9)): for the Weil curves C: y^e = gamma x^f + delta, (e,f) = 1, F = Q(gamma,delta) linearly
    disjoint from Q(zeta_e, zeta_f), their idempotent is eps = |G|^{-1} Sum_{g in G} Gamma_g with G = (Z/fZ x Z/eZ)^{n-1}, composed with
    E_{d,d'} = e_{d,d'}^{(x) n}: denominators divide (ef)^{n-1} times the isotypic idempotents — p-adic units if p does not divide e f
    and the bad primes S_{d,d'}. So FL's denominators are also uniform. But FL's characters are the Jacobi-sum characters lambda_{d,d'}
    of the CM fields K_{d,d'} = Q(zeta_{f_d}, zeta_{e_{d'}}) (FL l.115-135, 2.1); [K_{d,d'}:Q] = 2 forces K in {Q(sqrt(-3)), Q(i)}-type
    cyclotomic fields. For a general K (CH's examples: K = Q(sqrt(-Delta)) with q inert) FL do not apply; the elliptic-curve model
    above does. Either way the MODEL exists with unit denominators.
[V] Qiu (l.431-455): CM cycles Z(A) = c . Sum_{sigma in S_{2k-2}} sgn(sigma) sigma^* Gamma_0^{k-1} on A^{2k-2} subset Y, with c normalizing the
    self-intersection; [Z_g] is cohomologically trivial (l.455). These are Heegner-type null-homologous cycles with a symmetric-group
    average (denominator (2k-2)! c, unbounded in k): not correspondences, not uniform. Qiu's theorems (1.2.1 = 2.3.4, l.134-147) are
    modularity of the Hecke module generated by [Z] modulo the kernel of the height pairing, and a Gross–Zagier formula; nothing
    identifies a Kuga–Sato eigen-piece with a CM motive. NO for the purposes of check 2, and NO for uniform denominators.

## Check 2 — Chow-level comparison of the KUGA–SATO piece with the CM model: NO (not printed)
[V] Flapan–Lang never mention Kuga–Sato varieties (grep: zero hits). Their only statement about Scholl (FL l.1141-1150, verbatim):
    "3.4.2. Motives of CM modular forms. Suppose F = Q, K is an imaginary quadratic field, and chi is an algebraic Hecke character of K.
    Then one can form the theta series theta(chi), which is a modular eigenform. Scholl gave a construction attaching a homological (or
    Grothendieck) motive over Q to any given eigenform [Sch90]. In the very limited circumstances when F = Q and [K_{d,d'} : Q] = 2, our
    construction proves that Scholl's motives attached to theta(lambda^a lambda-bar^{n-a}) can be realized at the level of Chow motives."
    And the mechanism (FL l.1130-1143): the NUMERICAL realization of M_{d,d'} lies in M^{num,ab}_F, where Schappacher's standard motive
    M(lambda) is characterized by its L-function [Sch88, Thm I.5.1]; hence M_{d,d'} x_F F_{d,d'} = M(lambda^a lambda-bar^{n-a}) in M^{num}.
[D] Reading: "realized at the level of Chow motives" = there EXISTS a Chow motive (theirs) whose numerical motive is the numerical motive
    with Scholl's L-function. It is a lifting/existence statement in the numerical category, proved through L-functions. It is NOT an
    isomorphism of Chow motives (nor of homological motives) between Scholl's Kuga–Sato piece and M_{d,d'}; no correspondence between a
    Kuga–Sato variety and C^n is constructed or claimed. It applies only to F = Q, K in the [K_{d,d'}:Q] = 2 cyclotomic case, and to the
    Jacobi-sum characters lambda_{d,d'}, not to an arbitrary Psi_0 of conductor p_frak^m.
[D] What the contraction needs is exactly the missing object: an algebraic cycle
        C_n in CH^{phi(p^n)}( W_{r_n} x A^{phi(p^n)} )_F        (W_{r_n} the Kuga–Sato variety over the level-C curve)
    inducing the isomorphism of realizations M(theta(chi Psi_0^{phi}))_F -> M_CM(Psi_0^{phi})_F (and its twin for chi^{-1}); then
    Z_n = C_n' o C_n^{-1}. Its realization class is a Hodge/Tate class of the middle degree on W_{r_n} x A^{phi(p^n)}; its existence as a
    cycle is not printed (Flapan–Lang, Qiu, Scholl [Sch90], and the local corpus give only: realization/L-function agreement, and
    numerical-motive isomorphism inside M^{num,ab}). The same gap appears already in HOMOLOGICAL motives: no printed algebraic
    correspondence identifies Scholl's Kuga–Sato CM piece with a CM abelian motive; the known identification is in the category of
    motives for absolute Hodge cycles.
    Note on what a homological-level correspondence would buy: a correspondence acting on cohomology contracts the GALOIS-cohomology
    class (we already have that abstractly, 13 sec 2(b)); the point construction needs the Chow-level action on divisors. So the gap is
    genuinely Chow-level, and even the weaker homological statement is not printed.

## Correction (operator, 2026-09-16): C_n is necessary but NOT sufficient — three amendments
1. "It is the only missing piece" and "checks 1 and 3 remove every other objection" are WITHDRAWN. Grant C_n algebraic with bounded
   denominators for every n. The contraction the CM model supplies is its polarization (self-duality of h^1(A)^{(x)phi}, pairing the
   lambda-line with the lambda^c-line), transported through C_n. In the constituent slots of 13 sec 5 this is b_CM = (0, 1, +-1, 0): it
   contracts the FIXED constituent Ind chi^2 (trivial over F), not the moving constituent Ind Psi^{1-c} on which the CH class lives and
   whose weight-one limit is the live evaluation c_0 = (1, 0, 0, 1) (CH (1.8)). The class has zero Ind chi^2-component (CH Lemma 4.5(1),
   l.1146-1168), so the C_n route, fully completed, contracts kappa(f, g_{l_n}, h_{l_n}) to 0 for every n. The equivariance defect of c_0
   on the model is (a/b - 1, 0, 0, b/a - 1), vanishing only at weight one (13 sec 5(iii)). Checks 1 and 3 remove the character-shape and
   denominator objections FOR THE MODEL; the contraction objection is untouched and is prior to the comparison.
2. "The gap is genuinely Chow-level" (check 2, note) is WITHDRAWN as overstated. A morphism of homological motives is represented by an
   algebraic cycle Gamma modulo homological equivalence; any algebraic representative Gamma acts on Chow groups, D := Gamma_* Delta is a
   Chow class (of degree zero when Delta is null-homologous), and AJ(D) = Gamma_{et,*} AJ(Delta) by functoriality of the etale
   Abel–Jacobi map under correspondences (packet G9 (AJ-corr); Pro 6's (E2)). A homological-level comparison with an ALGEBRAIC
   representative therefore suffices for the class-level operation. What check 2 records is unchanged in substance — no algebraic
   representative of the Kuga–Sato-to-CM comparison is printed; the identification is known in absolute-Hodge motives — but the demand
   is algebraicity of a representative, not a Chow-level isomorphism.
3. Finite-coefficient obstruction, recorded. rho_n = Psi_{l_n}^{1-c} = 1 mod p^n (check 1), so the live functional exists mod p^n but lifts
   to no integral G_F-equivariant functional; the obstruction is the nonzero class beta_n(sigma) = (rho_n(sigma)^{-1} - 1)/p^n in
   H^1(G_F, O(rho_n^{-1}))[p^n] (13 sec 5(iv)). It is not the Sha obstruction and produces no point.
Operative correction (operator, verbatim): "The CM model exists; its modular-source comparison is unconstructed. But that comparison alone
is not sufficient: polarization on the model contracts the complementary finite-character constituent. The live contraction must be
matched explicitly after reduction, rather than inferred from the model's existence."
