## T4. Fixed-base dagger objects over `K_0` (MRS)

**Mandatory convention (S3 Lemma 1.3, Theorem 2.2, Example 1.5(ii)).** In an MRS twist, `σ` acts on
`I ⊗ V(L)` as `σ^{-1} ⊗ σ`; a rank-one lattice with character `χ` twists `V` by `χ^{-1}`.
BCPV's `M^† := M ⊗_{Z_p[G_r]} Z_p[G_r]^†` has `σ` acting by `<χ>_r^{-1/2}` with **no** inverse.
Therefore the lattice realizing BCPV's `†` is the one with character `c_r^{+1/2}`.

| Object | Definition | Tate module | Rational points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type B (recommended)** `A_r^† := I_r ⊗_{O_r} V_r` | `I_r := O_r`, `σ ∈ G_{K_0}` acts by multiplication by `c_r(σ)^{+1/2}` (and, on factor 3, by the tame twist of §03.2) | `T_p(A_r^†) ≅ Hom_Z(M_r, S_r) ⊗_{Z_p[G_r]} Z_p[G_r]^†` — exactly BCPV's `†` | `{x ∈ V_r(L_r^{(N)}) ⊗ Z_p : σx = c_r(σ)^{1/2}·x (twisted)}` (S3 Thm 1.4) |
| Type A (H9R8 receptacle) `A_r^{†,A} := Z[G_r](c_r^{+1/2}) ⊗_Z V_r` | permutation lattice over Z | `Z_p[G_r]^† ⊗_{Z_p} T_pV_r` — rank `\|G_r\|` times larger; NOT BCPV's module | power of `Res_{L_r/K_0} V_r` after choosing coset representatives (S3 Prop. 4.1) |
| `μ_r` | `A_r^{†,A} → A_r^†`, `g ⊗ v ↦ g·v` | K_0-rational surjection | — |

    P_r^{tr,†}   := { x ∈ A_r^†(K_0) ⊗ Z_p : x factors through B_r and lands in e'Ĵ_r }
