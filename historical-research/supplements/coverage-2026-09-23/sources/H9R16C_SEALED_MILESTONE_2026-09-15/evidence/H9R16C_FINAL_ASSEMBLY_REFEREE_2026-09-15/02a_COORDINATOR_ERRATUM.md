# 02a — Coordinator erratum to 02 (issued after the referee launched; 02 itself is unchanged)

1. **02 §D P4 and §E overstate one fact.** They say BCPV's tame push-downs "are the forgetful ϖ_{1,*}" and that
   "pr_h = forgetful ϖ_{1,*}". What BCPV actually print:
   - Their only printed definition of ϖ_1 is the p-direction map X_1(Np^r) → X_1(Np^{r−1}), (A,P) ↦ (A,pP)
     (BCPV.tex l.1198–1199).
   - For the tame push-downs X_1(Np^r) → X_1(N_h p^r) they reuse the symbol ϖ_{1,*} without a separate definition:
     `definition_big_diagonal_Hida_families` l.1457, and `pr_h` in (4.16) l.1486–1487.
   - The forgetful reading (A,P) ↦ (A,(N/N_h)P) is natural, but it is an interpretation, not a printed definition.
   - Item 3 must say which it is and what justifies it: e.g. DR22's conventions, the Hecke/q-expansion
     normalizations BCPV use, or independence of the final statement from the choice.
2. **02 §D P1 overstates one clause.** It says "a multiplication-type identification produces p^3 per level". The
   recorded p^3 (Lane 4 L4-T2) is specifically the type-A norm-image invariants with the transition s_r ⊗ ϖ_*. Whether
   any identification in the Lane 5 packaging produces a |G_r| or p^3 factor is item 4's question, not a recorded fact.
