## 9. Type findings recorded by lane 3 (details in 03)

- **T1 — model mismatch.** BCPV and DR22 use (A,P) moduli with ϖ_1(A,P) = (A,pP). HidaC and HidaL use (E, μ_{Np^r} ↪ E) with ∞ Q-rational, as does Ohta (i∞ Q-rational). Degeneracy maps and Atkin–Lehner operators must be transported through the model isomorphism before any formula from one family of sources is used in the other. NOT_PRINTED in any consulted source.
- **T2 — G_r is not a Galois group.** The projection s_r : Z[G_{r+1}] → Z[G_r] is a corestriction/norm only in the diagonal Galois direction (MRS Rem 5.11 covers this part). Along the complementary diamond directions it is a Galois-equivariant summation of lattice coordinates (MRS Cor 1.7(ii)). It is not a single Shapiro corestriction.
- **T3 — finite level only.** MRS twists exist only for finite Galois image. The Λ-adic Z_p[[G_∞]]^† of BCPV Def 4.12 is not an MRS twist, so the point side must be a projective system of finite-level twists.
- **T4 — coefficients.** MRS works over O = Z. The operators e'_ord and U'_p^{-1} exist only on p-completions (HidaC §2 (S), §5), not on A_r^† as an abelian variety.
- **T5 — base field.** HidaC and HidaL fix k and vary the level. The Weil-restricted tower {Res_{Q(μ_{p^r})/K_0} J_r} is not a fixed-k standard tower, so their control theorems do not apply to it verbatim.
- **T6 — subvariety towers vanish.** For old-form subvariety towers the integral ordinary pro-limit under π^{sr} is 0 (HidaC Prop 6.4); quotient towers are levelwise isomorphic (HidaC Lemma 6.2).
- **T7 — two tame levels.** BCPV (4.4) uses W_{Np^r} at the common tame level N, while (1.6) and A_r use W_{N_h p^r} at the native level. Their relation through the tame pushforward of Def 4.13 is NOT_PRINTED.
- **T8 — three W conventions.** HidaC/HidaL, Ohta and Cais print Galois-twist and degeneracy formulas for their own w_r that differ by diamond operators (e.g. Cais w_r^2 = ⟨−p^r⟩_N⟨−N⟩ vs HidaC's w_r^2 = 1; Cais σw_{r+1} = ⟨p⟩_N w_r ρ). BCPV's W is none of these by definition, because BCPV never defines it.
