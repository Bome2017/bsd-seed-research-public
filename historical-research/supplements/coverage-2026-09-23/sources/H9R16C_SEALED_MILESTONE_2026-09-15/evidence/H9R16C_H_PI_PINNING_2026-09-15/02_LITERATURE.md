## §1. DR22 = arXiv 2207.01314, §1.3

Local copy `~/H9R16C_LANE5_MPC_PROVER_2026-09-15/sources/tex/DR22.tex` (and `.../DR22_2207.01314.txt`).
Setting (l.1110–1112): `X_r := X_1(Mp^r)`, `M` the tame level — identical to our `X^g_s` with `M = N_g`.

* l.1146–1147 `\label{eqn:def-varpi1}`:
  `ϖ_1 : X_{r+1} → X_r` "corresponds to the map `(A,P) ↦ (A,pP)`, and to the map `τ ↦ τ` on upper
  half planes."  **This is exactly the `ϖ_1` of H_pi.**
* l.1152–1155: `ϖ_2 : X_{r+1} → X_r`, `(A,P) ↦ (A/⟨p^rP⟩, P + ⟨p^rP⟩)`, `τ ↦ pτ`.
* l.1197–1201: the tower `H^1_ét(X̄^*_∞,Z_p) := invlim_{ϖ_{1*}} H^1_ét(X̄_r,Z_p)`, "where the transition
  maps arise from the pushforward induced by the morphism `ϖ_1`".
* **l.1210–1211 (verbatim):** "The transition maps in \eqref{eqn:proj-lim-H1} are compatible with the
  action of the Hecke operators `T_n` for all `n` that are not divisible by `p`."
  → (G1) and, since `ℓ | M` gives `T_ℓ = U_ℓ` at level `Mp^r`, also (G3) — but written for the
  **unprimed** `T_n`.  DR22 do not repeat it for `T'_n`; in their tower every operator is starred
  from l.1216 onwards, so the intended reading is the starred one, and `04` verifies **both**.
* l.1216: `U_p^* := π_{1*}π_2^*` (their `U_p^*` = our `U'_p`).
* **l.1220–1222:** "The operator `U_p^*` is compatible with the transition maps defining
  `H^1_ét(X̄^*_∞,Z_p)`."  (The commutative square is present but commented out in the source,
  l.1230–1240.)
* **l.1569 (verbatim):** "Since `U_p^*` commutes with the push-forward maps `ϖ_{1*}`, this idempotent
  operates on `H^1_ét(X̄^*_∞,Z_p)`."  → (G2), and also the observation used in `01` §3.1 that (G2)
  alone makes `e'_ord` compatible with the tower.

**What DR22 does NOT contain:** any finite-level Hecke *ring* `T'_{Mp^r}`, any map `π_r` between such

## §2. KLZ17 = arXiv 1503.02888

Local copy `.../sources/tex/KLZ17.tex`.

* `prop:controlthm` (`\label` at **l.2225**, statement l.2226–2246).  Part (1) defines
  `H^1_ord(Np^∞) := e'_ord · varprojlim_r H^1_ét(Y_1(Np^r)_{Q̄}, Z_p(1))` and asserts it is
  finitely generated projective over `Λ_D = Z_p[[Z_p^×]]`, "**acting via the inverse diamond
  operators at `p`, so `u ∈ Z_p^×` acts on the `r`-th layer in the inverse limit as the diamond
  operator `⟨u^{-1}⟩_{p^r}`**" (l.2234–2236).  That parenthesis is the printed form of **(G4)**:
  a single `Λ_D`-action on the limit whose `r`-th-layer incarnation is `⟨u^{-1}⟩_{p^r}` forces the
  transition maps to intertwine `⟨u^{-1}⟩_{p^{r+1}}` with `⟨u^{-1}⟩_{p^r}`.
  Part (2): the limit has actions of `G_{Q,S}` and of `T'_n` for `n ≥ 1`, commuting.
  Part (4) is the "perfect control" theorem `H^1_ord(Np^∞)/I_{k,r} ≅ e'_ord H^1_ét(Y_1(Np^r),
  TSym^k(H_{Z_p})(1))`, `I_{k,r} = ([1+p^r] − (1+p^r)^k)`, "compatible with the actions of `G_{Q,S}`
  and the operators `T'_n` and `W_Q`".
  **Standing hypothesis `p ≥ 5`** (l.2222: "We assume, for the remainder of this paper, that
  `p ≥ 5`") — H_pi is asserted for all odd `p`, so KLZ17 does not cover `p = 3`.
  Attribution: "due to Ohta, [Ohta-ordinary, Ohta-ordinaryII]".
* l.2251: "`𝕋_{Np^∞}` for the Hecke algebra acting on `H^1_ord(Np^∞)` (generated by the operators
  `T'_n` for all `n ≥ 1`) … finite projective `Λ_D`-algebras."  **The finite-level rings `R_r` and
  the maps `π_r` are never introduced.**
* `thm:ordinaryfiltration` (`\label` at **l.2254**, statement l.2255–2281) is the `F^±` ordinary
  filtration + Ohta's pairing (v).  It is about the **limit** module only; it contains **nothing**
  about finite-level transition maps, and so is **not** a source for H_pi.  Its proof (l.2282–2290)
  says it "is equivalent to Corollary 1.3.8 and Corollary 2.3.6 of [Ohta-ordinaryII]".
* `prop:pridentity1` (l.594–603): for `pr_1, pr_2 : Y(M,Np) → Y(M,N)`,
  `(pr_2)_*U'_p = p^{k+1}(pr_1)_*` and `(pr_1)_*U'_p = T'_p(pr_1)_* − [[p,0],[0,p^{-1}]]^*(pr_2)_*`,
  proof "Explicit calculation."  The appearance of `T'_p` (not `U'_p`) on the target forces
  `p ∤ N` here: this is the **tame-to-first-layer** step, *not* the `r ≥ 1` step of H_pi.
  For `p | N` the identity degenerates to `(pr_1)_*U'_p = U'_p(pr_1)_*`, which KLZ17 does not print.
  (`04` verifies both regimes.)
* l.1464: the moment map "commutes with the Hecke operators `T'_ℓ` for `ℓ∤N`, `U'_ℓ` for `ℓ|N`, and
  `[[a,0],[0,1]]`" — the right list of generators, but for the **moment map**, not `(pr_1)_*`.

## §3. Ohta 1999 (Compositio Math. 115 (1999) 241–301), local copy `Ohta1999_Compositio115_user_icloud.{pdf,txt}`

Notation (p.245–246, txt l.330–345): `Γ_r = Γ_1(N_r)`, `N_r = Np^r`; `Y_r = Y_1(N_r)`,
`X_r = X_1(N_r)`; `H^1_ét(Ȳ_r, F_{S^d(Z_p)}) ≅ H^1(Γ_r; S^d(Z_p))` (1.1.4); `e^* := lim_n T^*(p)^{n!}`
(1.1.6) — **`T^*` is Ohta's star = our prime**, so `e^* = e'_ord`.  (1.2.4) (p.247, txt l.443–452)
prints the commuting square identifying the étale **trace** `Tr` on `H^1(Ȳ_s)` with the group-cohomology
**corestriction** `Cor` on `H^1(Γ_s; S^d(Z_p))`.  The covering `Y_s → Y_r` is the one attached to
`Γ_s ⊂ Γ_r`, i.e. `ϖ_1^{s−r}`; so **`Cor = ϖ_{1,*}^{s−r}`**.

* **COROLLARY (1.2.11)** (p.249, txt l.681): "The corestriction `e^*H^1(Γ^r_s; S^d(Z_p)) →
  e^*H^1(Γ_r; S^d(Z_p))` is an isomorphism."  (`Γ^r_s := Γ_r ∩ Γ_0(p^s)`, (1.2.1).)
* **LEMMA (1.2.12)** (p.249, txt l.689–695): "The corestriction induces an isomorphism
  `e^*H^1(Γ_s; S^d(Z_p))_{U_r/U_s} ⟶ e^*H^1(Γ_r; S^d(Z_p))`, where the subscript means the
  coinvariant."  `U_r = 1 + p^rZ_p`.
  → **This is the printed surjectivity of `e'ϖ_{1,*}` on ordinary parts**, with the kernel
  identified (the `U_r/U_s`-augmentation part).  Take `s = r+1`, `d = 0`: `e'ϖ_{1,*}` is onto
  `e'H^1(Ȳ_r, Z_p)`.  Used in `01` §3.3 (U2) and in `03` for uniqueness.
* **(1.5.2)** (p.252, txt l.941–950): `H_k(Γ_r;Z)`, `H^*_k(Γ_r;Z) ⊂ End_C(M_k(Γ_r))` generated by
  `T(n), T(q,q)` resp. `T^*(n), T^*(q,q)` (`T(q,q)` = diamond).
* **(1.5.4)** (p.252, txt l.958–963): `'|τ_r'∘T(n) = '|τ_r'∘T^*(n)` with `τ_r = [[0,−1],[N_r,0]]`;
  "Thus `H_k(Γ_r;R)` and `H^*_k(Γ_r;R)` are canonically isomorphic via `T(n) ↔ T^*(n)`."
  → the level-by-level Atkin–Lehner transport between the primed and unprimed algebras.
* **(1.5.5)** (p.252, txt l.969–978, verbatim): `H_k(N;R) := lim_{←,r≥1} H_k(Γ_r;R)`,
  `H^*_k(N;R) := lim_{←,r≥1} H^*_k(Γ_r;R)`; "**relative to the natural homomorphisms
  (`T(n) ↦ T(n)` and `T(q,q) ↦ T(q,q)` etc.)**".
  → **The closest printed statement to H_pi(ring):** the *existence* of homomorphisms
  `H^*_k(Γ_{r+1};R) → H^*_k(Γ_r;R)` sending generators to generators, for the **starred** algebras.
  Three gaps against H_pi: (a) Ohta's algebras are subalgebras of `End_C(M_k(Γ_r))` (modular forms);
  the transfer to `End(H^1)` is stated only over `Q` ("via the Eichler–Shimura isomorphism, we may
  identify … with the subalgebras of `End_Q(H^1(Γ_r;S^d(Q)))`", p.253, txt l.995–998) — a
  **rational** identification, whereas H_pi is an integral statement in `End_{Z_p}(H^1(·,Z_p))`;
  (b) no `ϖ_{1,*}`-intertwining is asserted — "natural homomorphisms" is not unpacked, and no proof
  is given; (c) no surjectivity is claimed at (1.5.5).
* **THEOREM-DEFINITION (1.5.7)(iii)** (p.253, txt l.1010–1030): `e^*H^*(N;o)/ω_{r,d} ⟶
  e^*H^*_{d+2}(Γ_r;o)` is an isomorphism, `ω_{r,d} = (1+T)^{p^{r−1}} − u^{dp^{r-1}}`; the proof
  begins "we have a natural **surjective** homomorphism `e^*H^*(N;o)/ω_{r,d} ↠ e^*H^*_k(Γ_r;o)`".
  Since `((1+T)^{p^{r}}−1) ⊆ ((1+T)^{p^{r−1}}−1)` in `Λ` (`d = 0`), the big algebra factors
  `e^*H^*(N;o) ↠ e^*H^*(N;o)/ω_{r+1,0} ↠ e^*H^*(N;o)/ω_{r,0}` and (iii) turns this into a
  **surjection `R^{Ohta}_{r+1} ↠ R^{Ohta}_r` matching `T^*(n) ↦ T^*(n)`** — H_pi(surj), for Ohta's
  carrier.
* **Ohta 2000** (Math. Ann. 318, `[Ohta-ordinaryII]`) is the source KLZ17 cites for the pairing and
  filtration.  It is **NOT_OBTAINED** in this program.  *Nothing in the verdict of this lane depends
  on it*: `03` proves H_pi outright from (1.5.5)-free ingredients, and the only Ohta item used as a
  citation is Lemma (1.2.12) of the 1999 paper, which is in hand.

## §4. Hida

`~/H9R16C_LANE4_HOSTILE_OBSTRUCTION_2026-09-15/sources/Hida_ControlLambdaAdicMW_jhc70.{pdf,txt}`
= "Control of Λ-adic Mordell–Weil groups", §1 (Introduction), **p.3**, txt l.158–166, verbatim:

> `h_r(Z) = Z[T(n), U(l) : l|Np, (n,Np)=1] ⊂ End(J_r)`, and put `h_r(R) = h_r(Z) ⊗_Z R` … Then we
> define `h_r = e(h_r(Z_p))`.  **The restriction morphism `h_s(Z) ∋ h ↦ h|_{J_r} ∈ h_r(Z)` for
> `s > r` induces a projective system `{h_r}_r` whose limit gives rise to a big ordinary Hecke
> algebra `h = h(N) := lim_r h_r`.**  … we have an identity `l⟨l⟩ = T(l)^2 − T(l^2) ∈ h_r(Z_p)` for
> all primes `l ∤ Np`. … It is now well known that `h` is free of finite rank over `Λ` and
> `h_r = h ⊗_Λ Λ/(γ^{p^{r−ε}} − 1)` (cf. [H86a]).

This is the **only** printed construction of the finite-level transition map found anywhere in the
consulted corpus, and it is important that its mechanism is **restriction along an inclusion of
Jacobians**, not the `ϖ_{1,*}`-intertwining:  `J_r ↪ J_s` is `π^*` (Picard functoriality), spelled
out in the companion paper *Limit Mordell–Weil groups* §5, p.23, txt l.1327–1336: "Write `A_s`
(`s > r`) for the image of `A_r` in `J_s` under the morphism `π^* : J_r → J_s` given by Picard
functoriality from the projection `π : X_s → X_r`".  Hida's `h_r` is the **unprimed** algebra;
`h_r(Z) → h^*_r(Z)` is via the Rosati involution (LMWM §5, txt l.1352–1354).
`h_r = h ⊗_Λ Λ/(γ^{p^{r−ε}}−1)` gives H_pi(surj) for the unprimed algebras on Jacobians.

**Gaps against H_pi:** unprimed operators; carrier `End(J_r)`, not `End(H^1_ét(X̄_r,Z_p(1)))`; no
`ϖ_{1,*}`-intertwining; well-definedness of `h ↦ h|_{J_r}` is asserted, not proved (it is the same
(WD) of `01` §3.2, in the Picard direction).

## §6. Conclusion of the literature pass

H_pi as stated — *a ring homomorphism `π_r : R_{r+1} → R_r` of the finite-level **ordinary primed**
Hecke rings **acting on `H^1_ét`**, characterised by `ϖ_{1,*}t = π_r(t)ϖ_{1,*}`, integrally, and
surjective* — is **NOT_PRINTED**.  What is printed is:

1. all four generator intertwinings (G1)–(G4), across DR22 §1.3 and KLZ17 `prop:controlthm`(1),
   with (G3) only inside a sentence about unprimed `T_n`;
2. the *existence* of generator-to-generator transition homomorphisms for the starred finite-level
   Hecke algebras **acting on modular forms** — Ohta99 (1.5.5), asserted without proof;
3. surjectivity, in Ohta99 (1.5.7)(iii) and Hida *Control* p.3, in each case for the printed
   carrier (forms; Jacobians), not for `End(H^1_ét)`, and not linked to `ϖ_{1,*}`;
4. **surjectivity of `e'ϖ_{1,*}` itself** — Ohta99 **Lemma (1.2.12)**, p.249, which is the one
   printed statement that this lane can use verbatim.

So the remaining work is exactly `01` §3.2 (WD) plus the bookkeeping that makes (1)–(4) land on the
right carrier with the right (primed) operators, integrally.  That is `03`.
