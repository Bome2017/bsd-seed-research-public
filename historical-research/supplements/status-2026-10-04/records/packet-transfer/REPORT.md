# Result of the authorized construction attempt

**NO_NEW_UNIFORM_INPUT_FOR_THE_THREE_TESTED_CONSTRUCTIONS**

The requested uniform generation formula was not constructed. No new seed primary vanishing, FIN, ORDER9, or Q36 conclusion was obtained. This is a completed bounded attempt, not a claim of an impossible route or an instruction to launch a larger campaign.

Two exact auxiliary formulas were obtained in the course of the attempt:

1. **Six-term local transfer.** On the formal marked projective line, `(1-w)(U-1)^2 W_2 = 2(e_0-e_infinity) mod p`. Its translates generate all degree-zero marked-point differences. The integer correction and a two-prime tensor variant are proved in CONSTRUCTION.md Section 2. The non-affine transformations have **not** been realized as admissible Gross/Kurihara packet changes.
2. **Crossed cyclic-p-squared transfer.** On integral modular-curve homology, `beta_* alpha^* = T_p^2-(p+1)`. Its elliptic period multiplier is `a_p^2-p-1`, a p-unit at every good seed prime p>=5, including supersingular primes. This repairs the p-divisible same-map trace by changing the correspondence. It transports an arbitrary supplied seed period; it does **not** generate the required selected witness periods. Its level-p predecessor was already in the record; novelty is not asserted.

The actual construction tests were:

| Operation | Attempted role | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Change compatible CM system; then retain auxiliary-conductor branches | Obtain independently controlled moments | Same-system changes are affine; the trivial-character projection of branches remains uncontrolled |
| Apply projective transformations to quadratic weights | Express a fixed nonzero period as a bounded combination of moments | Explicit local coefficients obtained; admissible arithmetic realization not obtained |
| Lift to cyclic-p-squared level and use crossed descent | Repair the loss from dividing a trace by p, then realize the previous operation | Crossed descent is integral on the seed period; quadratic weighting annihilates the tested full-pullback splice |

The last failure is specific: on a regular marking cover, a full pulled-back cycle is deck-invariant, and the quadratic weight has augmentation zero modulo p. It does not exclude another operation inserted before weighting. No such arithmetic operation preserving the necessary descent implication was constructed in this attempt.

The files contain a written author-side derivation, not an independent mathematical review. Exact controls cover 24 prime models for the marked identities, five tensor models, 24 small seed point counts by two methods, four cyclic-subgroup models, and scalar inverses modulo p, p^2 and p^3. These are normalization controls, not computations of seed Sha.

The proposed continuation has therefore not earned a claim of main-line BSD progress. The new formulas are retained so that a later candidate can use them without redoing them. No automatic successor task is proposed or launched.
