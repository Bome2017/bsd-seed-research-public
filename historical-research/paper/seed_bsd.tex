\title[A rank-two elliptic curve: certified arithmetic and remaining BSD obligations]{A rank-two elliptic curve of conductor $6503188012738584$:\\ certified arithmetic, conditional criteria,\\ and the remaining Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer obligations}
\author{Sanjit Singh Mehat}
\date{Private research-review draft 0.2.2, 23 September 2026}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We study the elliptic curve $E\colon y^2=x^3-22735x^2+106272484x$ over $\Q$, of conductor $N=2^3\cdot3\cdot2243\cdot4547^2\cdot5843$. We record what is proved about the Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture for $E$, what is proved only conditionally, and exactly what remains.

\emph{Proved.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item $E(\Q)\simeq\Z/2\oplus\Z^2$ with an explicit basis, and the analytic rank is exactly $2$; the analytic-rank statement is a computer-assisted certificate for $L''(E,1)$.
\item The BSD quotient $q=L''(E,1)/(2\Omega_E\Reg)$ lies in $(35.99427,36.00573)$.
\item The $p$-primary parts of $\Sha(E/\Q)$ for $p\le47$ are $0$ except at $p=3$, where $\Sha(E/\Q)[3^\infty]\simeq(\Z/3)^2$; at $17\le p\le47$ the certificates apply a theorem of Ota as stated in its arXiv version.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Conditional.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item For every good prime $p\ge5$, $p\nmid7N$, the nonvanishing modulo $p$ of one anticyclotomic Gross-point coefficient $M_2(p)$ implies $\Sha(E/\Q)[p^\infty]=0$. The algebraic core of the supersingular case is checked in Lean.
\item At the split multiplicative prime $2243$, a single coefficient, not yet computed, would prove $\Sha(E/\Q)[2243^\infty]=0$ if it is nonzero modulo $2243$; a zero value would prove nothing. Every stated theoretical input is supplied at its recorded import or certificate grade, including an independent certificate that the split $2243$-adic height is nondegenerate.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Open.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Full BSD for $E$ remains open: it requires $\Sha$ finite of order $9$ and the exact identity $q=36$. Finiteness is also tracked as a separate intermediate milestone.
\end{itemize}

We also record a family of scoped obstructions, and separate new results from applications of published theorems. Each principal claim carries an identifier into the accompanying claim-to-evidence index.
\end{abstract}
