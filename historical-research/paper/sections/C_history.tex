\section{Research history}\label{app:history}

The chronology below is taken from the record dates.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{August--mid-September 2026.} Work on other curves: qualified BSD results for twist families of $62a1$ and $66b1$, the H41 mod-4 successor, and Lean point-group bridges (Appendix~\ref{app:other}). Then the H9 program (generalized Kato classes), which stopped at a missing comparison.
\item \emph{17--18 September.} The curve $E$ is selected from Zywina's family. The residual images and the Manin constant are settled. A first $5$-primary certificate is built in floating-point arithmetic, and the first architecture audits are run.
\item \emph{19 September.} The rigorous analytic-rank certificate, the $3$-primary theorem (depth four), the rigorous $5$-primary upgrade and the regulator certificate. Audits of Criterion SS, LINE, witness content and the division fields, plus the source audits of Destrempes--Malinin and of the Eisenstein routes. The canonical master record is created.
\item \emph{20--21 September.} The R1/R2 repair and its review, the visibility deduction, and the Eisenstein second-jet analyses with their certified residuals. The $7$-, $11$- and $13$-primary theorems and the $D=-23$ twist certificate. A reconciliation of historical coverage.
\item \emph{22 September.} The frontier freeze. The $D=-7$ certificate and the frozen ordinary anticyclotomic bridge. The supersingular bridge, its review, its Lean core and its promotion. The bounded feasibility check at the bad primes.
\item \emph{22--23 September.} Preparation of the $2243$ evaluator and its two reviews (the restart defect). The D2/D5 reconciliation. The compression attempt, stopped at a precise obstruction. The independent $2243$-adic height certificate.
\end{itemize}

The repository contains the indexed retained stopped-route records; it does not claim to recover every older task or artifact. The canonical master record (read-only here) holds the claim-specific supersession history up to 21 September.
