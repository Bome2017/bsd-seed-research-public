\section{Contradictions and withdrawn claims}\label{app:register}

\path{claims/CONTRADICTIONS.md} lists every discrepancy found while this package was assembled. Resolved items are kept visible. Six are unresolved:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Stein--Wuthrich's multiplicative ``if and only if'' and its attribution to Jones. This is a literature question, and it is not used here.
\item A disputed exceptional zero at $5843$ for the twist $E^{-7}$. It is off the critical path.
\item Three changed metadata files in the external $C_{13}$ record. Appendix~\ref{subsec:c13custody} records their mismatches and the intact calculation-input chain; the historical status remains unrecovered.
\item The canonical master is intentionally frozen. Later results supersede it additively; this release does not edit that master.
\item Two reviewers grade the $2243$ restart defect differently.
\item The canonical proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:ordbridge} has not been chosen between the two routes.
\end{enumerate}

The withdrawn register (\texttt{CLAIM\_INDEX.md}, ids beginning with \texttt{W}) includes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item a claimed unit specialization at $13$;
\item an ``arrow direction'' admission rule;
\item superseded values of $C_{13}$;
\item overstatements of the order of the theta element and of the regulator input;
\item corrected coefficient relations;
\item superseded cost figures;
\item the evaluator's isolation claim.
\end{itemize}
Older withdrawals are in the master record's frozen register.
