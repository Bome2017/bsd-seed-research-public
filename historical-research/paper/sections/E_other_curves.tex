\section{Results for other curves}\label{app:other}

The same programme produced several results about other elliptic curves. They are recorded for context only; none transfers to $E$, because each rank-zero argument consumes $L(\cdot,1)\ne0$.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \cid{H.1}: explicit $2$-isogeny descent for twists of $62a1$ by primes $q\equiv5\pmod8$ with $(q/31)=-1$ (relative natural density $1/8$). It gives rank zero and trivial $2$-primary $\Sha$, with a qualified full-BSD assembly through published and preprint results.
\item \cid{H.2}: the parallel family for $66b1$.
\item \cid{H.3}: an H41 mod-4 successor, which closes the retained twist-family arguments at their stated tier.
\item \cid{H.4}: Lean proofs, for the family $y^2=x^3+(1-2r)qx^2+r^2q^2x$ with $r=\pm2^m$ and $m\ge3$, under the frozen Params/H41 hypotheses, that the rational-point quotient $E(\Q)/2E(\Q)$ is $\Z/2$, that the rank is zero, and that the rational-point group is finite. This is finiteness of $E(\Q)$, not of $\Sha$.
\item \cid{H.5}: a geometric compatibility theorem for a Jacobian point tower, with its explicit non-claims.
\end{itemize}
